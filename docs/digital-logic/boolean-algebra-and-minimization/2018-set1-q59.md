# 2018 CS Q59: Essential prime implicants from a minterm list with don't-cares

## Question

Consider the minterm list form of a Boolean function $F$ given below.

$$F(P,Q,R,S) = \sum m(0, 2, 5, 7, 9, 11) + d(3, 8, 10, 12, 14)$$

Here, $m$ denotes a minterm and $d$ denotes a don't care term. The number of essential prime implicants of the function $F$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**K-map** (rows $PQ$, columns $RS$; `1` = minterm, `x` = don't care, `0` = off):

| $PQ \backslash RS$ | 00 | 01 | 11 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | 1 | 0 | x | 1 |
| 01 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| 11 | x | 0 | 0 | x |
| 10 | x | 1 | 1 | x |

**Prime implicants** (largest groups that are valid): $\bar Q\bar S$ (0, 2, 8, 10), $P\bar Q$ (8, 9, 10, 11), $\bar PQS$ (5, 7), $\bar P RS$ (3, 7), $P\bar S$ (8, 10, 12, 14) and $\bar Q R$ (2, 3, 10, 11).

**Essential ones** cover a minterm that no other prime implicant covers:
- $\bar Q\bar S$ is the only cover of minterm **0**.
- $\bar PQS$ is the only cover of minterm **5**.
- $P\bar Q$ is the only cover of minterm **9**.

The others are not essential: for example minterm 7 is covered by both $\bar PQS$ and $\bar PRS$, and minterm 2 by both $\bar Q\bar S$ and $\bar QR$.

The number of essential prime implicants is **3**.

**Code check:** a Quine-McCluskey script listed the same six prime implicants and the same three essential ones.

**Key idea:** a prime implicant is essential if some required minterm (not a don't-care) is covered by it alone.

</details>
