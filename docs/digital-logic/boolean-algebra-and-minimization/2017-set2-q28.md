# 2017 CS2 Q28: Minimum product-of-sums form of a function with don't cares

## Question

Given $f(w,x,y,z) = \sum_m (0,1,2,3,7,8,10) + \sum_d (5,6,11,15)$, where $d$ represents the *don't-care* condition in Karnaugh maps. Which of the following is a minimum product-of-sums (POS) form of $f(w,x,y,z)$?

- (A) $f = (\bar{w} + \bar{z})(\bar{x} + z)$
- (B) $f = (\bar{w} + z)(x + z)$
- (C) $f = (w + z)(\bar{x} + z)$
- (D) $f = (w + \bar{z})(\bar{x} + z)$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Zeros of $f$.** All 16 minterms minus the ones and don't-cares: $f = 0$ at $4, 9, 12, 13, 14$. Don't-cares are $5, 6, 11, 15$.

**POS via the complement.** Group the cells where $f = 0$ (using don't-cares to enlarge groups) to get $\bar f$, then complement:

- $x\bar z$: cells $4, 6, 12, 14$ (6 is a don't-care) covers zeros $4, 12, 14$.
- $wz$: cells $9, 11, 13, 15$ (11, 15 are don't-cares) covers zeros $9, 13$.

$$\bar f = x\bar z + wz \;\Rightarrow\; f = (\bar x + z)(\bar w + \bar z).$$

That is **(A)**: two sum terms with two literals each.

**Check against the other options** (using $w$ as the most significant bit):

| Option | Wrongly gives | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| (B) | 0 at minterms 0, 2, 8, 10 and 1 at 4, 9, 13 | $(x + z)$ is 0 when $x=z=0$ |
| (C) | 0 at minterms 0, 2 and 1 at 9, 13 | $(w+z)=0$ for $w=z=0$ |
| (D) | 0 at minterms 1, 3, 7 and 1 at 9, 13 | $(w + \bar z)=0$ for $w=0,z=1$ |

**Code check:** for each option the program evaluated all 16 inputs and required $1$ on every minterm and $0$ on every zero ($4,9,12,13,14$), leaving don't-cares free. Only (A) passes; the failing inputs for (B), (C), (D) are those in the table.

**Key idea:** for POS, cover the zeros of $f$ (don't-cares allowed) and complement, or build sum terms from groups of zeros.

</details>
