# 2008 CS Q5: Minimal form from a Karnaugh map with don't cares

## Question

In the Karnaugh map shown below, X denotes a don't care term. What is the minimal form of the function represented by the Karnaugh map?

![A Karnaugh map with rows cd = 00, 01, 11, 10 and columns ab = 00, 01, 11, 10. Row 00 has 1 in columns 00, 01 and 10, and is empty in column 11. Row 01 has X in column 00 and is otherwise empty. Row 11 has X in column 00 and is otherwise empty. Row 10 has 1 in columns 00 and 01, is empty in column 11, and has X in column 10.](../../assets/2008/set1-q5.png)

- (A) $\overline{b}.\overline{d} + \overline{a}.\overline{d}$
- (B) $\overline{a}.\overline{b} + \overline{b}.\overline{d} + \overline{a}.b.\overline{d}$
- (C) $\overline{b}.\overline{d} + \overline{a}.b.\overline{d}$
- (D) $\overline{a}.\overline{b} + \overline{b}.\overline{d} + \overline{a}.\overline{d}$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Reading the map ($m$ = minterm number with $a$ as the most significant bit):

- 1s at $m_0, m_2, m_4, m_6, m_8$;
- don't cares at $m_1, m_3, m_{10}$.

**Groups.**

- $m_0, m_2, m_8, m_{10}$ (where $m_{10}$ is a don't care) is the group $b = 0,\ d = 0$: $\overline{b}\,\overline{d}$.
- $m_0, m_2, m_4, m_6$ is the group $a = 0,\ d = 0$: $\overline{a}\,\overline{d}$.

Together they cover all the 1s, so the minimal form is

$$f = \overline{b}\,\overline{d} + \overline{a}\,\overline{d}.$$

This is (A): two terms with two literals each.

- (B), (C) and (D) also cover the required 1s but use extra or larger terms: (B) and (D) add the redundant group $\overline{a}\,\overline{b}$, and (C) uses the three-literal term $\overline{a}\,b\,\overline{d}$ instead of the two-literal $\overline{a}\,\overline{d}$.

**Code check:** I evaluated each option against the map. All four cover every 1 and none covers a 0 (the empty cells), so they are all correct covers. They use 4, 7, 5 and 6 literals for (A), (B), (C) and (D), so (A) is the minimal form.

**Key idea:** use the don't care cells to make the largest possible groups, then keep only as many groups as needed to cover the 1s.

</details>
