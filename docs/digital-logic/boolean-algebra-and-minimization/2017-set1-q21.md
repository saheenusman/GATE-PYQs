# 2017 CS1 Q21: Minimum number of 2-input NOR gates for a K-map with don't cares

## Question

Consider the Karnaugh map given below, where X represents "*don't care*" and blank represents 0.

![Karnaugh map with rows labelled dc = 00, 01, 11, 10 and columns labelled ba = 00, 01, 11, 10. Row 00 has X at columns 01 and 11. Row 01 has 1 at column 00 and X at column 10. Row 11 has 1 at columns 00 and 10. Row 10 has X at columns 01 and 11. All other cells are 0.](../../assets/2017/set1-q21.png)

Assume for all inputs $(a, b, c, d)$, the respective complements $(\bar a, \bar b, \bar c, \bar d)$ are also available. The above logic is implemented using 2-input NOR gates only. The minimum number of gates required is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**1**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Read the map.** Rows are $dc$, columns are $ba$.

| Cell $(ba, dc)$ | Value | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $d$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (00, 01) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| (00, 11) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| (10, 11) | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| (10, 01) | X | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |

**Group.** The three 1s and the don't-care at $(10, 01)$ fill the whole block $a=0,\ c=1$ (columns $ba=00,10$ and rows $dc=01,11$). That is one quad:

$$f = \bar a\, c$$

The other don't-cares are not needed.

**NOR realization.** By De Morgan, $\overline{a + \bar c} = \bar a\, c$. Since $\bar c$ is available as an input, one 2-input NOR gate computes $f$:

$$f = \text{NOR}(a,\ \bar c)$$

The function is not a single literal, so at least one gate is needed. **Minimum = 1.**

**Code check:** $f=\bar a c$ matched all three 1-cells and all blank (0) cells of the map; the five don't-care cells were left free. $\text{NOR}(a,\bar c)$ equalled $\bar a c$ for all 16 input combinations.

**Key idea:** with complements available, a product of two literals $\bar a\,c$ is one NOR gate: $\text{NOR}(a,\bar c)$.

</details>
