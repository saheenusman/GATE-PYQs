# 2014 CS3 Q7: Minimal SOP with don't-care terms

## Question

Consider the following minterm expression for $F$:

$$F(P,Q,R,S) = \sum 0, 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13, 15$$

The minterms 2, 7, 8 and 13 are 'do not care' terms. The minimal sum-of-products form for $F$ is

- (A) $Q\bar{S} + \bar{Q}S$
- (B) $\bar{Q}\bar{S} + QS$
- (C) $\bar{Q}\bar{R}\bar{S} + \bar{Q}R\bar{S} + Q\bar{R}S + QRS$
- (D) $\bar{P}\bar{Q}\bar{S} + \bar{P}QS + PQS + P\bar{Q}\bar{S}$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The function must be 1 on the **required** minterms $\lbrace 0, 5, 10, 15 \rbrace$. The minterms $\lbrace 2, 7, 8, 13 \rbrace$ are free.

K-map groups (using the don't-cares):

- $\lbrace 0, 2, 8, 10 \rbrace$: $Q = 0$ and $S = 0$, so the term $\bar{Q}\bar{S}$. It covers the required 0 and 10.
- $\lbrace 5, 7, 13, 15 \rbrace$: $Q = 1$ and $S = 1$, so the term $QS$. It covers the required 5 and 15.

So $F = \bar{Q}\bar{S} + QS$ (this is $Q \odot S$, XNOR), with 2 terms of 2 literals each.

- (A) is the XOR, the complement, and it is 0 on minterm 0.
- (C) and (D) are also valid covers but are not minimal: 4 terms of 3 literals each.

**Code check:** all four options were tested over the 16 inputs. (B), (C) and (D) cover every required minterm and avoid every zero; (A) fails. Of these, (B) is the smallest.

**Key idea:** use don't-cares to enlarge groups: two groups of four give two 2-literal terms.

</details>
