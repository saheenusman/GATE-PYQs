# 2026 CS2 Q16: Which is not a property of Boolean algebra

## Question

Which one of the following options is **not** a property of Boolean Algebra?

*Note:* $+$ is OR, $\cdot$ is AND, and $'$ is NOT.

- (A) $a + b = b + a$
- (B) $a \cdot a' = 1$
- (C) $a + a' = 1$
- (D) $a \cdot b = b \cdot a$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- **(A)** Commutative law for OR. True.
- **(B)** $a \cdot a'$ is a variable AND its own complement. This is always **0**, not 1. **Not a property.**
- **(C)** Complement law for OR: $a + a' = 1$. True.
- **(D)** Commutative law for AND. True.

Check with $a = 1$: $1 \cdot 0 = 0$. With $a = 0$: $0 \cdot 1 = 0$. So $a \cdot a' = 0$ always.

**Key idea:** the complement laws are $a + a' = 1$ and $a \cdot a' = 0$.

</details>
