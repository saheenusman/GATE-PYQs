# 2024 CS2 Q30: False Boolean identities

## Question

For a Boolean variable $x$, which of the following statements is/are **FALSE**?

- (A) $x \cdot 1 = x$
- (B) $x + 1 = x$
- (C) $x \cdot x = 0$
- (D) $x + \bar{x} = 1$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B and C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- **(A) True.** AND with 1 is the identity: $x \cdot 1 = x$.
- **(B) False.** OR with 1 always gives 1 (the null/dominance law): $x + 1 = 1$, not $x$.
- **(C) False.** The idempotent law says $x \cdot x = x$. The product is 0 only for $x \cdot \bar{x}$.
- **(D) True.** The complement law: $x + \bar{x} = 1$.

**Key idea:** $x + 1 = 1$, $x \cdot 0 = 0$, $x \cdot x = x$, $x + x = x$, $x \cdot \bar{x} = 0$, $x + \bar{x} = 1$.

</details>
