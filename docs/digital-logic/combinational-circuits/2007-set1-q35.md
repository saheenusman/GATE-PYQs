# 2007 CS Q35: AND and OR gates in a 4-bit look-ahead carry generator

## Question

In a look-ahead carry generator, the carry generate function $G_i$ and the carry propagate function $P_i$ for inputs $A_i$ and $B_i$ are given by:

$$P_i = A_i \oplus B_i \quad\text{and}\quad G_i = A_iB_i$$

The expressions for the sum bit $S_i$ and the carry bit $C_{i+1}$ of the look-ahead carry adder are given by:

$$S_i = P_i \oplus C_i, \qquad C_{i+1} = G_i + P_iC_i,$$

where $C_0$ is the input carry.

Consider a two-level logic implementation of the look-ahead carry generator. Assume that all $P_i$ and $G_i$ are available for the carry generator circuit and that the AND and OR gates can have any number of inputs. The number of AND gates and OR gates needed to implement the look-ahead carry generator for a 4-bit adder with $S_3, S_2, S_1, S_0$ and $C_4$ as outputs are respectively:

- (A) 6, 3
- (B) 10, 4
- (C) 6, 4
- (D) 10, 5

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Expanding the carries in two levels (AND of terms, then one OR per carry):

| Carry | Expression | ANDs | ORs |
|---|---|---|---|
| $C_1$ | $G_0 + P_0C_0$ | 1 | 1 |
| $C_2$ | $G_1 + P_1G_0 + P_1P_0C_0$ | 2 | 1 |
| $C_3$ | $G_2 + P_2G_1 + P_2P_1G_0 + P_2P_1P_0C_0$ | 3 | 1 |
| $C_4$ | $G_3 + P_3G_2 + P_3P_2G_1 + P_3P_2P_1G_0 + P_3P_2P_1P_0C_0$ | 4 | 1 |

The lone $G_i$ term needs no AND gate. Total: $1+2+3+4 = 10$ ANDs and $4$ ORs: (B).

The sum bits $S_i = P_i \oplus C_i$ use XOR gates, which are not counted, and $P_i$, $G_i$ are given.

- (A) and (C) count only 6 ANDs, as if the products were shared.
- (D) adds a fifth OR; there are only four carries to generate ($C_1$ to $C_4$).

**Code check:** Python: counted terms in the expanded expressions for $C_1..C_4$: 10 AND gates, 4 OR gates.

**Key idea:** in two-level look-ahead, $C_{i+1}$ needs $i+1$ AND gates and one OR gate.
