# 2010 CS Q31: Output of a seven-gate NOR network

## Question

What is the Boolean expression for the output $f$ of the combinational logic circuit of NOR gates given below?

![A circuit of seven 2-input NOR gates. In the top half, one NOR takes P and Q and another takes Q and R; their outputs feed a third NOR. In the bottom half, one NOR takes P and R and another takes Q and R; their outputs feed a fourth NOR. The outputs of the two second-level NOR gates feed the final NOR gate, whose output is f.](../../assets/2010/set1-q31.png)

- (A) $\overline{Q + R}$
- (B) $\overline{P + Q}$
- (C) $\overline{P + R}$
- (D) $\overline{P + Q + R}$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Work from the inputs. Top half:

- $g_1 = \overline{P + Q}$ and $g_2 = \overline{Q + R}$.
- $h_1 = \overline{g_1 + g_2} = (P + Q)(Q + R) = Q + PR$.

Bottom half:

- $g_3 = \overline{P + R}$ and $g_4 = \overline{Q + R}$.
- $h_2 = \overline{g_3 + g_4} = (P + R)(Q + R) = R + PQ$.

Output:

$$f = \overline{h_1 + h_2} = \overline{Q + PR + R + PQ} = \overline{Q + R}$$

since $PR$ is absorbed by $R$ and $PQ$ by $Q$. So (A).

- At $PQR = 100$ the circuit gives $\overline{0 + 0} = 1$, while (B), (C) and (D) all give $0$. Only (A) fits.

**Code check:** simulating the seven gates on all 8 inputs matched only option (A).

**Read by eye:** the gate wiring was traced from the page image; each NOR's inputs are the labelled pair on its left.

**Key idea:** NOR of two NORs is an AND of ORs; absorption collapses the expression to $\overline{Q+R}$.

</details>
