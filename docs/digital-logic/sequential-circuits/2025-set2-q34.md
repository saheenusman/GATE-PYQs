# 2025 CS2 Q34: Frequency of a 4-bit ripple counter

## Question

In a 4-bit ripple counter, if the period of the waveform at the last flip-flop is 64 microseconds, then the frequency of the ripple counter in kHz is ________. (Answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**250**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

In a ripple counter each flip-flop **divides the frequency by 2**. After 4 flip-flops the output has period $2^4 = 16$ times the clock period $T$.

$$16\,T = 64\ \mu\text{s} \Rightarrow T = 4\ \mu\text{s}.$$

The clock frequency is

$$f = \frac{1}{4\ \mu\text{s}} = 250{,}000 \text{ Hz} = 250 \text{ kHz}.$$

**Key idea:** the last stage of an $n$-bit ripple counter has period $2^n$ times the input clock period.

</details>
