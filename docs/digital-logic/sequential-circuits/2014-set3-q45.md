# 2014 CS3 Q45: State sequence of a JK flip-flop circuit

## Question

![Synchronous circuit of three JK flip-flops sharing one clock C. The left flip-flop has output Q2, the middle one Q1 and the right one Q0. Q2 drives J of the middle flip-flop and its complement drives K of the middle flip-flop. Q1 drives J of the right flip-flop. The complement of Q0 is fed back to K of the right flip-flop. The complement of Q1 goes around the bottom to J of the left flip-flop, and Q0 goes around the top to K of the left flip-flop.](../../assets/2014/set3-q45.png)

The above synchronous sequential circuit built using JK flip-flops is initialized with $Q_2Q_1Q_0 = 000$. The state sequence for this circuit for the next 3 clock cycles is

- (A) 001, 010, 011
- (B) 111, 110, 101
- (C) 100, 110, 111
- (D) 100, 011, 001

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Reading the wiring from the figure:

| Flip-flop | J | K |
|---|---|---|
| FF2 (left) | $\bar{Q_1}$ | $Q_0$ |
| FF1 (middle) | $Q_2$ | $\bar{Q_2}$ |
| FF0 (right) | $Q_1$ | $\bar{Q_0}$ |

A JK flip-flop gives: $J K = 00$ hold, $01$ reset (0), $10$ set (1), $11$ toggle.

| Clock | State $Q_2Q_1Q_0$ | FF2 ($J,K$) | FF1 ($J,K$) | FF0 ($J,K$) | Next state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 000 | $(1,0)$ set | $(0,1)$ reset | $(0,1)$ reset | **100** |
| 2 | 100 | $(1,0)$ set | $(1,0)$ set | $(0,1)$ reset | **110** |
| 3 | 110 | $(0,0)$ hold | $(1,0)$ set | $(1,1)$ toggle | **111** |

So the sequence is 100, 110, 111: **C**.

- (A) is a binary up-count and (B) and (D) do not follow these J/K inputs.

**Code check:** a simulation of the three flip-flops from $000$ gives `100, 110, 111`.

**Key idea:** apply the JK characteristic equation $Q^+ = J\bar{Q} + \bar{K}Q$ to every flip-flop using the current state.

</details>
