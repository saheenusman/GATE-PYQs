# 2011 CS Q51: Number of distinct states of the counter after reset

## Question

**Common data for Questions 50 and 51.** Consider the following circuit involving three D-type flip-flops used in a certain type of counter configuration.

![A circuit with three D flip-flops P, Q and R sharing one clock. P's D input is wired to R's Q output. Q's D input comes from a NOR gate whose two inputs are the Q outputs of P and R. R's D input comes from an AND gate whose two inputs are the Q output of Q and the complemented output of R (Q-bar of R).](../../assets/2011/set1-q51.png)

If all the flip-flops were reset to $0$ at power on, what is the total number of distinct outputs (states) represented by $PQR$ generated by the counter?

- (A) 3
- (B) 4
- (C) 5
- (D) 6

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The equations (see Q50) are $P^+ = R$, $Q^+ = \overline{P + R}$, $R^+ = Q \cdot \bar R$. The wiring was traced by hand from the figure.

Start from $PQR = 000$:

| Present $PQR$ | $P^+ = R$ | $Q^+ = \overline{P+R}$ | $R^+ = Q \bar R$ | Next |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $000$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ | $010$ |
| $010$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ | $011$ |
| $011$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ | $100$ |
| $100$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $000$ |

The sequence is $000 \to 010 \to 011 \to 100 \to 000$: a cycle of **$4$ distinct states**: (B).

The other four states ($001$, $101$, $110$, $111$) are never reached from reset (they lead into the cycle: $001 \to 100$, $101 \to 100$, $110 \to 001$, $111 \to 100$).

**Code check:** simulating from $000$ visited $000, 010, 011, 100$ and returned to $000$.

**Key idea:** follow the next-state equations from the reset state until the sequence repeats; count the distinct states in the cycle.

</details>
