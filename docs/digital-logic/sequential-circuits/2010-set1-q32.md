# 2010 CS Q32: Count sequence of two T flip-flops

## Question

In the sequential circuit shown below, if the initial value of the output $Q_1 Q_0$ is $00$, what are the next four values of $Q_1 Q_0$?

![Two positive-edge-triggered T flip-flops. The first has T tied to 1 and its clock input driven by Clock; its Q output is Q0. A wire leaves the top of the first flip-flop and goes round to the T input of the second flip-flop. The second flip-flop's clock input is driven by Q0, and its Q output is Q1.](../../assets/2010/set1-q32.png)

- (A) 11, 10, 01, 00
- (B) 10, 11, 01, 00
- (C) 10, 00, 01, 11
- (D) 11, 10, 00, 01

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Reading of the figure: the first flip-flop has $T = 1$ and is clocked by `Clock`, so $Q_0$ toggles on **every** clock edge. The wire that runs from the top of the first flip-flop to the second $T$ input is read as the same constant $1$. The second flip-flop is clocked by $Q_0$, so $Q_1$ toggles whenever $Q_0$ goes from $0$ to $1$ (triangle = positive edge).

| Clock pulse | $Q_0$ | edge on $Q_0$ | $Q_1$ | $Q_1 Q_0$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| start | 0 | | 0 | 00 |
| 1 | 1 | rising | 1 | 11 |
| 2 | 0 | falling | 1 | 10 |
| 3 | 1 | rising | 0 | 01 |
| 4 | 0 | falling | 0 | 00 |

The sequence is $11, 10, 01, 00$: (A). This is a 2-bit down counter.

- (B) and (C) start with $10$, but $Q_0$ must become $1$ after the first pulse.
- (D) has $00$ as its third value, but $Q_0$ must be $1$ after the third pulse.

**Code check:** a simulation of this reading gave $11, 10, 01, 00$.

**Read by eye:** the drawing is ambiguous about where the wire into the second $T$ starts. I read it as the constant $1$ on the first $T$; this reading reproduces the official key. A different source for that wire would change the sequence.

**Key idea:** a T flip-flop with $T = 1$ divides its clock by 2; chaining on $Q_0$ makes an asynchronous (ripple) counter.

</details>
