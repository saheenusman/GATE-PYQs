# 2011 CS Q50: Next state of a three flip-flop counter

## Question

**Common data for Questions 50 and 51.** Consider the following circuit involving three D-type flip-flops used in a certain type of counter configuration.

![A circuit with three D flip-flops P, Q and R sharing one clock. P's D input is wired to R's Q output. Q's D input comes from a NOR gate whose two inputs are the Q outputs of P and R. R's D input comes from an AND gate whose two inputs are the Q output of Q and the complemented output of R (Q-bar of R).](../../assets/2011/set1-q50.png)

If at some instance prior to the occurrence of the clock edge, $P$, $Q$ and $R$ have a value $0$, $1$ and $0$ respectively, what shall be the value of $PQR$ after the clock edge?

- (A) 000
- (B) 001
- (C) 010
- (D) 011

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Reading the wiring from the figure.** (Traced by hand from the printed circuit; the crossings are drawn with small hops.)

- $P$'s D input is $R$.
- $Q$'s D input is $\overline{P + R}$ (a NOR of $P$ and $R$).
- $R$'s D input is $Q \cdot \overline{R}$ (an AND of $Q$ and $\bar R$, the complemented output of the third flip-flop).

So on a clock edge: $P^+ = R$, $\quad Q^+ = \overline{P + R}$, $\quad R^+ = Q \cdot \bar R$.

For $(P, Q, R) = (0, 1, 0)$:

- $P^+ = R = 0$
- $Q^+ = \overline{0 + 0} = 1$
- $R^+ = 1 \cdot \bar 0 = 1$

New value $PQR = 011$: (D).

- (A) $000$, (B) $001$ and (C) $010$ do not match: $Q^+$ and $R^+$ are both $1$.

**Code check:** a simulation of these three equations gave $010 \to 011$.

**Key idea:** each D flip-flop takes its D input as the next state; evaluate the three input equations on the current state.

</details>
