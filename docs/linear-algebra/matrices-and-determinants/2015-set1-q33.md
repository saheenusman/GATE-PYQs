# 2015 CS1 Q33: Lower diagonal entry of L in an LU decomposition

## Question

In the LU decomposition of the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 4 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$, if the diagonal elements of $U$ are both 1, then the lower diagonal entry $l_{22}$ of $L$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**5**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Write $A = LU$ with

$$L = \begin{bmatrix} l_{11} & 0 \\ l_{21} & l_{22} \end{bmatrix},\qquad U = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & u_{12} \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$LU = \begin{bmatrix} l_{11} & l_{11}u_{12} \\ l_{21} & l_{21}u_{12} + l_{22} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 4 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$$

- $l_{11} = 2$
- $l_{11}u_{12} = 2 \Rightarrow u_{12} = 1$
- $l_{21} = 4$
- $l_{21}u_{12} + l_{22} = 9 \Rightarrow 4 + l_{22} = 9 \Rightarrow l_{22} = 5$

**Key idea:** with a unit-diagonal $U$, match entries of $LU$ with $A$ row by row (Crout form).

</details>
