# 2013 CS Q3: Determinant that does NOT equal Vandermonde

## Question

Which one of the following does **NOT** equal
$\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x & x^2\\ 1 & y & y^2\\ 1 & z & z^2\end{vmatrix}$ ?

- (A) $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x(x+1) & x+1\\ 1 & y(y+1) & y+1\\ 1 & z(z+1) & z+1\end{vmatrix}$
- (B) $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & x+1 & x^2+1\\ 1 & y+1 & y^2+1\\ 1 & z+1 & z^2+1\end{vmatrix}$
- (C) $\begin{vmatrix} 0 & x-y & x^2-y^2\\ 0 & y-z & y^2-z^2\\ 1 & z & z^2\end{vmatrix}$
- (D) $\begin{vmatrix} 2 & x+y & x^2+y^2\\ 2 & y+z & y^2+z^2\\ 1 & z & z^2\end{vmatrix}$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $\Delta$ be the given determinant. It is the Vandermonde determinant, $\Delta = (y-x)(z-x)(z-y)$.

**Test each option with row/column operations:**

- (B): $C_2 \to C_2 - C_1$ and $C_3 \to C_3 - C_1$ turn it into $\Delta$. Equal.
- (C): this is $\Delta$ after $R_1 \to R_1 - R_2$ and $R_2 \to R_2 - R_3$. Equal.
- (D): this is $\Delta$ after $R_1 \to R_1 + R_2$ and $R_2 \to R_2 + R_3$. Equal (the operation matrix is unit upper triangular, determinant 1).
- (A): the columns are $1,\ x^2+x,\ x+1$. In the basis $(1, x, x^2)$ these are $(1,0,0),\ (0,1,1),\ (1,1,0)$. The coefficient matrix has determinant $-1$, so (A) $= -\Delta$.

(A) differs from $\Delta$ by a sign, so it is not equal.

**Code check:** sympy gave (B), (C), (D) identical to $\Delta$ and (A) equal to $-\Delta$.

**Key idea:** a row or column operation changes the determinant by the determinant of its operation matrix. Here (B), (C), (D) use operations of determinant 1, while (A) gives $-1$.

</details>
