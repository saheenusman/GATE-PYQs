# 2014 CS2 Q4: Determinant of a rank-one matrix

## Question

If the matrix $A$ is such that

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ -4 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 9 & 5 \end{bmatrix}$$

then the determinant of $A$ is equal to ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$A$ is a column times a row, an outer product, so every row of $A$ is a multiple of $[1\ 9\ 5]$:

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 18 & 10 \\ -4 & -36 & -20 \\ 7 & 63 & 35 \end{bmatrix}$$

The rows are linearly dependent (rank 1 < 3), so $\det A = 0$.

**Code check:** `numpy.linalg.det` of this matrix gives 0.

**Key idea:** an outer product has rank 1, so its determinant is zero for size above 1.

</details>
