# 2026 CS2 Q31: Parameters giving infinitely many solutions

## Question

Consider the system of linear equations given below.

$$ax + y = b$$
$$16x + ay = 24$$

Suppose the values of $a$ and $b$ are chosen such that the system of linear equations produce multiple solutions. Then the product of $a$ and $b$ is ________. (answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**24**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Condition for multiple solutions:** the two equations must describe the **same line**, so the coefficient matrix is singular *and* the system is consistent.

**Step 1: singular.**

$$\det\begin{pmatrix} a & 1 \\ 16 & a \end{pmatrix} = a^2 - 16 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; a = \pm 4$$

**Step 2: consistent.** Row 2 must be a multiple of row 1, including the right-hand side.

| $a$ | Row 2 = $k \times$ Row 1 | Needs | $b$ | $ab$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $4$ | $k = 4$ | $24 = 4b$ | $6$ | $24$ |
| $-4$ | $k = -4$ | $24 = -4b$ | $-6$ | $24$ |

Both cases give $ab = \mathbf{24}$.

**Check:** for $a=4, b=6$ the equations are $4x + y = 6$ and $16x + 4y = 24$. The second is $4\times$ the first, so infinitely many solutions. 

**Key idea:** infinite solutions needs $\det = 0$ and rank of the coefficient matrix equal to the rank of the augmented matrix.

</details>
