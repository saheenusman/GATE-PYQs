# 2016 CS1 Q5: Determinant of a real 3x3 matrix from two eigenvalues

## Question

Two eigenvalues of a $3 \times 3$ real matrix $P$ are $(2 + \sqrt{-1})$ and $3$. The determinant of $P$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**15**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Complex eigenvalues of a real matrix come in conjugate pairs.** The characteristic polynomial has real coefficients, so $2 + i$ forces $2 - i$ to be an eigenvalue too. With $3$ that makes all three eigenvalues:

$$2 + i,\quad 2 - i,\quad 3$$

**Determinant = product of eigenvalues.**

$$\det P = (2+i)(2-i)\cdot 3 = (4 + 1)\cdot 3 = 15$$

**Key idea:** conjugate pairing of complex eigenvalues, then $\det$ = product of eigenvalues.

</details>
