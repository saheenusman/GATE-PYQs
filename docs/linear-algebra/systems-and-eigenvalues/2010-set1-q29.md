# 2010 CS Q29: Unknown entries from the eigenvalues of a 2 by 2 matrix

## Question

Consider the following matrix

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ x & y \end{bmatrix}$$

If the eigenvalues of $A$ are $4$ and $8$, then

- (A) $x = 4,\ y = 10$
- (B) $x = 5,\ y = 8$
- (C) $x = -3,\ y = 9$
- (D) $x = -4,\ y = 10$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

For a $2 \times 2$ matrix, the eigenvalues satisfy:

- **sum = trace:** $4 + 8 = 2 + y$, so $y = 10$.
- **product = determinant:** $4 \times 8 = 2y - 3x$, so $32 = 20 - 3x$ and $x = -4$.

So $x = -4,\ y = 10$: (D).

- (A) has the right $y$ but $\det = 20 - 12 = 8 \ne 32$.
- (B) has trace $2 + 8 = 10 \ne 12$.
- (C) has trace $2 + 9 = 11 \ne 12$.

**Code check:** `numpy.linalg.eigvals` on each option gave eigenvalues $\lbrace 4, 8 \rbrace$ only for option (D). The others gave about $0.71, 11.29$ (A), $0.10, 9.90$ (B) and $3.70, 7.30$ (C).

**Key idea:** eigenvalue sum = trace and eigenvalue product = determinant.

</details>
