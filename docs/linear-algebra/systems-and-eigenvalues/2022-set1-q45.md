# 2022 CS Q45: LU decomposition of a 3×3 system

## Question

Consider solving the following system of simultaneous equations using LU decomposition.

$$x_1 + x_2 - 2x_3 = 4$$
$$x_1 + 3x_2 - x_3 = 7$$
$$2x_1 + x_2 - 5x_3 = 7$$

where $L$ and $U$ are denoted as

$$L = \begin{pmatrix} L_{11} & 0 & 0 \\ L_{21} & L_{22} & 0 \\ L_{31} & L_{32} & L_{33} \end{pmatrix},\quad U = \begin{pmatrix} U_{11} & U_{12} & U_{13} \\ 0 & U_{22} & U_{23} \\ 0 & 0 & U_{33} \end{pmatrix}$$

Which one of the following is the correct combination of values for $L_{32}$, $U_{33}$, and $x_1$?

- (A) $L_{32} = 2,\ U_{33} = -\tfrac12,\ x_1 = -1$
- (B) $L_{32} = 2,\ U_{33} = 2,\ x_1 = -1$
- (C) $L_{32} = -\tfrac12,\ U_{33} = 2,\ x_1 = 0$
- (D) $L_{32} = -\tfrac12,\ U_{33} = -\tfrac12,\ x_1 = 0$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D ($L_{32} = -1/2$, $U_{33} = -1/2$, $x_1 = 0$)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Decompose** $A = LU$ with $L_{11} = L_{22} = L_{33} = 1$ (the Doolittle form). Gaussian elimination on $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -2 \\ 1 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 1 & -5 \end{pmatrix}$:

- $R_2 \leftarrow R_2 - 1\cdot R_1$ (multiplier $L_{21} = 1$): row 2 becomes $(0, 2, 1)$.
- $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 - 2\cdot R_1$ (multiplier $L_{31} = 2$): row 3 becomes $(0, -1, -1)$.
- $R_3 \leftarrow R_3 + \tfrac12 R_2$ (multiplier $L_{32} = -\tfrac12$): row 3 becomes $(0, 0, -\tfrac12)$.

$$L = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -\tfrac12 & 1 \end{pmatrix},\quad U = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & -\tfrac12 \end{pmatrix}$$

So $L_{32} = -\tfrac12$ and $U_{33} = -\tfrac12$.

**Solve.** $Ly = b$ with $b = (4, 7, 7)$: $y_1 = 4$, $y_2 = 7 - 4 = 3$, $y_3 = 7 - 8 + \tfrac32 = \tfrac12$. Then $Ux = y$: $x_3 = \tfrac12 \big/ (-\tfrac12) = -1$, $2x_2 + x_3 = 3$ gives $x_2 = 2$, and $x_1 + 2 + 2 = 4$ gives $x_1 = 0$.

The solution is $(x_1, x_2, x_3) = (0, 2, -1)$, so option D.

**Convention note:** the question does not say that $L$ has a unit diagonal. With the other common convention (Crout: unit diagonal in $U$), $L_{32} = -1$ and $U_{33} = 1$, which is not in any option. So the key assumes the unit-diagonal $L$ above.

**Code check:** a symbolic LU decomposition gave the same $L$, $U$ and solution.

**Key idea:** $L$ holds the elimination multipliers and $U$ the reduced matrix; solve with forward then back substitution.

</details>
