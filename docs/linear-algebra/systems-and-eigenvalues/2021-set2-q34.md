# 2021 CS2 Q34: Rank of a matrix from its null space

## Question

Suppose that $P$ is a $4\times5$ matrix such that every solution of the equation $Px=0$ is a scalar multiple of $[2\ 5\ 4\ 3\ 1]^T$. The rank of $P$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**4**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The null space of $P$ is contained in the span of one non-zero vector, so its dimension (the nullity) is at most 1.

$P$ has 5 columns and only 4 rows, so $\operatorname{rank}(P)\le4$. By rank-nullity, nullity $=5-\operatorname{rank}(P)\ge1$.

So nullity $=1$ exactly, and

$$\operatorname{rank}(P)=5-1=4.$$

A numeric check: a $4\times5$ matrix built from four vectors orthogonal to $[2\ 5\ 4\ 3\ 1]^T$ has rank 4 and sends that vector to 0.

**Key idea:** rank + nullity = number of columns.

</details>
