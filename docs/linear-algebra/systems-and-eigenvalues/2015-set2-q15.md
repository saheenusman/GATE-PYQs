# 2015 CS2 Q15: Larger eigenvalue of a 2x2 matrix

## Question

The larger of the two eigenvalues of the matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**6**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Trace $= 4 + 1 = 5$ and determinant $= 4\cdot 1 - 5 \cdot 2 = -6$.

$$\lambda^2 - 5\lambda - 6 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; (\lambda - 6)(\lambda + 1) = 0$$

Eigenvalues are $6$ and $-1$. The larger is **6**.

**Key idea:** $\lambda^2 - (\text{trace})\lambda + \det = 0$ for a $2\times2$ matrix.

</details>
