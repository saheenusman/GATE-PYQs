# 2016 CS2 Q6: Determinant of the transpose of the inverse of a matrix

## Question

Suppose that the eigenvalues of matrix $A$ are 1, 2, 4. The determinant of $(A^{-1})^T$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.125**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- $\det A$ is the product of the eigenvalues: $1 \times 2 \times 4 = 8$.
- $\det(A^{-1}) = 1/\det A = 1/8$.
- A transpose has the same determinant, so $\det\big((A^{-1})^T\big) = 1/8 = 0.125$.

**Key idea:** $\det = 1/\prod\lambda_i = 1/8$ (inverse flips it, transpose keeps it).

</details>
