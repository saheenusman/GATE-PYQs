# 2021 CS1 Q62: Largest eigenvalue of the 4x4 matrix J minus I

## Question

Consider the following matrix.

$$\begin{pmatrix}0&1&1&1\\1&0&1&1\\1&1&0&1\\1&1&1&0\end{pmatrix}$$

The largest eigenvalue of the above matrix is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The matrix is $A=J-I$, where $J$ is the all-ones $4\times4$ matrix.

- $J$ has rank 1. Its eigenvalues are $4$ (eigenvector all ones) and $0$ (three times).
- Subtracting $I$ shifts every eigenvalue by $-1$: $A$ has eigenvalues $3,-1,-1,-1$.

Check with the all-ones vector: every row sums to 3, so $A\mathbf 1=3\cdot\mathbf 1$.

The largest eigenvalue is **3**. A numerical check with `numpy.linalg.eigvalsh` gave $[-1,-1,-1,3]$.

**Key idea:** for a matrix with constant row sums $r$, $r$ is an eigenvalue (eigenvector all ones), and here it is the largest.

</details>
