# 2007 CS Q25: Eigenvalue of a block matrix

## Question

Let $A$ be a $4 \times 4$ matrix with eigenvalues $-5, -2, 1, 4$. Which of the following is an eigenvalue of
$$\begin{bmatrix} A & I \\ I & A \end{bmatrix},$$
where $I$ is the $4 \times 4$ identity matrix?

- (A) $-5$
- (B) $-7$
- (C) $2$
- (D) $1$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $Av = \lambda v$. Then
$$\begin{bmatrix} A & I \\ I & A \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v \\ v \end{bmatrix} = (\lambda + 1)\begin{bmatrix} v \\ v \end{bmatrix},\qquad \begin{bmatrix} A & I \\ I & A \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v \\ -v \end{bmatrix} = (\lambda - 1)\begin{bmatrix} v \\ -v \end{bmatrix}.$$

So the eight eigenvalues are $\lambda \pm 1$ for each $\lambda \in \lbrace -5,-2,1,4 \rbrace$:
$$-6,\ -4,\ -3,\ -1,\ 0,\ 2,\ 3,\ 5.$$

$2 = 1 + 1$ is in the list: (C).

- (A) $-5$ would need $\lambda = -4$ or $-6$, which are not eigenvalues of $A$.
- (B) $-7$ would need $\lambda = -6$ or $-8$.
- (D) $1$ would need $\lambda = 0$ or $2$.

**Code check:** numpy: for a random matrix with eigenvalues $-5,-2,1,4$, the block matrix has eigenvalues $-6,-4,-3,-1,0,2,3,5$.

**Key idea:** eigenvalues of $\begin{bmatrix}A&I\\I&A\end{bmatrix}$ are those of $A+I$ and $A-I$.
