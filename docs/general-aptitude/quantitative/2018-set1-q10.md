# 2018 CS Q10: Most likely outcome of seven rolls of a biased die

## Question

A six sided unbiased die with four green faces and two red faces is rolled seven times. Which of the following combinations is the most likely outcome of the experiment?

- (A) Three green faces and four red faces.
- (B) Four green faces and three red faces.
- (C) Five green faces and two red faces.
- (D) Six green faces and one red face.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Probabilities per roll:** $P(\text{green}) = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{2}{3}$ and $P(\text{red}) = \frac{1}{3}$.

For $k$ greens in 7 rolls:

$$P(k) = \binom{7}{k}\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^k\left(\tfrac{1}{3}\right)^{7-k} = \frac{\binom{7}{k}2^k}{3^7}$$

| Option | Greens $k$ | $\binom{7}{k}2^k$ | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 3 | $35\cdot8 = 280$ | 0.128 |
| B | 4 | $35\cdot16 = 560$ | 0.256 |
| C | 5 | $21\cdot32 = 672$ | 0.307 |
| D | 6 | $7\cdot64 = 448$ | 0.205 |

Option C has the largest value, so it is the most likely.

**Key idea:** compare binomial terms $\binom{n}{k}p^k q^{n-k}$; the peak is near $np = 7\cdot\frac{2}{3}\approx 4.7$, so 5 greens wins.

</details>
