# 2018 CS Q8: Ratio of males to females among attendees

## Question

In a party, 60% of the invited guests are male and 40% are female. If 80% of the invited guests attended the party and if all the invited female guests attended, what would be the ratio of males to females among the attendees in the party?

- (A) 2:3
- (B) 1:1
- (C) 3:2
- (D) 2:1

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Take 100 invited guests.** 60 are male and 40 are female.

- Attendees: 80% of 100 $= 80$.
- All 40 females attended, so males who attended $= 80 - 40 = 40$.

Ratio of males to females $= 40 : 40 = 1:1$.

**Why the others fail:** 3:2 would be the *invited* ratio (60:40). The question asks about *attendees*, and the 20 absent guests are all male.

**Key idea:** pick a convenient total (100) and track the counts, not the percentages.

</details>
