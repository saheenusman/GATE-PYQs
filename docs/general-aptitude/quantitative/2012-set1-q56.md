# 2012 CS Q56: Output that maximises profit

## Question

The cost function for a product in a firm is given by $5q^2$, where $q$ is the amount of production. The firm can sell the product at a market price of ₹ 50 per unit. The number of units to be produced by the firm such that the profit is maximized is

- (A) 5
- (B) 10
- (C) 15
- (D) 25

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Profit $= $ revenue $-$ cost $= 50q - 5q^2$.

$$\frac{d}{dq}(50q - 5q^2) = 50 - 10q = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; q = 5.$$

The second derivative is $-10 < 0$, so this is a maximum: (A).

- (B) $10$ makes profit $50 \cdot 10 - 500 = 0$.
- (C), (D) give negative profit ($-375$ and $-1875$).

**Code check:** a search over $q = 0..29$ put the maximum at $q = 5$ (profit $125$).

**Key idea:** profit is maximal where marginal revenue equals marginal cost ($50 = 10q$).

</details>
