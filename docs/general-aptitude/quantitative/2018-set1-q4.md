# 2018 CS Q4: Smallest number leaving remainder 7 for 20, 42 and 76

## Question

What would be the smallest natural number which when divided either by 20 or by 42 or by 76 leaves a remainder of 7 in each case?

- (A) 3047
- (B) 6047
- (C) 7987
- (D) 63847

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Subtract the remainder.** If $N$ leaves remainder 7 each time, then $N-7$ is a common multiple of 20, 42 and 76.

**LCM:** $20 = 2^2\cdot5$, $42 = 2\cdot3\cdot7$, $76 = 2^2\cdot19$.

$$\text{LCM} = 2^2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot19 = 7980$$

The smallest positive choice is $N = 7980 + 7 = 7987$.

**Why the others fail:** $3047-7 = 3040$ and $6047-7 = 6040$ are not multiples of 42. $63847-7 = 63840 = 8\cdot7980$ works, but it is not the smallest.

**Note on the wording:** $N = 7$ also leaves remainder 7 (quotient 0), but 7 is not an option. The question intends the smallest $N > 7$, which is 7987.

**Key idea:** "remainder $r$ in each case" means $N - r$ is a multiple of the LCM.

</details>
