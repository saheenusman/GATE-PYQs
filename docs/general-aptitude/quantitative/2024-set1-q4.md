# 2024 CS1 Q4: Share of money in ₹5 coins

## Question

The number of coins of ₹1, ₹5, and ₹10 denominations that a person has are in the ratio $5:3:13$. Of the total amount, the percentage of money in ₹5 coins is

- (A) 21%
- (B) $14\frac{2}{7}\%$
- (C) 10%
- (D) 30%

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C (10%)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Take 5, 3, 13 coins.** Money in each denomination:

| Coin | Count | Value |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1 | 5 | 5 |
| ₹5 | 3 | 15 |
| ₹10 | 13 | 130 |

Total = $5 + 15 + 130 = 150$. Share of ₹5 coins:

$$\frac{15}{150} = 10\%$$

**Key idea:** a ratio of counts is not a ratio of money. Multiply each count by its coin value first.

</details>
