# 2024 CS1 Q3: Median of a sample

## Question

Consider the following sample of numbers:

$9, 18, 11, 14, 15, 17, 10, 69, 11, 13$

The median of the sample is

- (A) 13.5
- (B) 14
- (C) 11
- (D) 18.7

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A (13.5)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Sort the 10 values:**

$9, 10, 11, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 69$

With an even count, the median is the mean of the 5th and 6th values:

$$\frac{13 + 14}{2} = 13.5$$

**Why the others fail:** 14 is just the 6th value. 11 is the mode. 18.7 is the **mean** ($187/10$), which the outlier 69 drags upward.

**Key idea:** the median ignores outliers. For an even count, average the two middle values.

</details>
