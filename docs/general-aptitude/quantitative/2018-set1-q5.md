# 2018 CS Q5: Missing term of 2, 12, 60, 240, 720, 1440

## Question

What is the missing number in the following sequence?

2, 12, 60, 240, 720, 1440, _____, 0

- (A) 2880
- (B) 1440
- (C) 720
- (D) 0

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Look at the ratios of consecutive terms:**

| From | To | Multiplied by |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 12 | 6 |
| 12 | 60 | 5 |
| 60 | 240 | 4 |
| 240 | 720 | 3 |
| 720 | 1440 | 2 |
| 1440 | ? | 1 |
| ? | 0 | 0 |

The multiplier drops by 1 each step. The multiplier 1 gives $1440\times1 = 1440$, and the next multiplier 0 gives $0$, as shown.

**Why the others fail:** 2880 would use multiplier 2 again, and 720 or 0 break the pattern 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0.

**Key idea:** when terms grow then end in 0, check the *ratios*; a falling multiplier ending at 0 explains the final 0.

</details>
