# 2012 CS Q64: Effect of adding and scaling a list on mean and standard deviation

## Question

Which of the following assertions are **CORRECT**?

- **P:** Adding $7$ to each entry in a list adds $7$ to the mean of the list
- **Q:** Adding $7$ to each entry in a list adds $7$ to the standard deviation of the list
- **R:** Doubling each entry in a list doubles the mean of the list
- **S:** Doubling each entry in a list leaves the standard deviation of the list unchanged

Options:

- (A) P, Q
- (B) Q, R
- (C) P, R
- (D) R, S

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- **P true.** The mean shifts by the constant: $\bar{x} + 7$.
- **Q false.** The spread does not change when every value shifts: the standard deviation is unchanged.
- **R true.** The mean scales with the entries: $2\bar{x}$.
- **S false.** The standard deviation also scales: it doubles.

So P and R: (C).

**Code check:** on a sample list, the mean rose by exactly $7$, the standard deviation changed by $0$ under the shift, and both mean and standard deviation doubled under doubling.

**Key idea:** shifting changes location only; scaling changes both location and spread.

</details>
