# 2014 CS3 Q59: The odd number in the series 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50, 64

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q4) Which number does not belong in the series below?

2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50, 64

- (A) 17
- (B) 37
- (C) 64
- (D) 26

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Each term has the form $n^2 + 1$:

$$1^2+1=2,\ 2^2+1=5,\ 3^2+1=10,\ 4^2+1=17,\ 5^2+1=26,\ 6^2+1=37,\ 7^2+1=50,\ 8^2+1=65.$$

The last term should be $65$, so **64** does not belong.

**Code check:** `[n*n+1 for n in range(1,9)]` gives `[2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50, 65]`.

**Key idea:** look at the differences ($3, 5, 7, 9, \ldots$) or compare with squares.

</details>
