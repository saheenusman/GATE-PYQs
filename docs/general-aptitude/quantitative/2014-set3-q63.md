# 2014 CS3 Q63: Change in GDP in USD when the rupee weakens

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q8) The Gross Domestic Product (GDP) in Rupees grew at 7% during 2012-2013. For international comparison, the GDP is compared in US Dollars (USD) after conversion based on the market exchange rate. During the period 2012-2013 the exchange rate for the USD increased from Rs. 50/USD to Rs. 60/USD. India's GDP in USD during the period 2012-2013

- (A) increased by 5 %
- (B) decreased by 13%
- (C) decreased by 20%
- (D) decreased by 11%

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let the GDP in 2012 be $G$ rupees.

- 2012 in USD: $G/50$.
- 2013 in USD: $1.07\,G/60$.

$$\frac{\text{GDP}_{2013}}{\text{GDP}_{2012}} = \frac{1.07/60}{1/50} = \frac{1.07 \times 50}{60} = 0.8917$$

So the GDP in USD fell by about $10.8\% \approx 11\%$.

**Code check:** `1.07*50/60` gives `0.89166...`.

Wrong options: (A) ignores the exchange rate: the rupee GDP grew 7%, but each USD now costs 20% more rupees. (B) and (C) do not match the calculation: $-13\%$ would need a ratio of $0.87$, and $-20\%$ a ratio of $0.80$.

**Key idea:** percentage changes multiply: $1.07 \times (50/60)$.

</details>
