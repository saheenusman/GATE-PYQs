# 2021 CS1 Q8: Probability that two chocolates are identical

## Question

There are five bags each containing identical sets of ten distinct chocolates. One chocolate is picked from each bag.

The probability that at least two chocolates are identical is ______.

- (A) 0.3024
- (B) 0.4235
- (C) 0.6976
- (D) 0.8125

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Each pick has 10 equally likely outcomes, so there are $10^5$ equally likely picks.

Use the complement. All five chocolates are different in $10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6=30240$ ways.

$P(\text{at least two identical})=1-\dfrac{30240}{100000}=1-0.3024=0.6976$

Option (C). Option (A) 0.3024 is the probability that all five are different, i.e. the complement.

Verified numerically: $1-P(10,5)/10^5=0.6976$.

**Key idea:** "at least two the same" is the complement of "all different" (the birthday problem).

</details>
