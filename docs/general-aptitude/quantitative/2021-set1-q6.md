# 2021 CS1 Q6: Volume ratio of a rolled cylinder to a cube

## Question

We have 2 rectangular sheets of paper, M and N, of dimensions 6 cm x 1 cm each.

Sheet M is rolled to form an open cylinder by bringing the short edges of the sheet together. Sheet N is cut into equal square patches and assembled to form the largest possible closed cube. Assuming the ends of the cylinder are closed, the ratio of the volume of the cylinder to that of the cube is ______.

- (A) $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
- (B) $\dfrac{3}{\pi}$
- (C) $\dfrac{9}{\pi}$
- (D) $3\pi$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Cylinder.** Bringing the 1 cm edges together means the 6 cm side wraps round. Circumference $=6$ and height $h=1$.

$2\pi r=6 \Rightarrow r=3/\pi$

$V_{cyl}=\pi r^2 h=\pi\cdot\dfrac{9}{\pi^2}=\dfrac{9}{\pi}$

**Cube.** Sheet N has area 6, and a cube has 6 faces, so each face is $1\ \text{cm}^2$ (side 1). $V_{cube}=1$.

Ratio $=\dfrac{9/\pi}{1}=\dfrac{9}{\pi}$, which is option (C).

Verified numerically: ratio $=2.8648$, and $9/\pi=2.8648$.

**Key idea:** the sheet's rolled edge becomes the circumference, so $r=C/2\pi$.

</details>
