# 2015 CS2 Q4: Weight of ten poles from two statements

## Question

Based on the given statements, select the most appropriate option to solve the given question.

What will be the total weight of 10 poles each of same weight?

Statements:

- (I) One fourth of the weight of a pole is 5 kg.
- (II) The total weight of these poles is 160 kg more than the total weight of two poles.

- (A) Statement I alone is not sufficient.
- (B) Statement II alone is not sufficient.
- (C) Either I or II alone is sufficient.
- (D) Both statements I and II together are not sufficient.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let one pole weigh $w$ kg.

- Using I: $w/4 = 5 \Rightarrow w = 20$, so 10 poles weigh $200$ kg. Sufficient.
- Using II: $10w = 2w + 160 \Rightarrow 8w = 160 \Rightarrow w = 20$, so again $200$ kg. Sufficient.

Each statement alone is enough: option (C). (A) and (B) each call a sufficient statement insufficient, and (D) is wrong since even one statement suffices.

**Key idea:** each statement independently determines $w = 20$.

</details>
