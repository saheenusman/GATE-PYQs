# 2026 CS2 Q4: Profit from two stocks

## Question

The values of Stock A and Stock B on a particular day are Rs. 50 and Rs. 80, respectively. An investor invests Rs. 100 in Stock A and Rs. 80 in Stock B. He sells all the stocks the next day when the value of Stock A is Rs. 55 and Stock B is Rs. 70. The profit made by the investor is Rs. ________

- (A) 0
- (B) 5
- (C) 10
- (D) 20

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Work out how many units of each stock he bought.

| Stock | Price | Invested | Units | New price | Sale value | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 50 | 100 | $100/50 = 2$ | 55 | $2 \times 55 = 110$ | **+10** |
| B | 80 | 80 | $80/80 = 1$ | 70 | $1 \times 70 = 70$ | **−10** |

Total profit $= 10 - 10 = \mathbf{0}$.

**Check:** invested $100 + 80 = 180$, got back $110 + 70 = 180$.

**Key idea:** convert money to units first. A percentage change on each holding adds up to the total gain or loss.

</details>
