# 2019 CS2 Q9: Total students given club overlaps and 75% outside

## Question

In a college, there are three student clubs. Sixty students are only in the Drama club, 80 students are only in the Dance club, 30 students are only in the Maths club, 40 students are in both Drama and Dance clubs, 12 students are in both Dance and Maths clubs, 7 students are in both Drama and Maths clubs, and 2 students are in all the clubs. If 75% of the students in the college are not in any of these clubs, then the total number of students in the college is _____.

- (A) 1000
- (B) 975
- (C) 900
- (D) 225

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Reading the overlaps.** "Both Drama and Dance" includes the 2 students who are in all three clubs. So the students in **exactly** two clubs are:

| Region | Count |
|---|---|
| Drama and Dance only | $40 - 2 = 38$ |
| Dance and Maths only | $12 - 2 = 10$ |
| Drama and Maths only | $7 - 2 = 5$ |

**Students in some club:**

$$60 + 80 + 30 + 38 + 10 + 5 + 2 = 225.$$

These are the 25% who are in a club, so the total is $225 / 0.25 = 900$.

**Why the others fail:** 225 is only the club members. If the "both" counts excluded the triple overlap, the club total would be 231 and the college 924, which is not an option. So the intended reading is the one above.

**Key idea:** inclusion-exclusion. Subtract the triple overlap from each pairwise "both" count.

</details>
