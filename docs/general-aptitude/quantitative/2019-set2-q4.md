# 2019 CS2 Q4: Cost of a gift when two friends drop out

## Question

Ten friends planned to share equally the cost of buying a gift for their teacher. When two of them decided not to contribute, each of the other friends had to pay Rs 150 more. The cost of the gift was Rs. ____.

- (A) 666
- (B) 3000
- (C) 6000
- (D) 12000

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let the cost be $C$. Each of the 10 would pay $C/10$. After two drop out, each of the 8 pays $C/8$.

$$\frac{C}{8} - \frac{C}{10} = 150 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{C}{40} = 150 \;\Rightarrow\; C = 6000.$$

**Check:** $6000/10 = 600$ and $6000/8 = 750$, a difference of 150.

**Why the others fail:** 3000 gives a difference of 75, and 12000 gives 300. 666 gives about 8.

**Key idea:** write the per-person share before and after, and set the difference equal to the given extra amount.

</details>
