# 2016 CS1 Q61: Razor type with the greatest share of yearly revenue

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q6) A shaving set company sells 4 different types of razors, Elegance, Smooth, Soft and Executive. Elegance sells at Rs. 48, Smooth at Rs. 63, Soft at Rs. 78 and Executive at Rs. 173 per piece. The table below shows the numbers of each razor sold in each quarter of a year.

| Quarter | Elegance | Smooth | Soft | Executive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 27300 | 20009 | 17602 | 9999 |
| Q2 | 25222 | 19392 | 18445 | 8942 |
| Q3 | 28976 | 22429 | 19544 | 10234 |
| Q4 | 21012 | 18229 | 16595 | 10109 |

Which product contributes the greatest fraction to the revenue of the company in that year?

- (A) Elegance
- (B) Executive
- (C) Smooth
- (D) Soft

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Revenue = price $\times$ yearly units.

| Product | Units | Revenue (Rs.) |
|---|---|---|
| Elegance | 102,510 | 4,920,480 |
| Smooth | 80,059 | 5,043,717 |
| Soft | 72,186 | 5,630,508 |
| Executive | 39,284 | 6,796,132 |

Executive is the largest, about $30\%$ of the total of $22{,}390{,}837$. **(B)**.

**Shortcut.** Rounding gives Executive $\approx 39{,}000 \times 173 \approx 6.7$ million and Soft $\approx 72{,}000 \times 78 \approx 5.6$ million, so exact sums are not needed.

**Code check:** the totals above come from running the sums.

**Key idea:** revenue is price $\times$ units; the dearest product (Executive) wins despite the fewest units.

</details>
