# 2015 CS1 Q10: Average marks from a table of correct answers

## Question

The number of students in a class who have answered correctly, wrongly, or not attempted each question in an exam, are listed in the table below. The marks for each question are also listed. There is no negative or partial marking.

| Q No. | Marks | Answered Correctly | Answered Wrongly | Not Attempted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 21 | 17 | 6 |
| 2 | 3 | 15 | 27 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 | 11 | 29 | 4 |
| 4 | 2 | 23 | 18 | 3 |
| 5 | 5 | 31 | 12 | 1 |

What is the average of the marks obtained by the class in the examination?

- (A) 2.290
- (B) 2.970
- (C) 6.795
- (D) 8.795

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Only correct answers earn marks.

| Q | Marks $\times$ correct | Total |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $2 \times 21$ | 42 |
| 2 | $3 \times 15$ | 45 |
| 3 | $1 \times 11$ | 11 |
| 4 | $2 \times 23$ | 46 |
| 5 | $5 \times 31$ | 155 |

Total marks $= 299$. Each row sums to $44$ students ($21+17+6=44$, and so on).

$$\text{Average} = \frac{299}{44} \approx 6.795$$

Option (C). (A) and (B) are the average per question, not per student. (D) adds the wrong amount.

**Key idea:** average per student $=$ total marks $\div$ number of students.

</details>
