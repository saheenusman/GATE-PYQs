# 2021 CS1 Q1: Total number of students for a 7 to 3 ratio

## Question

The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 7 to 3.

Among the options below, an acceptable value for the total number of students in the class is:

- (A) 21
- (B) 37
- (C) 50
- (D) 73

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Boys $=7k$ and girls $=3k$ for a whole number $k$, so the total is $10k$.

The total must be a multiple of 10.

- (A) 21: not a multiple of 10.
- (B) 37: not a multiple of 10.
- (C) 50: $k=5$, so 35 boys and 15 girls. Valid.
- (D) 73: not a multiple of 10.

**Key idea:** a ratio $a:b$ forces the total to be a multiple of $a+b$.

</details>
