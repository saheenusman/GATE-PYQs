# 2011 CS Q63: Production quantity minimizing total cost

## Question

The variable cost ($V$) of manufacturing a product varies according to the equation $V = 4q$, where $q$ is the quantity produced. The fixed cost ($F$) of production of same product reduces with $q$ according to the equation $F = 100/q$. How many units should be produced to minimize the total cost ($V + F$)?

- (A) 5
- (B) 4
- (C) 7
- (D) 6

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Total cost $C(q) = 4q + \dfrac{100}{q}$.

$$C'(q) = 4 - \frac{100}{q^2} = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; q^2 = 25 \;\Rightarrow\; q = 5$$

$C''(q) = \dfrac{200}{q^3} > 0$, so this is a minimum. $C(5) = 20 + 20 = 40$.

Values near it: $C(4) = 41$, $C(6) = 40.67$, $C(7) = 42.29$.

So (A).

**Code check:** the minimum of $4q + 100/q$ over $q = 1..19$ is at $q = 5$ with cost $40$.

**Key idea:** minimize 4q + 100/q by setting the derivative to zero (or by AM-GM when the two terms are equal).

</details>
