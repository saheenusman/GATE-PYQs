# 2019 CS2 Q3: Time for two cars to be 20 km apart

## Question

Two cars start at the same time from the same location and go in the same direction. The speed of the first car is 50 km/h and the speed of the second car is 60 km/h. The number of hours it takes for the distance between the two cars to be 20 km is ___.

- (A) 1
- (B) 2
- (C) 3
- (D) 6

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Both cars move the same way, so the gap grows at the **difference** of their speeds:

$$60 - 50 = 10 \text{ km/h}.$$

Time for a 20 km gap: $t = 20 / 10 = 2$ hours.

**Why the others fail:** after 1 h the gap is 10 km, after 3 h it is 30 km, after 6 h it is 60 km.

**Key idea:** for motion in the same direction, use the relative speed (difference).

</details>
