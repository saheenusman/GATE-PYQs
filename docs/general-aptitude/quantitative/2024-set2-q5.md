# 2024 CS2 Q5: Missing term in a number sequence

## Question

In the sequence $6, 9, 14, x, 30, 41$, a possible value of $x$ is

- (A) 25
- (B) 21
- (C) 18
- (D) 20

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B (21)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Look at the differences:** $9-6 = 3$, $14-9 = 5$. The differences are consecutive odd numbers: $3, 5, 7, 9, 11$.

| Term | Value | Added |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | |
| 2 | 9 | 3 |
| 3 | 14 | 5 |
| 4 | **21** | 7 |
| 5 | 30 | 9 |
| 6 | 41 | 11 |

So $x = 14 + 7 = 21$, and the later terms 30 and 41 fit.

**Key idea:** if the terms look irregular, check the differences of consecutive terms.

</details>
