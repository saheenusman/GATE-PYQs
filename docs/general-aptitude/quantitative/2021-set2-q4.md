# 2021 CS2 Q4: Solve a difference of two squares equation

## Question

If $\left(x-\dfrac12\right)^2-\left(x-\dfrac32\right)^2=x+2$, then the value of $x$ is:

- (A) 2
- (B) 4
- (C) 6
- (D) 8

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Use $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$ with $a=x-\tfrac12$ and $b=x-\tfrac32$.

- $a-b=1$
- $a+b=2x-2$

So the left side is $2x-2$. Setting $2x-2=x+2$ gives $x=4$.

Check: $(3.5)^2-(2.5)^2=12.25-6.25=6=4+2$. (B).

**Key idea:** the difference of two squares factors, and here it makes the quadratic terms cancel.

</details>
