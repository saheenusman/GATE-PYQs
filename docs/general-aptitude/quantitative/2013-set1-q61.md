# 2013 CS Q61: Telescoping sum of reciprocals of square-root sums

## Question

Find the sum of the expression

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt1+\sqrt2} + \frac{1}{\sqrt2+\sqrt3} + \frac{1}{\sqrt3+\sqrt4} + \cdots + \frac{1}{\sqrt{80}+\sqrt{81}}$$

- (A) 7
- (B) 8
- (C) 9
- (D) 10

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rationalise each term:

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt k+\sqrt{k+1}} = \frac{\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k}{(k+1)-k} = \sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k$$

The sum telescopes from $k=1$ to $80$:

$$\sqrt{81} - \sqrt{1} = 9 - 1 = 8$$

**Why the others fail:** 9 forgets to subtract $\sqrt1$; 7 and 10 come from miscounting the end terms.

**Code check:** the numeric sum was $8.0$.

**Key idea:** rationalising turns the terms into a telescoping series.

</details>
