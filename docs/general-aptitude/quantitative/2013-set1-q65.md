# 2013 CS Q65: Erection cost with changed wages and hours

## Question

The current erection cost of a structure is Rs. 13,200. If the labour wages per day increase by 1/5 of the current wages and the working hours decrease by 1/24 of the current period, then the new cost of erection in Rs. is

- (A) 16,500
- (B) 15,180
- (C) 11,000
- (D) 10,120

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Cost $=$ wage per day $\times$ number of days.

- Wage per day becomes $\tfrac65$ of the old wage.
- The period decreases by $1/24$, so the number of days becomes $\tfrac{23}{24}$ of the old.

$$\text{New cost} = 13200 \times \frac65 \times \frac{23}{24} = 13200 \times \frac{138}{120} = 15180$$

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) $16500 = 13200\times\frac54$ treats the wage rise as $+1/4$.
- (C) $11000 = 13200\times\frac56$ and (D) $10120 = 13200\times\frac45\times\frac{23}{24}$ apply a wage *decrease* instead of an increase.

**Code check:** the product was Rs. 15,180.

**Key idea:** multiply the cost by the factor for each change; "increase by $1/5$" is a factor of $6/5$.

</details>
