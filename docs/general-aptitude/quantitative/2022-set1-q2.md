# 2022 CS Q2: Area under a step function

## Question

A function $y(x)$ is defined in the interval $[0, 1]$ on the $x$-axis as

$$y(x) = \begin{cases} 2 & 0 \le x < \tfrac13 \\ 3 & \tfrac13 \le x < \tfrac34 \\ 1 & \tfrac34 \le x \le 1 \end{cases}$$

Which one of the following is the area under the curve for the interval $[0, 1]$ on the $x$-axis?

- (A) $5/6$
- (B) $6/5$
- (C) $13/6$
- (D) $6/13$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C ($13/6$)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Add the area of each rectangle** (height × width):

$$2\cdot\tfrac13 + 3\left(\tfrac34-\tfrac13\right) + 1\left(1-\tfrac34\right) = \tfrac23 + \tfrac54 + \tfrac14 = \tfrac23 + \tfrac32 = \tfrac{13}{6}$$

**Why the others fail:** the heights are at least 1 over an interval of length 1, so the area is at least 1. This rules out $5/6$ and $6/13$. The average height is $13/6 \approx 2.17$, which sits between 1 and 3. $6/5 = 1.2$ is too small for that.

**Key idea:** the area under a step function is the sum of height × width over its steps.

</details>
