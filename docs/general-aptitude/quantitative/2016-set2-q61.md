# 2016 CS2 Q61: Faculty connected only through Facebook

## Question

(GA Set-6, Q6) Among 150 faculty members in an institute, 55 are connected with each other through Facebook® and 85 are connected through WhatsApp®. 30 faculty members do not have Facebook® or WhatsApp® accounts. The number of faculty members connected only through Facebook® accounts is ______________.

- (A) 35
- (B) 45
- (C) 65
- (D) 90

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Faculty on at least one platform: $150 - 30 = 120$.

By inclusion-exclusion, the number on both:

$$|F \cap W| = 55 + 85 - 120 = 20$$

Only Facebook: $55 - 20 = 35$, **(A)**.

**Key idea:** union $= 120$, both $= 20$, so only Facebook $= 55 - 20 = 35$.

</details>
