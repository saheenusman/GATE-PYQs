# 2010 CS Q59: People playing neither hockey nor football

## Question

$25$ persons are in a room. $15$ of them play hockey, $17$ of them play football and $10$ of them play both hockey and football. Then the number of persons playing neither hockey nor football is:

- (A) $2$
- (B) $17$
- (C) $13$
- (D) $3$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

By inclusion-exclusion, the number playing at least one sport is

$$15 + 17 - 10 = 22.$$

So $25 - 22 = 3$ play neither: (D).

- (B) $17$ is just the number who play football.
- (A) $2$ and (C) $13$ are not $25 - 22$.

**Code check:** arithmetic: $25 - (15 + 17 - 10) = 3$.

**Key idea:** $|H \cup F| = |H| + |F| - |H \cap F|$.

</details>
