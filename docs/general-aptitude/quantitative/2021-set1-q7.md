# 2021 CS1 Q7: Discount on item Q as a percentage of marked price

## Question

Details of prices of two items P and Q are presented in the table below.

| Item | Cost (Rs) | Profit % | Marked Price (Rs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | 5,400 | --- | 5,860 |
| Q | --- | 25 | 10,000 |

The ratio of cost of item P to cost of item Q is 3:4. Discount is calculated as the difference between the marked price and the selling price. The profit percentage is calculated as the ratio of the difference between selling price and cost, to the cost $\left(\text{Profit \%} = \dfrac{\text{Selling price}-\text{Cost}}{\text{Cost}}\times 100\right)$.

The discount on item Q, as a percentage of its marked price, is ______.

- (A) 25
- (B) 12.5
- (C) 10
- (D) 5

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- Cost of Q: $5400\times\dfrac{4}{3}=7200$.
- Selling price of Q at 25% profit: $7200\times1.25=9000$.
- Discount on Q: $10000-9000=1000$.
- As a percentage of the marked price: $\dfrac{1000}{10000}\times100=10\%$.

Option (C). The other options come from mixing up the base: 25 is the profit %, and 12.5 and 5 are not obtained from any consistent step. The P row is only there to find Q's cost.

Verified numerically: cost of Q $=7200$, selling price $=9000$, discount $=10.0\%$.

**Key idea:** profit % is on cost, discount % is on marked price. Use the right base for each.

</details>
