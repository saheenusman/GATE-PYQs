# 2012 CS Q65: Next term in a letter-pair sequence

## Question

Given the sequence of terms, AD CG FK JP, the next term is

- (A) OV
- (B) OW
- (C) PV
- (D) PW

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Treat the letters as numbers ($A = 1, B = 2, \dots$).

| Term | First letter | Second letter |
|---|---|---|
| AD | 1 | 4 |
| CG | 3 | 7 |
| FK | 6 | 11 |
| JP | 10 | 16 |

First letters: differences $2, 3, 4$, so the next difference is $5$: $10 + 5 = 15 = $ **O**.

Second letters: differences $3, 4, 5$, so the next difference is $6$: $16 + 6 = 22 = $ **V**.

The next term is OV: (A).

- (B) OW, (C) PV and (D) PW each break one of the two difference patterns.

**Code check:** differences of the letter positions came out as $2, 3, 4$ and $3, 4, 5$, giving $15$ (O) and $22$ (V).

**Key idea:** in letter sequences, convert to numbers and look at successive differences.

</details>
