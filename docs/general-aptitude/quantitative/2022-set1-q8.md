# 2022 CS Q8: Probability of orange on the second draw

## Question

A box contains five balls of same size and shape. Three of them are green coloured balls and two of them are orange coloured balls. Balls are drawn from the box one at a time. If a green ball is drawn, it is not replaced. If an orange ball is drawn, it is replaced with another orange ball.

First ball is drawn. What is the probability of getting an orange ball in the next draw?

- (A) $1/2$
- (B) $8/25$
- (C) $19/50$
- (D) $23/50$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D ($23/50$)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Split on the colour of the first ball.**

- First green ($3/5$): the box now has 2 green and 2 orange, so $P(\text{orange}) = 2/4$.
- First orange ($2/5$): it is replaced by another orange, so the box is unchanged (3 green, 2 orange) and $P(\text{orange}) = 2/5$.

$$P = \tfrac35\cdot\tfrac12 + \tfrac25\cdot\tfrac25 = \tfrac{15}{50} + \tfrac{8}{50} = \tfrac{23}{50}$$

**Why the others fail:** $1/2$ ignores the replacement rule. $8/25 = 16/50$ is only the "orange then orange" branch. $19/50$ does not match either branch.

**Key idea:** total probability, conditioning on the first draw.

</details>
