# 2013 CS Q62: Probability a 2-digit number is not divisible by 7

## Question

Out of all the 2-digit integers between 1 and 100, a 2-digit number has to be selected at random. What is the probability that the selected number is not divisible by 7?

- (A) 13/90
- (B) 12/90
- (C) 78/90
- (D) 77/90

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- Two-digit integers: 10 to 99, so $90$ of them.
- Multiples of 7 in that range: $14 = 7\cdot2, \dots, 98 = 7\cdot14$. That is $13$ numbers.
- Not divisible: $90 - 13 = 77$.

$$P = \frac{77}{90}$$

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) $13/90$ is the probability of being divisible by 7.
- (B) $12/90$ miscounts the multiples (leaves out one).
- (C) $78/90$ miscounts the multiples (counts 12).

**Code check:** counting gave 13 multiples out of 90.

**Key idea:** count the complement, then subtract.

</details>
