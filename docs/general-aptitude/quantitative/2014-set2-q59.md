# 2014 CS2 Q59: Average of the multiples of 10 from 2 to 198

## Question

(GA Set-8, Q4) What is the average of all multiples of 10 from 2 to 198?

- (A) 90
- (B) 100
- (C) 110
- (D) 120

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The multiples of 10 in $[2, 198]$ are $10, 20, \ldots, 190$. They form an arithmetic progression, so the average is the mean of the first and last terms:

$$\frac{10 + 190}{2} = 100$$

- (A) 90 and (C) 110 are off-centre values.
- (D) 120 would need the list to run up to 230.

**Key idea:** the mean of an arithmetic progression is (first + last)/2.

</details>
