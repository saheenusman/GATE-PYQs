# 2024 CS1 Q7: Cylinder and cube from the same sheet

## Question

A rectangular paper sheet of dimensions $54\text{ cm} \times 4\text{ cm}$ is taken. The two longer edges of the sheet are joined together to create a cylindrical tube. A cube whose surface area is equal to the area of the sheet is also taken.

Then, the ratio of the volume of the cylindrical tube to the volume of the cube is

- (A) $1/\pi$
- (B) $2/\pi$
- (C) $3/\pi$
- (D) $4/\pi$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A ($1/\pi$)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Cylinder.** Joining the two *longer* (54 cm) edges makes the **4 cm side the circumference** and 54 cm the height.

$$2\pi r = 4 \Rightarrow r = \frac{2}{\pi},\qquad V_{cyl} = \pi r^2 h = \pi\cdot\frac{4}{\pi^2}\cdot 54 = \frac{216}{\pi}.$$

**Cube.** Surface area $= 54 \times 4 = 216$, so $6a^2 = 216$, $a = 6$, $V_{cube} = 216$.

$$\frac{V_{cyl}}{V_{cube}} = \frac{216/\pi}{216} = \frac{1}{\pi}.$$

**Why the others fail:** 2, 3, 4 over $\pi$ come from wrongly using the 54 cm side as the circumference or from slips in $r$.

**Key idea:** decide which edge becomes the circumference before computing the radius.

</details>
