# 2013 CS Q58: Maximum sum of 44, 42, 40, ...

## Question

What will be the maximum sum of 44, 42, 40, ...... ?

- (A) 502
- (B) 504
- (C) 506
- (D) 500

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The series is an arithmetic progression with $a = 44$, $d = -2$. Terms stay non-negative down to $2$ (the next is $0$, which adds nothing; negative terms reduce the sum).

Number of positive terms: $44 = 2\times22$, so the terms are $2\times22, 2\times21, \dots, 2\times1$, which is $22$ terms.

$$S = \frac{22}{2}(44 + 2) = 11 \times 46 = 506$$

**Why the others fail:** 504 stops before the last term 2 (21 terms), and 500 stops after 20 terms. 502 is not a partial sum of the series.

**Code check:** $44 + 42 + \dots + 2 = 506$.

**Key idea:** the sum is maximum when all positive terms are included and no negative term is.

</details>
