# 2015 CS2 Q8: Evaluating nested max, min and remainder functions

## Question

If $p, q, r, s$ are distinct integers such that:

- $f(p,q,r,s) = \max(p,q,r,s)$
- $g(p,q,r,s) = \min(p,q,r,s)$
- $h(p,q,r,s) =$ remainder of $(p \times q)/(r \times s)$ if $(p \times q) > (r \times s)$, or remainder of $(r \times s)/(p \times q)$ if $(r \times s) > (p \times q)$

Also a function $fgh(p,q,r,s) = f(p,q,r,s) \times g(p,q,r,s) \times h(p,q,r,s)$. Also the same operations are valid with two variable functions of the form $f(p,q)$.

What is the value of $fg(h(2,5,7,3), 4, 6, 8)$?

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**8**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Inner call.** $h(2,5,7,3)$: $p\times q = 10$ and $r\times s = 21$. Since $21 > 10$, take the remainder of $21/10$, which is $1$.

**Outer call.** $fg(1, 4, 6, 8) = f(1,4,6,8) \times g(1,4,6,8) = \max \times \min = 8 \times 1 = 8$.

(The product $fg$ follows the pattern of $fgh$, with the two functions $f$ and $g$ multiplied.)

**Key idea:** evaluate the inner function first, then apply $\max \times \min$ to the four values.

</details>
