# 2026 CS1 Q4: Maximum elective credits

## Question

A student needs to enroll for a minimum of 60 credits. A student cannot enroll for more than 70 credits. The credits are divided amongst project and three distinct sets of courses namely, core courses, specialization courses, and elective courses. It is compulsory for a student to enroll for exactly 15 credits of core courses and exactly 20 credits of project. In addition, a student has to enroll for a minimum of 10 credits of specialization courses.

The maximum credits of elective courses that a student can enroll for is ________

- (A) 10
- (B) 15
- (C) 20
- (D) 25

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**25**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

To make electives as large as possible, make everything else as small as allowed and the total as large as allowed.

| Part | Credits |
|---|---|
| Total (maximum) | 70 |
| Core (fixed) | 15 |
| Project (fixed) | 20 |
| Specialization (minimum) | 10 |
| **Electives (maximum)** | $70 - 15 - 20 - 10 = \mathbf{25}$ |

**Why not use 60?** 60 is the *minimum* total. The maximum elective credits come from the maximum total, 70.

**Key idea:** to maximise one part, take the largest total and the smallest allowed value of every other part.

</details>
