# 2014 CS1 Q63: Fare for a group of 5 on round-trip tickets

## Question

(GA Set-7, Q8) Round-trip tickets to a tourist destination are eligible for a discount of 10% on the total fare. In addition, groups of 4 or more get a discount of 5% on the total fare. If the one way single person fare is Rs 100, a group of 5 tourists purchasing round-trip tickets will be charged Rs _________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**850**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Total fare for 5 people, round trip: $5 \times 2 \times 100 = 1000$.

Both discounts apply to the *total fare*, so they add: $10\% + 5\% = 15\%$.

$$1000 \times (1 - 0.15) = 850$$

**Reading note:** applying them one after the other gives $1000 \times 0.9 \times 0.95 = 855$. The official key is 850, so "in addition ... on the total fare" is read as additive.

**Code check:** `5*200*(1-0.10-0.05)` gives `850.0`.

**Key idea:** discounts stated on the same base add.

</details>
