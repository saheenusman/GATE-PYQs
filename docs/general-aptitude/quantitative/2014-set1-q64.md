# 2014 CS1 Q64: Percent of respondents who do not own a scooter

## Question

(GA Set-7, Q9) In a survey, 300 respondents were asked whether they own a vehicle or not. If yes, they were further asked to mention whether they own a car or scooter or both. Their responses are tabulated below. What percent of respondents do not own a scooter?

| | | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own vehicle | Car | 40 | 34 |
| | Scooter | 30 | 20 |
| | Both | 60 | 46 |
| Do not own vehicle | | 20 | 50 |

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**48**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Owners of a scooter are the *Scooter* and *Both* rows:

$$30 + 20 + 60 + 46 = 156$$

Check the total: $40+34+30+20+60+46+20+50 = 300$.

Not owning a scooter: $300 - 156 = 144$.

$$\frac{144}{300} \times 100 = 48\%$$

**Code check:** `(300-(30+20+60+46))/300*100` gives `48.0`.

**Key idea:** "Both" owners also own a scooter; count them.

</details>
