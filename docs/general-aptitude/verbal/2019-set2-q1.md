# 2019 CS2 Q1: Verb choice for expenditure that is itemised

## Question

The expenditure on the project _____ as follows: equipment Rs.20 lakhs, salaries Rs.12 lakhs, and contingency Rs.3 lakhs.

- (A) break down
- (B) break
- (C) breaks down
- (D) breaks

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Step 1: agreement.** The subject is "the expenditure", which is singular. The verb needs the singular form with **-s**. That removes (A) and (B).

**Step 2: meaning.** The sentence lists the parts of the expenditure. The phrasal verb for that is **break down** ("divide into parts"), so the full verb is "breaks down".

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) *break down*: plural verb with a singular subject.
- (B) *break*: plural again, and "break" alone does not mean "itemise".
- (D) *breaks*: agrees with the subject, but "breaks as follows" does not mean "is itemised as follows".

**Key idea:** check subject-verb agreement first, then pick the phrasal verb that carries the intended meaning.

</details>
