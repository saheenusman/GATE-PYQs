# 2014 CS1 Q58: Meaning of 'The buck stops here'

## Question

(GA Set-7, Q3) In a press meet on the recent scam, the minister said, "The buck stops here". What did the minister convey by the statement?

- (A) He wants all the money
- (B) He will return the money
- (C) He will assume final responsibility
- (D) He will resist all enquiries

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The idiom *the buck stops here* means the speaker accepts final responsibility and will not pass the blame on.

- (A) and (B) take *buck* literally as money.
- (D) accepting responsibility is the opposite of resisting enquiries.

**Key idea:** idiom: accept final responsibility.

</details>
