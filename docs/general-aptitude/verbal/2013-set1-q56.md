# 2013 CS Q56: Closest in meaning to Nadir

## Question

Which one of the following options is the closest in meaning to the word given below?

**Nadir**

- (A) Highest
- (B) Lowest
- (C) Medium
- (D) Integration

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Nadir* is the lowest point, the point opposite the *zenith*. So the closest meaning is **Lowest**.

**Why the others fail:** "Highest" is the opposite (zenith). "Medium" and "Integration" have nothing to do with a low point.

**Key idea:** nadir = lowest point; zenith = highest point.

</details>
