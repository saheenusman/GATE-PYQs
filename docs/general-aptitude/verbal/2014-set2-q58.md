# 2014 CS2 Q58: Match words with their meanings

## Question

(GA Set-8, Q3) Match the columns.

| Column 1 | Column 2 |
|---|---|
| 1) eradicate | P) misrepresent |
| 2) distort | Q) soak completely |
| 3) saturate | R) use |
| 4) utilize | S) destroy utterly |

- (A) 1:S, 2:P, 3:Q, 4:R
- (B) 1:P, 2:Q, 3:R, 4:S
- (C) 1:Q, 2:R, 3:S, 4:P
- (D) 1:S, 2:P, 3:R, 4:Q

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- *eradicate* means to destroy utterly: **S**.
- *distort* means to misrepresent or twist: **P**.
- *saturate* means to soak completely: **Q**.
- *utilize* means to use: **R**.

That is 1:S, 2:P, 3:Q, 4:R. Only (A) has all four right.

**Key idea:** word-meaning matching.

</details>
