# 2014 CS3 Q58: Antonym of 'coherent'

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q3) Choose the word that is opposite in meaning to the word "coherent".

- (A) sticky
- (B) well-connected
- (C) rambling
- (D) friendly

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Coherent* means logical and well organised, with parts that fit together. Its opposite is *rambling* (disorganised and wandering).

- (A) *sticky* (adhesive) is unrelated to logic.
- (B) *well-connected* is a near-synonym of coherent.
- (D) *friendly* is unrelated.

**Key idea:** the antonym of "coherent" is a word for disorganised thought or speech.

</details>
