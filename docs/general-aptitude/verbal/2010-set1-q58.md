# 2010 CS Q58: We would leave a better planet if we manage to ___ our resources

## Question

Choose the most appropriate word from the options given below to complete the following sentence:

If we manage to ____________ our natural resources, we would leave a better planet for our children.

- (A) uphold
- (B) restrain
- (C) cherish
- (D) conserve

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The idea is to protect resources from being used up, so that something is left for the next generation. That is *conserve*: (D).

- (A) *uphold* is to support or maintain a principle or decision.
- (B) *restrain* is to hold back a person or an action.
- (C) *cherish* is to hold dear emotionally, and it does not suit a resource that must be managed.

**Key idea:** "conserve" means to protect from waste or loss.

</details>
