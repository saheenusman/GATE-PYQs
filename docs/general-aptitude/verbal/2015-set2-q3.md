# 2015 CS2 Q3: Generic term for items of clothing

## Question

A generic term that includes various items of clothing such as a skirt, a pair of trousers and a shirt is

- (A) fabric
- (B) textile
- (C) fibre
- (D) apparel

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Apparel* means clothing in general. *Fabric*, *textile* and *fibre* name the material that clothes are made from, not the finished items.

Option (D).

**Key idea:** *apparel* names clothing; the other words name the material.

</details>
