# 2010 CS Q57: Closest in meaning to circuitous

## Question

Which of the following options is closest in meaning to the word *circuitous*?

- (A) cyclic
- (B) indirect
- (C) confusing
- (D) crooked

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Circuitous* describes a route that is longer and less direct than it needs to be, a roundabout way. The closest word is *indirect*: (B).

- (A) *cyclic* means repeating in cycles, not taking a roundabout route.
- (C) *confusing* is about being hard to follow, not about directness.
- (D) *crooked* means bent, or dishonest; it does not mean roundabout.

**Key idea:** circuitous = roundabout = indirect.

</details>
