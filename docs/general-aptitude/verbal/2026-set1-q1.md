# 2026 CS1 Q1: Antonym of protagonist

## Question

The antonym of the word *protagonist* is ________.

- (A) agnostic
- (B) antagonist
- (C) arsonist
- (D) anarchist

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**antagonist**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- A **protagonist** is the main character, the one who drives the story.
- An **antagonist** is the one who opposes the protagonist. That is the direct opposite.

**Why the others fail:** an *agnostic* is someone unsure about the existence of God, an *arsonist* sets fires, and an *anarchist* rejects authority. None of them is the opposite role of "main character".

**Key idea:** *pro-* means for or forward, *ant(i)-* means against. Protagonist and antagonist are paired opposites.

</details>
