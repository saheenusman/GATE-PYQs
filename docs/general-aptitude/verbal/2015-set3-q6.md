# 2015 CS3 Q6: What can be inferred about Alexander turning to India

## Question

Alexander turned his attention towards India, since he had conquered Persia.

Which one of the statements below is logically valid and can be inferred from the above sentence?

- (A) Alexander would not have turned his attention towards India had he not conquered Persia.
- (B) Alexander was not ready to rest on his laurels, and wanted to march to India.
- (C) Alexander was completely in control of his army and could command it to move towards India.
- (D) Since Alexander's kingdom extended to Indian borders after the conquest of Persia, he was keen to move further.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The sentence gives the conquest of Persia as the **reason** he turned to India.

- (A) restates that reason as a condition: without the conquest, no turn to India. Valid.
- (B) adds his attitude ("not ready to rest") that the sentence never states.
- (C) adds that he controlled his army, which is not stated.
- (D) adds that his kingdom reached the Indian border, which is also not stated.

**Key idea:** a valid inference only restates what the sentence supports. Extra facts about motives or geography do not follow.

</details>
