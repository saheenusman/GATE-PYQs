# 2011 CS Q61: Topic that a unit on bereavement would not include

## Question

Few school curricula include a unit on how to deal with bereavement and grief, and yet all students at some point in their lives suffer losses through death and parting.

Based on the above passage which topic would **not** be included in a unit on bereavement?

- (A) how to write a letter of condolence
- (B) what emotional stages are passed through in the healing process
- (C) what the leading causes of death are
- (D) how to give support to a grieving friend

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The passage is about teaching students to **cope** with loss: grief, healing, and support.

- (A) writing a condolence letter is a way of responding to a loss, so it fits.
- (B) the emotional stages of healing are the core of understanding grief.
- (D) supporting a grieving friend is part of dealing with bereavement.
- (C) the leading causes of death is a statistical topic about *why people die*, not how to deal with losing someone, so it would **not** be included: (C).

**Key idea:** pick the option that does not help students deal with grief.

</details>
