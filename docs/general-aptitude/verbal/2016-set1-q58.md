# 2016 CS1 Q58: Classifying Archimedes' lever statement

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q3) Archimedes said, "Give me a lever long enough and a fulcrum on which to place it, and I will move the world."

The sentence above is an example of a ___________ statement.

- (A) figurative
- (B) collateral
- (C) literal
- (D) figurine

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Nobody can literally move the world with a lever; the statement is an exaggeration used to show the power of leverage. That is **figurative** language, (A).

- (B) collateral means secondary or additional; it is not a type of statement here.
- (C) literal would mean he meant it word for word.
- (D) figurine is a small statuette.

**Key idea:** an exaggeration meant to convey an idea is figurative.

</details>
