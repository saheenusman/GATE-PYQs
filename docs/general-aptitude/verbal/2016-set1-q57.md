# 2016 CS1 Q57: Word for a rewording of something written or spoken

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q2) A rewording of something written or spoken is a ______________.

- (A) paraphrase
- (B) paradox
- (C) paradigm
- (D) paraffin

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- (A) **paraphrase**: restating something in other words.
- (B) paradox: a statement that seems self-contradictory but may be true.
- (C) paradigm: a model or pattern.
- (D) paraffin: a waxy fuel.

**Key idea:** paraphrase = restating in other words.

</details>
