# 2026 CS2 Q1: Words similar to expedite, hasten, hurry

## Question

Expedite, Hasten, Hurry, ________

Fill the blank by choosing a word with a meaning similar to that of the words given above.

- (A) Accelerate
- (B) Retard
- (C) Provide
- (D) Disable

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Accelerate**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

All three given words mean *to speed something up* or *make it happen faster*.

- **Accelerate** also means to speed up. It fits.
- **Retard** means to slow down. That is the opposite.
- **Provide** and **Disable** have no link with speed.

**Key idea:** find the common meaning of the given words, then look for a synonym, not an antonym.

</details>
