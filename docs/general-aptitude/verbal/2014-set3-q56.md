# 2014 CS3 Q56: Which underlined part of the sentence is not appropriate

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q1) While trying to collect an envelope from under the table, Mr. X fell down and was losing consciousness.

Which one of the above underlined parts of the sentence is NOT appropriate?

The four underlined parts are: (I) "While trying to collect", (II) "from under the table", (III) "Mr. X fell down", (IV) "was losing consciousness".

- (A) I
- (B) II
- (C) III
- (D) IV

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The sentence narrates two completed events in the past: Mr. X **fell**, and then he **lost** consciousness. Part IV uses the past continuous, "was losing", which describes an ongoing process and does not fit the sequence. It should be "lost consciousness".

- I, "While trying to collect", is a fine participial opening.
- II, "from under the table", is a correct prepositional phrase.
- III, "Mr. X fell down", is correct simple past.

**Key idea:** use the simple past for a sequence of completed actions; the continuous form needs an ongoing background.

</details>
