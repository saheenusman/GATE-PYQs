# 2022 CS Q1: Fill the blanks: fare and fair

## Question

The _________ is too high for it to be considered _________.

- (A) fair / fare
- (B) faer / fair
- (C) fare / fare
- (D) fare / fair

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D (fare / fair)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Identify each blank.** The first blank needs a noun meaning the price of a journey: *fare*. The second needs an adjective meaning "reasonable": *fair*. "The fare is too high for it to be considered fair."

**Why the others fail:**
- (A) *fair* as a noun and *fare* as an adjective: both are the wrong parts of speech.
- (B) *faer* is not a word.
- (C) *fare* in the second blank is a noun, so "considered fare" makes no sense.

**Key idea:** homophones (fare/fair) are separated by the part of speech each blank needs.

</details>
