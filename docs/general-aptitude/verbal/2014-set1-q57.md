# 2014 CS1 Q57: Word that fits: judges awarding first prize

## Question

(GA Set-7, Q2) Choose the most appropriate word from the options given below to complete the following sentence.

He could not understand the judges awarding her the first prize, because he thought that her performance was quite __________.

- (A) superb
- (B) medium
- (C) mediocre
- (D) exhilarating

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

He is puzzled that she won, so he must think the performance was poor or ordinary: **mediocre**.

- (A) *superb* and (D) *exhilarating* are praise, so he would not be puzzled.
- (B) *medium* is a size or degree, not a judgement of quality. *Mediocre* is the word that implies "not good enough to win".

**Key idea:** the clause "could not understand ... awarding her" signals a negative judgement.

</details>
