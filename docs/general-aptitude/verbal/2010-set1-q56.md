# 2010 CS Q56: His casual remarks on politics ___ his lack of seriousness

## Question

Choose the most appropriate word from the options given below to complete the following sentence:

His rather casual remarks on politics ___________ his lack of seriousness about the subject.

- (A) masked
- (B) belied
- (C) betrayed
- (D) suppressed

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The casual remarks **reveal**, usually without meaning to, that he is not serious. The word for that is *betrayed* (gave away): (C).

- (A) *masked* and (D) *suppressed* mean hid or held back, the opposite of what the remarks do.
- (B) *belied* means contradicted or gave a false impression of. Casual remarks do not contradict a lack of seriousness; they show it.

**Key idea:** "betray" can mean to reveal something unintentionally.

</details>
