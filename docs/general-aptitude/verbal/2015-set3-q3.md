# 2015 CS3 Q3: Choosing the right words for a film-news sentence

## Question

The Tamil version of ______ John Abraham-starrer *Madras Cafe* ______ cleared by the Censor Board with no cuts last week, but the film's distributors ______ no takers among the exhibitors for a release in Tamil Nadu ______ this Friday.

- (A) Mr., was, found, on
- (B) a, was, found, at
- (C) the, was, found, on
- (D) a, being, find at

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Fill the four blanks in order: "The Tamil version of **the** John Abraham-starrer *Madras Cafe* **was** cleared ... the distributors **found** no takers ... **on** this Friday."

- (C) the, was, found, on. Correct.
- (A) "Mr." is wrong before "John Abraham-starrer".
- (B) "at this Friday" needs "on" (a day takes "on").
- (D) "being cleared" gives no finite verb, and "find" is the wrong tense after "distributors".

**Key idea:** a specific film takes *the*; a day of the week takes *on*; the past tense goes with "last week".

</details>
