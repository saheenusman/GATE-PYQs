# 2014 CS2 Q57: Complete: 'Who ___ was coming to see us this evening?'

## Question

(GA Set-8, Q2) Who ___________ was coming to see us this evening?

- (A) you said
- (B) did you say
- (C) did you say that
- (D) had you said

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

This is a direct question with the embedded clause *was coming*, as in "Who did you say was coming ...?". The auxiliary *did* goes with *say* and no *that* is needed.

- (A) *you said* is a statement order, not a question.
- (C) *that* before *was coming* breaks the question: "Who did you say that was coming" is not standard.
- (D) *had you said* changes the tense and does not fit "this evening".

**Key idea:** question form with *did you say* embedding the clause.

</details>
