# 2011 CS Q60: Opposite of amalgamate

## Question

Choose the word from the options given below that is most nearly opposite in meaning to the given word:

**Amalgamate**

- (A) merge
- (B) split
- (C) collect
- (D) separate

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Amalgamate* means to combine or unite into one.

- (A) *merge* and (C) *collect* are close to the same meaning, not opposite.
- (B) *split* and (D) *separate* both express the opposite idea. *Separate* is the standard antonym of "combine or unite", and *split* more often means to break into parts forcefully or divide a share. The key gives (D).

**Judgement note.** Both (B) and (D) can be defended as opposites; the choice of (D) rests on "separate" being the usual direct antonym of "combine".

**Key idea:** the antonym of combining into one is separating.

</details>
