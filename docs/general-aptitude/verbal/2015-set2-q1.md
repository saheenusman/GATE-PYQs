# 2015 CS2 Q1: Word order of adverbs in a two-blank sentence

## Question

We ______ our friend's birthday and we ______ how to make it up to him.

- (A) completely forgot --- don't just know
- (B) forgot completely --- don't just know
- (C) completely forgot --- just don't know
- (D) forgot completely --- just don't know

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- **Second blank:** "we *just don't know* how to make it up" means "we simply have no idea". "Don't just know" would mean "do not merely know", which does not fit. This rules out (A) and (B).
- **First blank:** *completely forgot* is the natural order, with the adverb before the verb. This picks (C) over (D).

Option (C).

**Key idea:** "just" goes before "don't" to mean "simply"; "completely" sits before the verb it intensifies.

</details>
