# 2025 CS2 Q6: What can be inferred from Ramya's remark

## Question

Based only on the conversation below, identify the logically correct inference:

"Even if I had known that you were in the hospital, I would not have gone there to see you", Ramya told Josephine.

- (A) Ramya knew that Josephine was in the hospital.
- (B) Ramya did not know that Josephine was in the hospital.
- (C) Ramya and Josephine were once close friends; but now, they are not.
- (D) Josephine was in the hospital due to an injury to her leg.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

"If I had known ..., I would not have ..." is a **past counterfactual**: it describes a situation that did not happen. So Ramya did **not** actually know about the hospital stay.

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A | The opposite of what the counterfactual implies |
| **B** | Follows from "if I **had** known" |
| C | Nothing in the sentence says they were once close |
| D | The cause of the hospital stay is never mentioned |

**Key idea:** "Even if I had known" implies that the speaker did **not** know.

</details>
