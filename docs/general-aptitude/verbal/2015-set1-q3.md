# 2015 CS1 Q3: Identifying the incorrect word pair

## Question

Which one of the following combinations is incorrect?

- (A) Acquiescence - Submission
- (B) Wheedle - Roundabout
- (C) Flippancy - Lightness
- (D) Profligate - Extravagant

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Check whether each pair is a (near-)synonym pair.

- (A) *acquiescence* is passive acceptance, close to *submission*. Fine.
- (B) *wheedle* means to coax or cajole someone, which is not close to *roundabout* (indirect). **Incorrect pair.**
- (C) *flippancy* is a lack of seriousness, close to *lightness*. Fine.
- (D) *profligate* means wastefully extravagant. Fine.

**Key idea:** the odd pair out is the one whose words do not share a meaning.

</details>
