# 2014 CS3 Q61: Instruction for the dance programme

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q6) A dance programme is scheduled for 10.00 a.m. Some students are participating in the programme and they need to come an hour earlier than the start of the event. These students should be accompanied by a parent. Other students and parents should come in time for the programme. The instruction you think that is appropriate for this is

- (A) Students should come at 9.00 a.m. and parents should come at 10.00 a.m.
- (B) Participating students should come at 9.00 a.m. accompanied by a parent, and other parents and students should come by 10.00 a.m.
- (C) Students who are not participating should come by 10.00 a.m. and they should not bring their parents. Participating students should come at 9.00 a.m.
- (D) Participating students should come before 9.00 a.m. Parents who accompany them should come at 9.00 a.m. All others should come at 10.00 a.m.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The instruction must say: (1) participating students come at 9.00 with a parent; (2) everyone else comes by 10.00.

- (A) tells *all* students to come at 9.00 and parents at 10.00, so it ignores the accompanying parent.
- (B) **correct:** it covers both groups and both times.
- (C) bans non-participating students' parents, which the passage does not say.
- (D) "before 9.00" contradicts "an hour earlier" (exactly 9.00).

**Key idea:** the best instruction states every group's time and condition exactly as given, and adds nothing.

</details>
