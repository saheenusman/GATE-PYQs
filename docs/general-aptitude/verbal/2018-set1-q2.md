# 2018 CS Q2: The word that contrasts with 'organized'

## Question

"A _________ investigation can sometimes yield new facts, but typically organized ones are more successful."

The word that best fills the blank in the above sentence is

- (A) meandering
- (B) timely
- (C) consistent
- (D) systematic

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Look at the contrast word "but".** The sentence says the blank kind of investigation *sometimes* works, but *organized* ones work better. So the blank must describe an investigation that is **not** organized.

**Meandering** means wandering without a fixed plan. It is the opposite of organized, so it fits.

**Why the others fail:**
- **D** (systematic) means organized. "But organized ones" would then make no contrast.
- **B** (timely) and **C** (consistent) say nothing about being organized, so "but typically organized ones" has nothing to contrast with.

**Key idea:** a contrast word ("but", "however") tells you the blank is the opposite of what follows it.

</details>
