# 2020 CS Q3: Analogy Cook : Cook :: Fly : ___

## Question

Select the word that fits the analogy:

Cook : Cook :: Fly : ______

- (A) Flyer
- (B) Flying
- (C) Flew
- (D) Flighter

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**The pattern.** The first pair is *verb : person who does it*. To cook is the verb, and the person who does the cooking is a *cook*. (The two words happen to be spelled the same.)

**Apply it to "fly".** The person who flies is a **flyer** (also spelled *flier*).

**Why the others fail:**

- **(B) Flying:** the gerund (the activity), not the person doing it.
- **(C) Flew:** the past tense of the verb.
- **(D) Flighter:** not an English word.

**Note.** The first pair is a slightly unusual model, because the verb and the doer share one spelling. Even so, only (A) is a real word naming the doer, which is why it matches the official key.

**Key idea:** in an analogy, name the relationship first (here, verb to doer) and then apply it to the second pair.

</details>
