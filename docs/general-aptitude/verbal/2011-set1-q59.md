# 2011 CS Q59: Word completing a list of weak styles

## Question

Choose the most appropriate word from the options given below to complete the following sentence.

**If you are trying to make a strong impression on your audience, you cannot do so by being understated, tentative or ______.**

- (A) hyperbolic
- (B) restrained
- (C) argumentative
- (D) indifferent

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The blank must continue the list *understated, tentative*: styles of delivery that are mild and hold back, which do **not** make a strong impression.

- (B) *restrained* (held back, reserved) fits the list.
- (A) *hyperbolic* means exaggerated, which would help make a strong impression.
- (C) *argumentative* is combative, not mild.
- (D) *indifferent* describes an attitude of not caring rather than a style of delivery like the other two words.

**Key idea:** the missing word must belong to the same family as 'understated' and 'tentative': mild and held back.

</details>
