# 2013 CS Q60: Grammatically incorrect sentence

## Question

Choose the grammatically **INCORRECT** sentence:

- (A) He is of Asian origin.
- (B) They belonged to Africa.
- (C) She is an European.
- (D) They migrated from India to Australia.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**(C) is incorrect.** The article depends on the *sound*, not the spelling. "European" begins with the consonant sound /j/ (as in "yoo"), so it takes **a**: "She is a European."

**The others are correct:**

- (A) "of Asian origin" is idiomatic.
- (B) "belonged to Africa" is grammatical.
- (D) "migrated from India to Australia" is grammatical.

**Key idea:** use *a* before consonant sounds (a European, a university) and *an* before vowel sounds.

</details>
