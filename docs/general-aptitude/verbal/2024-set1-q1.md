# 2024 CS1 Q1: Analogy of increasing intensity (diet, fast, ...)

## Question

If '→' denotes increasing order of intensity, then the meaning of the words [dry → arid → parched] is analogous to [diet → fast → ________ ].

Which one of the given options is appropriate to fill the blank?

- (A) starve
- (B) reject
- (C) feast
- (D) deny

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A (starve)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- *dry → arid → parched*: each word is a stronger form of "lacking water".
- *diet → fast → ?*: each step is a stronger form of "eating less". The next, most intense step is **starve**.

**Why the others fail:** *reject* and *deny* are about refusing, not about eating less. *Feast* is the opposite direction (eating a lot).

**Key idea:** in an intensity analogy, keep the same theme and move one step further in the same direction.

</details>
