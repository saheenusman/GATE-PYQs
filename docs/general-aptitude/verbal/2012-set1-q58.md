# 2012 CS Q58: Closest in meaning to mitigate

## Question

Which one of the following options is the closest in meaning to the word given below?

**Mitigate**

- (A) Diminish
- (B) Divulge
- (C) Dedicate
- (D) Denote

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Mitigate* means to make less severe or less intense. **Diminish** (to make smaller or lessen) is the closest: (A).

- (B) Divulge: to reveal.
- (C) Dedicate: to devote.
- (D) Denote: to indicate or stand for.

**Key idea:** mitigate = lessen the severity.

</details>
