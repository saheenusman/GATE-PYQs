# 2019 CS2 Q5: Analogy: court and judge, ___ and teacher

## Question

A court is to a judge as _____________ is to a teacher.

- (A) a student
- (B) a punishment
- (C) a syllabus
- (D) a school

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The relation is **institution and the person who presides or works in it**: a judge works in a court, a teacher works in a school.

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) *a student*: the person a teacher teaches, not the place a teacher works.
- (B) *a punishment*: the output of a judge's decision. The analogue would be "a grade".
- (C) *a syllabus*: the material taught, not the institution.

**Key idea:** pin down the exact relation in the first pair, then test each option against it.

</details>
