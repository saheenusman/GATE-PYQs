# 2010 CS Q60: Analogy: Unemployed : Worker

## Question

The question below consists of a pair of related words followed by four pairs of words. Select the pair that best expresses the relation in the original pair.

Unemployed : Worker

- (A) fallow : land
- (B) unaware : sleeper
- (C) wit : jester
- (D) renovated : house

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

An *unemployed* worker is one who is not being put to use. In the same way, *fallow* land is land that is left unused (not being cultivated): (A).

- (B) A sleeper is unaware by the nature of sleeping; being unaware is not a state of lying idle.
- (C) A jester is characterised by wit; that is a quality, not a state of non-use.
- (D) *Renovated* describes something that has been improved; it is not a state of being unused.

**Judgement note:** analogy items rest on the relation I read into the pair. I read it as "a thing that is available but not in use"; this matches the official key.

**Key idea:** both pairs describe something capable of work that is currently idle.

</details>
