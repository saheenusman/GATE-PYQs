# 2018 CS Q1: Choosing their, they're and there

## Question

"From where are they bringing their books? ________ bringing _______ books from _____."

The words that best fill the blanks in the above sentence are

- (A) Their, they're, there
- (B) They're, their, there
- (C) There, their, they're
- (D) They're, there, there

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Fill each blank by the job it does in the sentence.**

1. Blank 1 needs a subject and the verb "are", so it is **they're** (they are): "They're bringing".
2. Blank 2 shows possession of the books, so it is **their**.
3. Blank 3 names a place, so it is **there**.

The full sentence reads: "They're bringing their books from there."

**Why the others fail:**
- **A** starts with "Their bringing", which has no verb "are".
- **C** starts with "There bringing", and ends with "they're" where a place is needed.
- **D** puts "there" in blank 2: "there books" is not possessive.

**Key idea:** *their* shows possession, *they're* is "they are", *there* names a place.

</details>
