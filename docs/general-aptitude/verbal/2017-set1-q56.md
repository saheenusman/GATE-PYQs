# 2017 CS1 Q56: Correct tense for a wish after returning from a voyage

## Question

After Rajendra Chola returned from his voyage to Indonesia, he ______ to visit the temple in Thanjavur.

- (A) was wishing
- (B) is wishing
- (C) wished
- (D) had wished

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Read the timeline.** The first clause, *returned from his voyage*, is in the simple past. The wish to visit the temple comes **after** the return, so it is another event in the same past sequence: simple past, **wished**.

**Why the others fail.**

- (A) *was wishing* is a continuous form. It suggests a wish that was in progress for some stretch of time, not a single event that followed the return.
- (B) *is wishing* is present tense, which clashes with the past narrative.
- (D) *had wished* is the past perfect: it would place the wish *before* the return, but the sentence says it came after.

So **(C)**.

**Status note:** no program applies; my reading matches the official key.

**Key idea:** events narrated in order, one after another, take the simple past; use the past perfect only for something earlier than the main past event.

</details>
