# 2016 CS2 Q58: Odd one out among mock, deride, praise and jeer

## Question

(GA Set-6, Q3) Find the odd one in the following group of words.

mock, deride, praise, jeer

- (A) mock
- (B) deride
- (C) praise
- (D) jeer

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Mock*, *deride* and *jeer* all mean to ridicule someone. *Praise* means to express approval, the opposite sense. The odd one is **praise**, (C).

**Key idea:** three words mean ridicule; praise means approval.

</details>
