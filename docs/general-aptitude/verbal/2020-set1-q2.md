# 2020 CS Q2: Excellent knowledge but classroom performance was ___

## Question

His knowledge of the subject was excellent but his classroom performance was ______.

- (A) extremely poor
- (B) good
- (C) desirable
- (D) praiseworthy

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The word **but** signals a contrast. The first half is positive ("excellent"), so the blank must be negative.

- **(A) extremely poor:** the only negative option, so it gives the contrast.
- **(B) good, (C) desirable, (D) praiseworthy:** all positive. With "but" they would repeat the first half instead of contrasting with it.

**Key idea:** read the connector (*but*, *although*, *and*) first; it tells you whether the blank continues or reverses the sentence.

</details>
