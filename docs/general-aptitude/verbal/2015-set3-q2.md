# 2015 CS3 Q2: Analogy: Children : Pediatrician

## Question

Select the pair that best expresses a relationship similar to that expressed in the pair:

Children : Pediatrician

- (A) Adult : Orthopaedist
- (B) Females : Gynaecologist
- (C) Kidney : Nephrologist
- (D) Skin : Dermatologist

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

In the stem, a **group of people** is paired with the doctor who specialises in treating that group.

- (B) *Females : Gynaecologist* has the same pattern: a group of people and their specialist. Correct.
- (C) *Kidney : Nephrologist* and (D) *Skin : Dermatologist* pair an **organ** with a specialist, not a group of people.
- (A) An orthopaedist treats bones and joints, not adults as a group.

**Key idea:** match the *type* of relationship (people group to specialist), not just "doctor".

</details>
