# 2025 CS1 Q1: Articles in a sentence about Ravi

## Question

Ravi had ______ younger brother who taught at ______ university. He was widely regarded as ______ honorable man.

Select the option with the correct sequence of articles to fill in the blanks.

- (A) a; a; an
- (B) the; an; a
- (C) a; an; a
- (D) an; an; a

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Choose **a** or **an** by the **sound** that starts the next word, not by its spelling.

| Blank | Next word | Starts with | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | younger | consonant sound | **a** |
| 2 | university | /yu/, a consonant sound | **a** |
| 3 | honorable | silent *h*, vowel sound /o/ | **an** |

So the sequence is *a; a; an*.

| Option | Why wrong |
|---|---|
| B | "the younger brother" is not introduced earlier, and "an university" is wrong |
| C | "an university" is wrong, and "a honorable" is wrong |
| D | "an younger" is wrong |

**Key idea:** *university* takes "a" (consonant sound /y/) and *honorable* takes "an" (silent h).

</details>
