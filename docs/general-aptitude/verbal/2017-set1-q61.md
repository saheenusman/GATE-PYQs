# 2017 CS1 Q61: What the author's remark on nationalist history implies

## Question

"The hold of the nationalist imagination on our colonial past is such that anything inadequately or improperly nationalist is just not history."

Which of the following statements best reflects the author's opinion?

- (A) Nationalists are highly imaginative.
- (B) History is viewed through the filter of nationalism.
- (C) Our colonial past never happened.
- (D) Nationalism has to be both adequately and properly imagined.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**What the sentence says.** The nationalist imagination has such a strong *hold* on the colonial past that an account that is not sufficiently nationalist is not accepted as history at all. The author is describing how the past is seen: only through a nationalist lens.

**Match the options.**

- **(B) History is viewed through the filter of nationalism.** This restates the point. Correct.
- (A) *Highly imaginative* shifts the focus to a trait of nationalists. The author comments on how history is judged, not on their creativity.
- (C) The author does not say the past did not happen, only that non-nationalist accounts get dismissed as "not history".
- (D) This takes the words "adequately and properly" and turns them into a demand on nationalism. In the sentence they describe the *non-nationalist* accounts that are rejected, so this reverses the meaning.

So **(B)**.

**Status note:** no program applies; my reading matches the official key.

**Key idea:** in an inference question, choose the option that restates the claim, not one that borrows its words.

</details>
