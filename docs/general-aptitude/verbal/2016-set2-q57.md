# 2016 CS2 Q57: Phrase closest in meaning to cope with

## Question

(GA Set-6, Q2) Nobody knows how the Indian cricket team is going to <u>cope with</u> the difficult and seamer-friendly wickets in Australia.

Choose the option which is closest in meaning to the underlined phrase in the above sentence.

- (A) put up with
- (B) put in with
- (C) put down to
- (D) put up against

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The underlined phrase is *cope with* (underlined in the original). To cope with something difficult is to deal with it or endure it: **put up with**, (A), is the closest of the four options.

- (B) *put in with* means to join or contribute alongside someone.
- (C) *put down to* means to attribute something to a cause.
- (D) *put up against* means to be matched against an opponent or to face someone.

(D) is the nearest distractor, but it means "pitted against", while the sentence is about managing conditions. The official key is (A).

**Key idea:** *cope with* means deal with or endure, closest to *put up with*.

</details>
