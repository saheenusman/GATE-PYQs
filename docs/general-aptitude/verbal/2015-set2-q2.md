# 2015 CS2 Q2: Using 'personnel' correctly

## Question

Choose the statement where underlined word is used correctly.

- (A) The industrialist had a <u>personnel</u> jet.
- (B) I write my experience in my <u>personnel</u> diary.
- (C) All <u>personnel</u> are being given the day off.
- (D) Being religious is a <u>personnel</u> aspect.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Personnel* is a noun meaning the staff or employees of an organisation. *Personal* is the adjective meaning "private" or "one's own".

- (A), (B), (D) need the adjective *personal* (a personal jet, a personal diary, a personal aspect).
- (C) uses *personnel* as a noun for staff. Correct.

**Key idea:** *personnel* (noun, staff) versus *personal* (adjective, private).

</details>
