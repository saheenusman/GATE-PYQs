# 2021 CS2 Q1: Fill in the comparison: play the keyboard ___ her sister

## Question

Gauri said that she can play the keyboard ______ her sister.

- (A) as well as
- (B) as better as
- (C) as nicest as
- (D) as worse as

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The blank needs a comparison of the form "as ... as". The adjective or adverb in the middle must be in its plain (positive) form.

- (A) "as well as" is the standard equal-comparison. **Correct.**
- (B) "as better as" is wrong: the comparative "better" cannot sit between "as ... as".
- (C) "nicest" is a superlative and does not suit "play ... as".
- (D) "worse" is a comparative, so "as worse as" is wrong for the same reason as (B).

**Key idea:** equal comparison is "as + positive form + as".

</details>
