# 2021 CS1 Q5: Analogy: surgery and writer

## Question

______ is to surgery as writer is to ______.

Which one of the following options maintains a similar logical relation in the above sentence?

- (A) Plan, outline
- (B) Hospital, library
- (C) Doctor, book
- (D) Medicine, grammar

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A **writer** produces a **book**, and a **doctor** performs **surgery**. The relation is "person : the work they do".

- (A) A plan is not a person.
- (B) A hospital is a place, not a person.
- (C) Doctor is to surgery as writer is to book. **Correct.**
- (D) Medicine is not a person.

**Key idea:** keep the same type of relation on both sides (agent and activity or product).

</details>
