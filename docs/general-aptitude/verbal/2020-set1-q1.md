# 2020 CS Q1: Fill the blanks: within six months, for the last three weeks

## Question

Raman is confident of speaking English ______ six months as he has been practising regularly ______ the last three weeks.

- (A) during, for
- (B) for, since
- (C) for, in
- (D) within, for

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Blank 1.** Raman is confident about a *deadline*: he expects to speak English fluently **within** six months, meaning before six months are over.

**Blank 2.** The practice started three weeks ago and is still going on. A duration that runs up to now takes **for**: "for the last three weeks".

**Why the others fail:**

- **(A) during, for:** "during six months" describes something happening throughout a period, not a goal to reach by its end.
- **(B) for, since:** *since* needs a starting point ("since Monday"), not a length of time like "the last three weeks".
- **(C) for, in:** "speaking English for six months" talks about speaking for a duration, which is not the intended meaning.

**Key idea:** *within* marks a deadline, *for* marks a duration, *since* marks a starting point.

</details>
