# 2014 CS1 Q56: Closest in meaning to 'cope with'

## Question

(GA Set-7, Q1) Which of the following options is the closest in meaning to the phrase underlined in the sentence below?

It is fascinating to see life forms <u>cope with</u> varied environmental conditions.

- (A) adopt to
- (B) adapt to
- (C) adept in
- (D) accept with

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

*Cope with* means to adjust successfully to a situation. Life forms **adapt to** conditions.

- (A) *adopt* means to take up or accept as one's own, and *adopt to* is not idiomatic.
- (C) *adept* is an adjective (skilled), not a verb.
- (D) *accept with* does not carry the sense of adjusting.

**Key idea:** adapt (adjust) vs adopt (take up) vs adept (skilled).

</details>
