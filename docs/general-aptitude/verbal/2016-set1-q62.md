# 2016 CS1 Q62: Logical inference from the statement about currency notes

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q7) Indian currency notes show the denomination indicated in at least seventeen languages. If this is not an indication of the nation's diversity, nothing else is.

Which of the following can be logically inferred from the above sentences?

- (A) India is a country of exactly seventeen languages.
- (B) Linguistic pluralism is the only indicator of a nation's diversity.
- (C) Indian currency notes have sufficient space for all the Indian languages.
- (D) Linguistic pluralism is strong evidence of India's diversity.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The passage says the many languages on the notes are a very strong indication of diversity ("if this is not... nothing else is"). The safe inference is that linguistic pluralism is **strong evidence** of diversity, (D).

- (A) says *exactly* seventeen; the passage says *at least* seventeen.
- (B) says it is the *only* indicator; the passage only says it is a very good one.
- (C) is about space on the notes, which the passage never discusses.

**Key idea:** the text supports 'strong evidence', not 'exactly' or 'only'.

</details>
