# 2021 CS1 Q3: Grammar and ambiguity in two sentences

## Question

Consider the following sentences:

(i) Everybody in the class is prepared for the exam.

(ii) Babu invited Danish to his home because he enjoys playing chess.

Which of the following is the CORRECT observation about the above two sentences?

- (A) (i) is grammatically correct and (ii) is unambiguous
- (B) (i) is grammatically incorrect and (ii) is unambiguous
- (C) (i) is grammatically correct and (ii) is ambiguous
- (D) (i) is grammatically incorrect and (ii) is ambiguous

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Sentence (i).** "Everybody" is singular and takes "is", so "Everybody ... is prepared" is correct.

**Sentence (ii).** "he" can refer to Babu or to Danish. Either of them may be the one who enjoys chess, so the sentence is ambiguous.

- (A) and (B) call (ii) unambiguous. Wrong.
- (B) and (D) call (i) incorrect. Wrong.
- (C) is the only option with (i) correct and (ii) ambiguous.

**Key idea:** a pronoun with two possible antecedents makes a sentence ambiguous.

</details>
