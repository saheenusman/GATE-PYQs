# 2019 CS2 Q2: Idiom for a business model centred on trust

## Question

The search engine's business model _____ around the fulcrum of trust.

- (A) revolves
- (B) plays
- (C) sinks
- (D) bursts

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A fulcrum is the pivot point of a lever. Something that turns about a pivot **revolves** around it, so "revolves around the fulcrum of trust" means the model is centred on trust.

**Why the others fail:**

- (B) *plays around*: means to fool about, not to depend on a pivot.
- (C) *sinks around*: not an idiom, and it contradicts a stable pivot.
- (D) *bursts around*: not an idiom either.

**Key idiom:** "revolves around" = has as its central point.

</details>
