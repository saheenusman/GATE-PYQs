# 2014 CS3 Q62: Inference from the paradigm-shift passage

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q7) By the beginning of the 20th century, several hypotheses were being proposed, suggesting a paradigm shift in our understanding of the universe. However, the clinching evidence was provided by experimental measurements of the position of a star which was directly behind our sun.

Which of the following inference(s) may be drawn from the above passage?

- (i) Our understanding of the universe changes based on the positions of stars
- (ii) Paradigm shifts usually occur at the beginning of centuries
- (iii) Stars are important objects in the universe
- (iv) Experimental evidence was important in confirming this paradigm shift

- (A) (i), (ii) and (iv)
- (B) (iii) only
- (C) (i) and (iv)
- (D) (iv) only

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Only what the passage states or directly implies can be inferred.

- (i) is a generalisation. The passage mentions one measurement of one star, not that understanding changes with star positions in general.
- (ii) "usually ... at the beginning of centuries" is unsupported: the passage mentions only one such case.
- (iii) the importance of stars is not discussed.
- (iv) **supported:** the "clinching evidence" came from experiment.

So only (iv): **D**.

**Key idea:** reject inferences that generalise from a single case or bring in outside claims.

</details>
