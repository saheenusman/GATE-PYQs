# 2013 CS Q59: Were you a bird, you ___ fly

## Question

Were you a bird, you ____________________ in the sky.

- (A) would fly
- (B) shall fly
- (C) should fly
- (D) shall have flown

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

"Were you a bird" is an **unreal (second) conditional**: the *if*-clause is in the past subjunctive (inverted here), so the main clause takes **would + base verb**: "you would fly in the sky".

**Why the others fail:**

- (B) *shall fly* is a simple future, which does not fit a hypothetical.
- (C) *should fly* expresses obligation or advice, not the result of a hypothetical.
- (D) *shall have flown* is a future perfect, wrong in tense and sense.

**Key idea:** If + past subjunctive takes *would* + verb in the result clause.

</details>
