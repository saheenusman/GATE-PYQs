# 2014 CS3 Q57: Fill the blanks: past unreal conditional

## Question

(GA Set-9, Q2) If she _______ how to calibrate the instrument, she _______ done the experiment.

- (A) knows, will have
- (B) knew, had
- (C) had known, could have
- (D) should have known, would have

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The second blank is followed by "done", so it needs a modal plus "have" (a perfect infinitive). That is the **third conditional**: *if* + past perfect, *would/could have* + past participle. Option (C) fits: "If she **had known** ... she **could have** done the experiment."

- (A) mixes the present "knows" with "will have done".
- (B) "knew ... had done" is not a valid conditional pair: "had" followed by "done" is a past perfect, not a conditional result.
- (D) "If she should have known" is not grammatical in the *if* clause.

**Key idea:** the past unreal conditional is *if* + past perfect, *would/could have* + past participle.

</details>
