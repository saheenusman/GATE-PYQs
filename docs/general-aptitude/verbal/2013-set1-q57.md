# 2013 CS Q57: Universalism : particularism analogy

## Question

Complete the sentence:

Universalism is to particularism as diffuseness is to ________________.

- (A) specificity
- (B) neutrality
- (C) generality
- (D) adaptation

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Universalism (rules that apply to all) is the **opposite** of particularism (rules depending on the specific case). So we need the opposite of *diffuseness* (spread out, general in scope). That is **specificity**.

**Why the others fail:**

- (B) neutrality is the opposite of affectivity, not diffuseness.
- (C) generality is close to diffuseness, a synonym, so it breaks the pattern.
- (D) adaptation has no link to the pair.

**Key idea:** the analogy pairs opposites: universalism/particularism and diffuseness/specificity.

</details>
