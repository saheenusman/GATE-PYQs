# 2023 CS Q1: Fill the blank: nearly missed

## Question

We reached the station late, and _______ missed the train.

- (A) near
- (B) nearly
- (C) utterly
- (D) mostly

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B (nearly)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Pick the word that modifies the verb *missed*.** "Nearly missed" means "came close to missing", which is the idiomatic way to describe a narrow miss.

**Why the others fail:**
- *near* is an adjective or preposition here, not an adverb that can modify a verb.
- *utterly* is an intensifier for strong states ("utterly ruined"); "utterly missed" is not idiomatic.
- *mostly* means "for the most part", which makes no sense for a single event.

**Key idea:** the blank needs an adverb that fits the verb's meaning.

</details>
