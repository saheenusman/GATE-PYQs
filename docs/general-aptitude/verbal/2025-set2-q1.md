# 2025 CS2 Q1: Fill in the blank: Rehman's ___ to contribute

## Question

Despite his initial hesitation, Rehman's _________ to contribute to the success of the project never wavered.

Select the most appropriate option to complete the above sentence.

- (A) ambivalence
- (B) satisfaction
- (C) resolve
- (D) revolve

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The blank needs a noun that can be followed by "to contribute" and that can "waver".

| Option | Fit |
|---|---|
| A, ambivalence | Means mixed feelings, which would suit "hesitation" but not "never wavered" |
| B, satisfaction | Does not take "to contribute" naturally |
| **C, resolve** | Firm determination: "Rehman's resolve to contribute never wavered" |
| D, revolve | A verb, and it makes no sense here |

**Key idea:** the contrast word *Despite* signals the blank is the opposite of hesitation, so firmness (*resolve*).

</details>
