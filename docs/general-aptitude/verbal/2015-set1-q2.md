# 2015 CS1 Q2: Closest meaning of 'enjoyed herself immensely'

## Question

Which of the following options is the closest in meaning to the sentence below?

She enjoyed herself immensely at the party.

- (A) She had a terrible time at the party
- (B) She had a horrible time at the party
- (C) She had a terrific time at the party
- (D) She had a terrifying time at the party

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

"Enjoyed herself immensely" means she had a very good time.

- (A), (B), (D): *terrible*, *horrible* and *terrifying* all describe a bad experience.
- (C) *terrific* here means excellent. Correct.

**Key idea:** *terrific* can mean "very good", while *terrible*, *horrible* and *terrifying* are negative.

</details>
