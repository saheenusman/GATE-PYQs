# 2015 CS3 Q1: Word that fits 'anything ___ to the requirements of the exam'

## Question

Extreme focus on syllabus and studying for tests has become such a dominant concern of Indian students that they close their minds to anything ______ to the requirements of the exam.

- (A) related
- (B) extraneous
- (C) outside
- (D) useful

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The students shut out everything that is **not part of** the exam, so the blank needs a word meaning "irrelevant".

- (B) *extraneous to* means "not belonging to". It fits both the meaning and the grammar. Correct.
- (A) *related* and (D) *useful* say the opposite: students would not close their minds to useful or related things in the way the sentence describes.
- (C) *outside* has the right sense, but "outside **to**" is not grammatical (it would need "outside of").

**Key idea:** *extraneous to* is the idiom for "not relevant to".

</details>
