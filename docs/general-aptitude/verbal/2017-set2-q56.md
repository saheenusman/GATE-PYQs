# 2017 CS2 Q56: Choose the pair of words that are not synonyms

## Question

Choose the option with words that are not synonyms.

- (A) aversion, dislike
- (B) luminous, radiant
- (C) plunder, loot
- (D) yielding, resistant

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Check each pair.**

- (A) *aversion* and *dislike* both mean a feeling of distaste. Synonyms.
- (B) *luminous* and *radiant* both mean giving off light, shining. Synonyms.
- (C) *plunder* and *loot* both mean to rob or steal by force. Synonyms.
- (D) *yielding* means giving way or submissive; *resistant* means opposing or not giving way. These are **opposites**.

The pair that is not synonyms is **(D)**.

**Status note:** vocabulary question (no program applies); the answer matches the official key.

**Key idea:** "not synonyms" asks for the odd pair out; here it is the antonym pair.

</details>
