# 2016 CS2 Q63: Inferences about hill-stations and lakes

## Question

(GA Set-6, Q8) All hill-stations have a lake. Ooty has two lakes.

Which of the statement(s) below is/are logically valid and can be inferred from the above sentences?

- (i) Ooty is not a hill-station.
- (ii) No hill-station can have more than one lake.

- (A) (i) only
- (B) (ii) only
- (C) both (i) and (ii)
- (D) neither (i) nor (ii)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

"All hill-stations have a lake" means every hill-station has **at least one** lake. It does not limit the number.

- **(i)** Ooty having two lakes is consistent with Ooty being a hill-station (it has at least one lake), so we cannot conclude it is not one.
- **(ii)** The first sentence says nothing about an upper limit, and Ooty (possibly a hill-station) has two lakes, so this does not follow.

Neither follows: **(D)**.

**Key idea:** 'have a lake' means at least one, so nothing rules out Ooty being a hill-station or hill-stations having two lakes.

</details>
