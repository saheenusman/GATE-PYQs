# 2010 CS Q62: Ordering four siblings by age from three clues

## Question

Hari (H), Gita (G), Irfan (I) and Saira (S) are siblings (i.e. brothers and sisters). All were born on 1st January. The age difference between any two successive siblings (that is born one after another) is less than $3$ years. Given the following facts:

- i. Hari's age + Gita's age $>$ Irfan's age + Saira's age
- ii. The age difference between Gita and Saira is $1$ year. However Gita is not the oldest and Saira is not the youngest.
- iii. There are no twins.

In what order were they born (oldest first)?

- (A) HSIG
- (B) SGHI
- (C) IGSH
- (D) IHSG

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

All were born on 1 January, so ages are whole numbers of years, all different (no twins), and neighbouring siblings differ by $1$ or $2$ years.

Check each option against the clues.

| Option | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| (A) HSIG | G and S have I between them, so their gap is at least $1 + 1 = 2$, not $1$ | fails ii |
| (B) SGHI | S is oldest, G one year younger (gap $1$). G not oldest, S not youngest. $H + G > I + S$ needs $H - I > S - G = 1$, so $H - I = 2$, which is allowed | **fits** |
| (C) IGSH | I is oldest and H youngest, so $H < S$ and $G < I$, hence $H + G < I + S$ | fails i |
| (D) IHSG | I oldest and G youngest, so $H < I$ and $G < S$, hence $H + G < I + S$ | fails i |

Only (B) works.

**Code check:** a Python search over all whole-number ages $1$ to $11$ listed every order meeting all clues: SGHI, HSGI, HGSI and HISG. Of the four options, only SGHI (B) is among them.

**Key idea:** turn each clue into an inequality on ages and test the options against them.

</details>
