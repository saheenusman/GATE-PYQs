# 2014 CS2 Q65: Clock time between 6 and 7 a.m. with an angle closest to 60 degrees

## Question

(GA Set-8, Q10) At what time between 6 a.m. and 7 a.m. will the minute hand and hour hand of a clock make an angle closest to $60^\circ$?

- (A) 6:22 a.m.
- (B) 6:27 a.m.
- (C) 6:38 a.m.
- (D) 6:45 a.m.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

At 6:$m$ the hour hand is at $180 + 0.5m$ degrees and the minute hand at $6m$ degrees. The angle between them is

$$|180 - 5.5m|$$

| Time | $\lvert 180 - 5.5m \rvert$ |
|---|---|
| 6:22 | 59° |
| 6:27 | 31.5° |
| 6:38 | 29° |
| 6:45 | 67.5° |

6:22 gives 59°, the closest to 60° (exact solution $m = 120/5.5 \approx 21.8$).

**Code check:** the angle computed for each option matches the table.

**Key idea:** the minute hand gains $5.5^\circ$ per minute on the hour hand.

</details>
