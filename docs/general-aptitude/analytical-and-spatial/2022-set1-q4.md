# 2022 CS Q4: Which two statements cannot both be true

## Question

Given below are four statements.

- Statement 1: All students are inquisitive.
- Statement 2: Some students are inquisitive.
- Statement 3: No student is inquisitive.
- Statement 4: Some students are not inquisitive.

From the given four statements, find the two statements that CANNOT BE TRUE simultaneously, assuming that there is at least one student in the class.

- (A) Statement 1 and Statement 3
- (B) Statement 1 and Statement 2
- (C) Statement 2 and Statement 4
- (D) Statement 3 and Statement 4

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A (Statement 1 and Statement 3)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Test each pair for a possible situation.** Since there is at least one student, "all" and "none" cannot both hold.

- (A) 1 says every student is inquisitive; 3 says none is. With at least one student, no class satisfies both. **Cannot be true together.**
- (B) 1 implies 2 (there is at least one student). Both can hold.
- (C) A class with one inquisitive and one non-inquisitive student satisfies both.
- (D) 3 implies 4 (at least one student, and that student is not inquisitive). Both can hold.

**Key idea:** the "at least one student" assumption makes universal statements imply the matching particular ones, so only the two universals clash.

</details>
