# 2017 CS2 Q58: Which building is in the middle of five buildings in a row

## Question

There are five buildings called V, W, X, Y and Z in a row (not necessarily in that order). V is to the West of W. Z is to the East of X and the West of V. W is to the West of Y. Which is the building in the middle?

- (A) V
- (B) W
- (C) X
- (D) Y

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Turn each statement into an order, West to East.**

- V is West of W: $V < W$.
- Z is East of X and West of V: $X < Z < V$.
- W is West of Y: $W < Y$.

**Chain them:** $X < Z < V < W < Y$. All five buildings are in one chain, so the order is fixed:

$$X,\ Z,\ V,\ W,\ Y.$$

The middle (3rd) building is **V**: **(A)**.

**Code check:** a search over all 120 orderings of the five buildings found exactly one that satisfies the three conditions, `XZVWY`, with V in the middle.

**Key idea:** combine the pairwise "West of" facts into a single chain.

</details>
