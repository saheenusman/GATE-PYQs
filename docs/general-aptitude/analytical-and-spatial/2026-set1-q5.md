# 2026 CS1 Q5: Which statement is not necessarily true

## Question

'When the teacher is in the room, all students stand silently.'

If the above statement is true, which one of the following statements is **not necessarily** true?

- (A) If any student is not standing silently, then the teacher is not in the room.
- (B) When the teacher is in the room, all students are silent.
- (C) If all students are standing, then the teacher is in the room.
- (D) When the teacher is in the room, all students are standing.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Write it as logic.** Let $T$ = "teacher is in the room" and $W$ = "all students stand silently" (standing **and** silent). The given statement is $T \Rightarrow W$.

- **(A)** "Not $W$ implies not $T$" is the contrapositive. Always true.
- **(B)** $T \Rightarrow$ silent. This follows from $W$ (silent is part of $W$). True.
- **(D)** $T \Rightarrow$ standing. This follows from $W$ (standing is part of $W$). True.
- **(C)** "All standing implies $T$." This reverses the arrow (the converse) and also drops "silently". Students can stand when the teacher is away. **Not necessarily true.**

**Key idea:** $P \Rightarrow Q$ gives you the contrapositive $\lnot Q \Rightarrow \lnot P$, never the converse $Q \Rightarrow P$.

</details>
