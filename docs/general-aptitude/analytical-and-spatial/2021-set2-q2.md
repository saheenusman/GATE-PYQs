# 2021 CS2 Q2: Folding a transparent sheet along a dotted line

## Question

A transparent square sheet shown above is folded along the dotted line. The folded sheet will look like ______.

![A square transparent sheet with a vertical dotted line through the middle. On the left of the line a curve rises from the lower left edge to a peak on the line, with two short vertical ticks beside it. On the right of the line the curve falls from the peak to the right edge, with two short horizontal ticks beside it.](../../assets/2021/set2-q2-a.png)

The four options are shown below. Each one is a narrow rectangle (the folded sheet) with a dashed outline on its left.

![Four folded-sheet options. (A) two curves meeting at a point on the left edge and opening to the right, with ticks outside the curves. (B) a leaf shape from the left edge to the lower right corner, with the ticks inside forming a stepped pattern. (C) a leaf shape with two parallel horizontal ticks inside. (D) a leaf shape with a zigzag tick inside.](../../assets/2021/set2-q2-b.png)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The sheet is transparent, so after folding **both halves stay visible**: the folded half is mirrored across the dotted line and drawn on top of the other half.

- Both curves start at the same point on the fold line and end at the right edge of the folded sheet. One rises steeply first, the other starts flat, so together they enclose a **leaf shape**. This removes (A), where the curves open out instead.
- The ticks of both halves are now inside the leaf. The vertical ticks from one half and the horizontal ticks from the other half join up into a stepped pattern.

I checked this by mirroring the left half of the figure onto the right half in an image program and overlaying the two. The result is a leaf with two horizontal ticks hanging from the upper curve and two vertical ticks rising from the lower curve, forming steps. That is option (B). Option (C) has only horizontal ticks and (D) has a zigzag, which do not match.

**Key idea:** folding a transparent sheet overlays the mirror image of one half on the other half.

</details>
