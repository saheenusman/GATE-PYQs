# 2024 CS1 Q9: Perimeter after folding a sheet three times

## Question

A rectangular paper of $20\text{ cm} \times 8\text{ cm}$ is folded 3 times. Each fold is made along the line of symmetry, which is perpendicular to its long edge. The perimeter of the final folded sheet (in cm) is

- (A) 18
- (B) 24
- (C) 20
- (D) 21

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A (18)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Track the shape fold by fold.** Each fold halves the *current* long edge, so the long edge can change.

| Fold | Before | Long edge | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 × 8 | 20 | 10 × 8 |
| 2 | 10 × 8 | 10 | 5 × 8 |
| 3 | 5 × 8 | **8** | 5 × 4 |

After fold 2 the sheet is 5 × 8, so the long edge is now 8 cm. Fold 3 halves **that** edge.

Final sheet is $5 \times 4$, perimeter $= 2(5+4) = 18$ cm.

**Why the others fail:** 21 cm comes from always halving the original 20 cm edge ($2.5 \times 8$). The fold rule says the new long edge is used each time.

**Key idea:** re-identify the long edge after every fold.

</details>
