# 2015 CS1 Q7: Which courses of action follow from a falling water level

## Question

The given statement is followed by some courses of action. Assuming the statement to be true, decide the correct option.

Statement: There has been a significant drop in the water level in the lakes supplying water to the city.

Courses of action:

- (I) The water supply authority should impose a partial cut in supply to tackle the situation.
- (II) The government should appeal to all the residents through mass media for minimal use of water.
- (III) The government should ban the water supply in lower areas.

- (A) Statements I and II follow.
- (B) Statements I and III follow.
- (C) Statements II and III follow.
- (D) All statements follow.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A sensible course of action should be proportionate and aimed at the problem.

- I: a partial cut reduces consumption in a controlled way. Follows.
- II: asking residents to save water is a reasonable, mild step. Follows.
- III: banning supply to lower areas is extreme and unfair to a section of residents. Does not follow.

So I and II follow: option (A). (B), (C) and (D) all include III.

**Key idea:** accept moderate, relevant steps and reject drastic, discriminatory ones.

</details>
