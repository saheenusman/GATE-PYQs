# 2016 CS1 Q60: Surface area of a cube after removing corner blocks

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q5) A cube is built using 64 cubic blocks of side one unit. After it is built, one cubic block is removed from every corner of the cube. The resulting surface area of the body (in square units) after the removal is __________.

- (A) 56
- (B) 64
- (C) 72
- (D) 96

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

64 blocks make a $4 \times 4 \times 4$ cube, so the original surface area is $6 \times 4^2 = 96$.

**Removing one corner block.** A corner block shows 3 faces on the outside. Taking it out removes those 3 unit faces, but exposes 3 new faces that were touching its neighbours. The net change is $0$.

There are 8 corners, so the area stays $96$, **(D)**.

**Why the others fail.** (A) $56$, (B) $64$, (C) $72$: each subtracts the removed faces without adding the newly exposed ones.

**Code check:** building the solid on a grid (56 blocks) and counting exposed faces gave $96$.

**Key idea:** a corner block hides 3 faces and exposes 3, so the area stays $6 \times 16 = 96$.

</details>
