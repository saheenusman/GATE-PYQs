# 2021 CS1 Q4: Unfolding a punched, folded paper

## Question

A circular sheet of paper is folded along the lines in the directions shown. The paper, after being punched in the final folded state as shown and unfolded in the reverse order of folding, will look like ______.

![A circle folded in half along a vertical line, then in half again along a horizontal line, giving a quarter circle. In the folded state a small rectangle is punched on the vertical fold edge, a smaller one on the horizontal fold edge, and a quarter circle at the corner. Four unfolded options (A) to (D) follow, each with a central circle and four rectangular slots.](../../assets/2021/set1-q4.png)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Unfolding reflects each punch across both fold lines. The corner of the folded sheet is the centre of the circle.

- The notch on the **vertical** fold edge is half of a wide, flat slot (about twice as wide as it is tall). Reflecting across the vertical line completes it, and reflecting across the horizontal line adds a copy on the other side. This gives two **wide horizontal slots**, one above and one below the centre.
- The notch on the **horizontal** fold edge is half of a narrow, tall slot. The same reflections give two **narrow vertical slots**, one left and one right of the centre.
- The quarter circle at the corner becomes a full circle at the centre.

Option (A) is the only one with wide horizontal slots at the top and bottom, narrow vertical slots at the sides, and a central circle. In (B) the sides are wide and flat (wrong shape). (C) and (D) have tall slots at the sides and small slots at the top and bottom (the swapped orientation).

**Key idea:** each fold line acts as a mirror when the paper is unfolded.

</details>
