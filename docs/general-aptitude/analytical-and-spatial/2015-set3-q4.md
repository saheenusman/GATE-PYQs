# 2015 CS3 Q4: Letter code: ROAD becomes URDG

## Question

If ROAD is written as URDG, then SWAN should be written as:

- (A) VXDQ
- (B) VZDQ
- (C) VZDP
- (D) UXDQ

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Compare the letters of ROAD and URDG:

| R | O | A | D |
|---|---|---|---|
| U | R | D | G |

Every letter moves **3 places forward** ($R \to U$, $O \to R$, $A \to D$, $D \to G$).

Apply it to SWAN: $S \to V$, $W \to Z$, $A \to D$, $N \to Q$, which gives **VZDQ**. Option (B).

- (A) has X for W (+1 instead of +3).
- (C) ends in P (+2 for N).
- (D) starts with U (+2 for S).

**Code check:** a shift of 3 turns ROAD into URDG and SWAN into VZDQ.

**Key idea:** find the constant shift from the given word, then apply it letter by letter.

</details>
