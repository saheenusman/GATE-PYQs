# 2017 CS1 Q65: Villages submerged when a flood reaches 525 m on a contour map

## Question

A contour line joins locations having the same height above the mean sea level. The following is a contour plot of a geographical region. Contour lines are shown at 25 m intervals in this plot. If in a flood, the water level rises to 525 m, which of the villages P, Q, R, S, T get submerged?

![Contour map with lines every 25 m and five villages. A hill on the left has nested closed contours labelled 500 and 550, with village P inside the innermost loop. A second hill on the right has nested contours with a 550 label, village Q inside the loop that surrounds the 550 line, and village T just outside it near a line labelled 500. Village R is in the saddle between the two hills, below a line labelled 450 at the top left. Village S lies at the edge of a small closed contour labelled 450 at the bottom right, a depression.](../../assets/2017/set1-q65.png)

- (A) P, Q
- (B) P, Q, T
- (C) R, S, T
- (D) Q, R, S

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Rule.** Water at 525 m covers every point whose height is **below 525 m**. A point enclosed by contours that rise to 525 m or more stays dry.

**Read the map.**

- **P** is inside the nested loops of the left hill, the innermost one above the 550 m line. P is higher than 550 m, so it stays **dry**.
- **Q** sits inside the loop that encloses the 550 m line of the right hill, which is at least 525 m. Q is **dry**.
- **R** is in the saddle between the two hills, outside both hills' outer (500 m) contours. It is below 500 m, so it is **submerged**.
- **T** is outside the loop around Q and next to the 500 m line, so it is lower than 525 m. **Submerged**.
- **S** is at the edge of a small closed contour whose label is 450. Heights fall toward the inside, so S is a low basin well below 525 m. **Submerged**.

Submerged: R, S, T, which is **(C)**.

**Why the others fail.** (A), (B) and (D) each include P or Q, which are on high ground above 525 m.

**Key idea:** on a contour map, a point is above a given level only if it lies inside a closed contour of that level or higher.

</details>
