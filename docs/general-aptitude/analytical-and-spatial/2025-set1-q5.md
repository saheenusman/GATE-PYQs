# 2025 CS1 Q5: Directions on a campus map

## Question

According to the map shown in the figure, which one of the following statements is correct?

Note: The figure shown is representative.

![Campus map. North is up. A vertical road, 1st Main Road, has Library above Physics Lab on its left, and Canteen above Hospital on its right. A horizontal road, 5th Cross Road, crosses it. Below that road, Hostels above Classrooms are on the left, and Chemistry Lab is on the right.](../../assets/2025/set1-q5.png)

- (A) The library is located to the northwest of the canteen.
- (B) The hospital is located to the east of the chemistry lab.
- (C) The chemistry lab is to the southeast of physics lab.
- (D) The classrooms and canteen are next to each other.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Take north as up and east as right. Positions from the map:

| Place | Side of 1st Main Road | Side of 5th Cross Road |
|---|---|---|
| Library, Physics Lab | west (left) | north (above) |
| Canteen, Hospital | east (right) | north (above) |
| Hostels, Classrooms | west (left) | south (below) |
| Chemistry Lab | east (right) | south (below) |

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A | The library and the canteen are in the same row, so the library is due **west** of the canteen, not northwest |
| B | The hospital is directly **north** of the chemistry lab, not east |
| **C** | The chemistry lab is east and south of the physics lab: **southeast** |
| D | The classrooms (southwest) and the canteen (northeast) are in opposite corners |

**Key idea:** locate each place as a (west/east, north/south) pair, then compare.

</details>
