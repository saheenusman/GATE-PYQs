# 2015 CS2 Q10: Distance between two branches from compass directions

## Question

Four branches of a company are located at M, N, O, and P. M is north of N at a distance of 4 km; P is south of O at a distance of 2 km; N is southeast of O by 1 km. What is the distance between M and P in km?

- (A) 5.34
- (B) 6.74
- (C) 28.5
- (D) 45.49

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Put $O$ at the origin, with north as $+y$.

- $P$ is 2 km south of $O$: $P = (0, -2)$.
- $N$ is 1 km south-east of $O$: $N = \left(\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}, -\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right) \approx (0.707, -0.707)$.
- $M$ is 4 km north of $N$: $M \approx (0.707,\ 3.293)$.

$$MP = \sqrt{0.707^2 + (3.293 + 2)^2} = \sqrt{0.5 + 28.01} \approx 5.34 \text{ km}$$

Option (A). (C) 28.5 is the *squared* distance, i.e. the value before taking the square root.

**Key idea:** use coordinates; "south-east by 1 km" splits into equal east and south parts of $1/\sqrt2$.

</details>
