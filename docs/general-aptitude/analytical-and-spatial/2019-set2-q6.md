# 2019 CS2 Q6: Who committed the crime: P, Q, R or S

## Question

The police arrested four criminals: P, Q, R and S. The criminals knew each other. They made the following statements:

- P says "Q committed the crime."
- Q says "S committed the crime."
- R says "I did not do it."
- S says "What Q said about me is false."

Assume only one of the arrested four committed the crime and only one of the statements made above is true. Who committed the crime?

- (A) P
- (B) R
- (C) S
- (D) Q

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Read S's statement as "S did not do it" (Q said S did it, and S says that is false).

Try each culprit and count the true statements:

| Culprit | P | Q | R | S | True |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P | F | F | T | T | 2 |
| Q | T | F | T | T | 3 |
| **R** | F | F | F | T | **1** |
| S | F | T | T | F | 2 |

Only **R** as the culprit leaves exactly one true statement (S's).

**Code check:** a brute-force run over the four cases gives R as the only culprit.

**Key idea:** with a fixed number of true statements, test each suspect and count.

</details>
