# 2024 CS2 Q9: Cuts needed to split a cube into 8 equal pieces

## Question

A cube is to be cut into 8 pieces of equal size and shape. Here, each cut should be straight and it should not stop till it reaches the other end of the cube.

The minimum number of such cuts required is

- (A) 3
- (B) 4
- (C) 7
- (D) 8

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A (3)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Make **one cut parallel to each pair of opposite faces**, each through the middle.

- Cut 1 halves the cube into 2 pieces.
- Cut 2 (perpendicular to the first) gives 4 pieces.
- Cut 3 (perpendicular to both) gives 8 equal small cubes.

Each full cut can at most double the number of pieces ($2^k$), so 8 pieces need at least $k = 3$ cuts. And 3 is enough.

**Key idea:** $k$ straight cuts can give at most $2^k$ pieces when pieces are not rearranged.

</details>
