# 2016 CS1 Q63: Inferences from four statements about poker players

## Question

(GA Set-5, Q8) Consider the following statements relating to the level of poker play of four players P, Q, R and S.

- I. P always beats Q
- II. R always beats S
- III. S loses to P only sometimes
- IV. R always loses to Q

Which of the following can be logically inferred from the above statements?

- (i) P is likely to beat all the three other players
- (ii) S is the absolute worst player in the set

- (A) (i) only
- (B) (ii) only
- (C) (i) and (ii)
- (D) neither (i) nor (ii)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Write the facts as arrows ($X \to Y$ means $X$ always beats $Y$): $P \to Q$, $R \to S$, $Q \to R$.

**(i) P is likely to beat all three.** We know $P$ beats $Q$ always. Against $S$, statement III says $S$ loses to $P$ only *sometimes*, so $S$ also beats $P$ at times. Nothing is said about $P$ against $R$ (beating $Q$, who beats $R$, does not force beating $R$). So (i) cannot be inferred.

**(ii) S is the absolute worst.** $S$ loses to $R$ always, and to $P$ sometimes. Nothing is said about $S$ against $Q$, and $S$ sometimes beats $P$, so (ii) cannot be inferred either.

Neither can be inferred: **(D)**.

**Key idea:** only the listed pairs are certain; 'beats' is not assumed transitive.

</details>
