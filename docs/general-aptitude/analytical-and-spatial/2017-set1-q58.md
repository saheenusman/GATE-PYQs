# 2017 CS1 Q58: Who sits opposite whom around a square table

## Question

Rahul, Murali, Srinivas and Arul are seated around a square table. Rahul is sitting to the left of Murali. Srinivas is sitting to the right of Arul. Which of the following pairs are seated opposite each other?

- (A) Rahul and Murali
- (B) Srinivas and Arul
- (C) Srinivas and Murali
- (D) Srinivas and Rahul

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Set up.** Four people sit one on each side of the square, all facing the centre. Place Murali on the south side facing north.

1. Rahul is to Murali's **left**. Facing north, Murali's left is the west side, so **Rahul is west**.
2. Two seats are left, north and east, for Srinivas and Arul. Srinivas is to Arul's **right**.
   - If Arul were north (facing south), his right would be west, which is Rahul. Not Srinivas. Rejected.
   - If Arul is east (facing west), his right is north. So **Arul is east and Srinivas is north**.

| Seat | Person |
|---|---|
| South | Murali |
| West | Rahul |
| North | Srinivas |
| East | Arul |

Opposite pairs: Murali and Srinivas, Rahul and Arul. So **(C)**.

**Why the others fail.** Rahul and Murali (A) and Srinivas and Arul (B) are neighbours, and Srinivas and Rahul (D) are neighbours.

**Code check:** enumerating all 24 seatings with "left" read either way (clockwise or anticlockwise) kept only seatings where Murali and Srinivas are opposite (and Rahul and Arul), so the answer does not depend on the convention.

**Key idea:** "left" and "right" are from each person's own view, facing the centre; fix one person and place the others one by one.

</details>
