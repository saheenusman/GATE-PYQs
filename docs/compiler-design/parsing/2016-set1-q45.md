# 2016 CS1 Q45: Value of an expression under custom operator precedence and associativity

## Question

The attributes of three arithmetic operators in some programming language are given below.

| Operator | Precedence | Associativity | Arity |
|---|---|---|---|
| $+$ | High | Left | Binary |
| $-$ | Medium | Right | Binary |
| $*$ | Low | Left | Binary |

The value of the expression $2 - 5 + 1 - 7 * 3$ in this language is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**9**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Evaluate from the highest precedence down.

**1. $+$ binds tightest:** $5 + 1 = 6$, so the expression is $2 - 6 - 7 * 3$.

**2. $-$ next, right associative.** The two $-$ operators group from the right:

$$2 - (6 - 7) = 2 - (-1) = 3$$

**3. $*$ is lowest,** so it is applied last, to the left part $2 - 6 - 7$ (which is $3$ from step 2) and the right part $3$:

$$3 \times 3 = 9$$

The full grouping is $(2 - ((5+1) - 7)) * 3 = 9$.

**Code check:** evaluating `(2 - ((5+1) - 7)) * 3` gives $9$.

**Key idea:** apply precedence first ($+$, then $-$, then $*$), and break the $-$ tie right-to-left.

</details>
