# 2012 CS Q53: Filling the missing entries of the LL(1) table

## Question

**Statement for Linked Answer Questions 52 and 53** (grammar and partial table as in Q52).

$$S \to aAbB \mid bAaB \mid \varepsilon \qquad A \to S \qquad B \to S$$

| | a | b | \$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | E1 | E2 | S → ε |
| **A** | A → S | A → S | error |
| **B** | B → S | B → S | E3 |

The appropriate entries for E1, E2, and E3 are

- (A) E1: $S \to aAbB,\ A \to S$; E2: $S \to bAaB,\ B \to S$; E3: $B \to S$
- (B) E1: $S \to aAbB,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E2: $S \to bAaB,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E3: $S \to \varepsilon$
- (C) E1: $S \to aAbB,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E2: $S \to bAaB,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E3: $B \to S$
- (D) E1: $A \to S,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E2: $B \to S,\ S \to \varepsilon$; E3: $B \to S$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Rules for the table:

- A production $X \to \alpha$ goes in row $X$, in the column of every terminal in $\text{FIRST}(\alpha)$.
- If $\varepsilon \in \text{FIRST}(\alpha)$, it also goes in the columns of $\text{FOLLOW}(X)$.

**Row S** ($\text{FOLLOW}(S) = \lbrace a, b, \$ \rbrace$ from Q52):

- $S \to aAbB$ has FIRST $= \lbrace a \rbrace$, so it goes in column $a$.
- $S \to bAaB$ goes in column $b$.
- $S \to \varepsilon$ goes in $a$, $b$ and $\$$ (all of $\text{FOLLOW}(S)$).

So E1 $=$ $\lbrace S \to aAbB,\ S \to \varepsilon \rbrace$ and E2 $=$ $\lbrace S \to bAaB,\ S \to \varepsilon \rbrace$.

**Row B, column $\$$.** $B \to S$ has $\varepsilon \in \text{FIRST}(S)$ and $\$ \in \text{FOLLOW}(B)$, so E3 $= B \to S$.

That is (C).

- (A), (D) put $A \to S$ or $B \to S$ in row $S$; every entry in a row belongs to that row's non-terminal.
- (B) E3 is in row $B$, so it cannot be $S \to \varepsilon$.

**Note.** E1 and E2 each hold two productions, so the grammar is **not** LL(1): the table has conflicts. The question calls it a "partial LL(1) table" but the correct fill-in shows the conflicts.

**Code check:** building the table from the computed FIRST and FOLLOW sets gave $(S,a) = \lbrace S \to aAbB, S \to \varepsilon \rbrace$, $(S,b) = \lbrace S \to bAaB, S \to \varepsilon \rbrace$ and $(B,\$) = \lbrace B \to S \rbrace$.

**Key idea:** an $\varepsilon$-production is placed under every terminal in FOLLOW of its left-hand side.

</details>
