# 2024 CS2 Q65: Counting SLR items in GOTO(I0, S)

## Question

Consider the following augmented grammar, which is to be parsed with a SLR parser. The set of terminals is $\lbrace a,b,c,d,\#,@ \rbrace$.

$$S' \to S$$
$$S \to SS \mid Aa \mid bAc \mid Bc \mid bBa$$
$$A \to d\# \qquad B \to @$$

Let $I_0 = \text{CLOSURE}(\lbrace S' \to \bullet S \rbrace)$. The number of items in the set $\text{GOTO}(I_0, S)$ is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**9**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Kernel of GOTO($I_0$, $S$):** move the dot over $S$ in every item of $I_0$ with the dot before $S$:

1. $S' \to S\bullet$
2. $S \to S\bullet S$

**Closure.** The second item has the dot before $S$, so add every $S$-production with the dot at the start:

3. $S \to \bullet SS$
4. $S \to \bullet Aa$
5. $S \to \bullet bAc$
6. $S \to \bullet Bc$
7. $S \to \bullet bBa$

Items 4 and 6 have the dot before $A$ and $B$, so add:

8. $A \to \bullet d\#$
9. $B \to \bullet @$

Total: **9 items.**

**Key idea:** GOTO = move the dot over the symbol, then take the closure. Count the kernel items plus everything the closure adds.

</details>
