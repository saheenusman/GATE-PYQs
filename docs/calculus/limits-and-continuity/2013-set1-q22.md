# 2013 CS Q22: Continuity at x = 3

## Question

Which one of the following functions is continuous at $x = 3$?

- (A) $f(x) = \begin{cases} 2, & x = 3 \\ x-1, & x > 3 \\ \dfrac{x+3}{3}, & x < 3 \end{cases}$
- (B) $f(x) = \begin{cases} 4, & x = 3 \\ 8 - x, & x \neq 3 \end{cases}$
- (C) $f(x) = \begin{cases} x+3, & x \le 3 \\ x-4, & x > 3 \end{cases}$
- (D) $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x^3 - 27}$, if $x \neq 3$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Continuity at 3 needs $\lim_{x\to3^-} f = \lim_{x\to3^+} f = f(3)$.

| Option | Left limit | Right limit | $f(3)$ | Continuous? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (A) | $(3+3)/3 = 2$ | $3-1 = 2$ | 2 | **Yes** |
| (B) | $8-3 = 5$ | $5$ | 4 | No (removable jump) |
| (C) | $3+3 = 6$ | $3-4 = -1$ | 6 | No (jump) |
| (D) | $-\infty$ | $+\infty$ | undefined | No |

**Code check:** a sympy run gave the same limits.

**Key idea:** limit from both sides must exist, agree, and equal the function value.

</details>
