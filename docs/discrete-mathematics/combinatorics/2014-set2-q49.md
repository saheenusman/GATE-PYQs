# 2014 CS2 Q49: Number of positive factors of 2014

## Question

The number of distinct positive integral factors of 2014 is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**8**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Factorise: $2014 = 2 \times 1007 = 2 \times 19 \times 53$.

Each prime appears once, so each is either in or out of a factor:

$$(1+1)(1+1)(1+1) = 2^3 = 8.$$

The factors are $1, 2, 19, 38, 53, 106, 1007, 2014$.

**Code check:** counting divisors of 2014 by trial gives 8, and SymPy's factorisation gives $\lbrace 2, 19, 53 \rbrace$.

**Classification flag:** this is elementary number theory, which has no topic here. Filed under `combinatorics` (the divisor-counting product rule).

**Key idea:** if $n = \prod p_i^{a_i}$, it has $\prod (a_i + 1)$ positive divisors.

</details>
