# 2021 CS2 Q60: Number of pairs (A, B) of subsets with A a subset of B

## Question

Let $S$ be a set consisting of 10 elements. The number of tuples of the form $(A,B)$ such that $A$ and $B$ are subsets of $S$, and $A\subseteq B$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**59049**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Decide, element by element, where it goes. For each element of $S$ there are three possibilities:

1. in $A$ (then also in $B$, since $A\subseteq B$),
2. in $B$ but not in $A$,
3. in neither.

The choices are independent, so the count is $3^{10}=59049$.

I confirmed the formula $3^n$ by brute force for $n=1,\dots,5$ (3, 9, 27, 81, 243).

**Key idea:** $A\subseteq B$ leaves each element exactly 3 states, so there are $3^n$ pairs.

</details>
