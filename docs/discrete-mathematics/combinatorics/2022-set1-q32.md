# 2022 CS Q32: Arrangements of identical balls in identical bins

## Question

The number of arrangements of six identical balls in three identical bins is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**7**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Identical balls and identical bins** means we count the ways to split 6 into at most 3 parts, where the order of the parts does not matter (partitions of 6 into at most 3 parts; empty bins are allowed):

$$6,\quad 5+1,\quad 4+2,\quad 4+1+1,\quad 3+3,\quad 3+2+1,\quad 2+2+2$$

That is **7** arrangements.

**Key idea:** when both objects and containers are identical, count integer partitions with a bounded number of parts.

</details>
