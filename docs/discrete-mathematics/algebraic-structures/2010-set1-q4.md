# 2010 CS Q4: Structure of the cube roots of unity under multiplication

## Question

Consider the set $S = \lbrace 1, \omega, \omega^2 \rbrace$, where $\omega$ and $\omega^2$ are cube roots of unity. If $*$ denotes the multiplication operation, the structure $(S, *)$ forms

- (A) A group
- (B) A ring
- (C) An integral domain
- (D) A field

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Check the group axioms for $*$ (ordinary multiplication), using $\omega^3 = 1$:

| $*$ | $1$ | $\omega$ | $\omega^2$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1$ | $1$ | $\omega$ | $\omega^2$ |
| $\omega$ | $\omega$ | $\omega^2$ | $1$ |
| $\omega^2$ | $\omega^2$ | $1$ | $\omega$ |

- **Closed:** every product is in $S$.
- **Associative:** inherited from multiplication of complex numbers.
- **Identity:** $1$.
- **Inverses:** $1^{-1}=1$, $\omega^{-1} = \omega^2$, $(\omega^2)^{-1} = \omega$.

So $(S, *)$ is a group (cyclic of order 3, abelian): (A).

- (B), (C), (D) need **two** operations. A ring would need $S$ to be an abelian group under addition too, but $1 + 1 = 2 \notin S$, and $1 + \omega = -\omega^2 \notin S$. With only $*$ given, none of these structures exist.

**Key idea:** the cube roots of unity form a cyclic group of order 3 under multiplication; rings, domains and fields need a second operation.

</details>
