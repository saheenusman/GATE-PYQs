# 2018 CS Q29: Largest proper subgroup of a group of order 84

## Question

Let $G$ be a finite group on 84 elements. The size of a largest possible proper subgroup of $G$ is ________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**42**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Lagrange's theorem:** the order of a subgroup divides $|G| = 84$.

A proper subgroup is not $G$ itself, so its order is a proper divisor of 84. The largest proper divisor of 84 is $84/2 = 42$.

**It can be reached.** In the cyclic group $\mathbb{Z}_{84}$ there is a subgroup of order 42 (the even residues). The question asks for the largest *possible* size, so the answer is **42**.

**Key idea:** subgroup orders divide the group order, so the largest proper one is $|G|$ divided by its smallest prime factor.

</details>
