# 2014 CS3 Q3: Size of a proper subgroup of a group of order 15

## Question

Let $G$ be a group with 15 elements. Let $L$ be a subgroup of $G$. It is known that $L \neq G$ and that the size of $L$ is at least 4. The size of $L$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**5**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

By **Lagrange's theorem**, $|L|$ divides $|G| = 15$. The divisors of 15 are $1, 3, 5, 15$.

- $L \ne G$ rules out 15.
- $|L| \ge 4$ rules out 1 and 3.

So $|L| = 5$.

**Code check:** the only divisor of 15 with $4 \le d < 15$ is 5.

**Key idea:** the order of a subgroup divides the order of the group.

</details>
