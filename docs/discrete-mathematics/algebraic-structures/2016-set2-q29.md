# 2016 CS2 Q29: Value of 13 to the power 99 modulo 17

## Question

The value of the expression $13^{99} \pmod{17}$, in the range 0 to 16, is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**4**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

By Fermat's little theorem, $13^{16} \equiv 1 \pmod{17}$. Since $99 = 6 \times 16 + 3$,

$$13^{99} \equiv 13^{3} = 2197 \pmod{17}$$

and $2197 = 17 \times 129 + 4$, so the result is $4$.

**Code check:** `pow(13, 99, 17)` gives `4`.

**Key idea:** Fermat: $13^{99} \equiv 13^3 \equiv 4 \pmod{17}$.

</details>
