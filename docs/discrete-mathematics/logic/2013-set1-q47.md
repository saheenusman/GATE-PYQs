# 2013 CS Q47: Not equivalent to the negation of an existential

## Question

Which one of the following is **NOT** logically equivalent to $\neg \exists x \left( \forall y\,(\alpha) \wedge \forall z\,(\beta) \right)$ ?

- (A) $\forall x \left( \exists z\,(\neg\beta) \to \forall y\,(\alpha) \right)$
- (B) $\forall x \left( \forall z\,(\beta) \to \exists y\,(\neg\alpha) \right)$
- (C) $\forall x \left( \forall y\,(\alpha) \to \exists z\,(\neg\beta) \right)$
- (D) $\forall x \left( \exists y\,(\neg\alpha) \to \exists z\,(\neg\beta) \right)$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**MTA (marks awarded to all; the key names no single option)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Start from the given formula.**

$$\neg\exists x\,(\forall y\,\alpha \wedge \forall z\,\beta) \;\equiv\; \forall x\,(\neg\forall y\,\alpha \vee \neg\forall z\,\beta) \;\equiv\; \forall x\,(\exists y\,\neg\alpha \vee \exists z\,\neg\beta)$$

**Check each option** (use $P = \forall y\,\alpha$, $Q = \forall z\,\beta$, so the target is $\forall x\,(\neg P \vee \neg Q)$):

- (B) $Q \to \neg P \equiv \neg Q \vee \neg P$. **Equivalent.**
- (C) $P \to \neg Q \equiv \neg P \vee \neg Q$. **Equivalent.**
- (A) $\neg Q \to P \equiv Q \vee P$. **Not** equivalent (it says $P$ or $Q$ holds, not "not both").
- (D) $\neg P \to \neg Q \equiv P \vee \neg Q$. **Not** equivalent.

**Two options are not equivalent, (A) and (D).** The question asks for *the* one that is not, so no single option is correct. That is why the official key gives marks to all (MTA).

**Code check:** a model check on small domains showed (B) and (C) equivalent to the target, and (A) and (D) not.

**Key idea:** push the negation inward, then rewrite $\neg P \vee \neg Q$ as an implication; $P \to \neg Q$ and $Q \to \neg P$ are the valid forms.

</details>
