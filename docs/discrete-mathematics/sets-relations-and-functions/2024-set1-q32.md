# 2024 CS1 Q32: Possible sizes of A given two bijections

## Question

Let $A$ and $B$ be non-empty finite sets such that there exist one-to-one and onto functions (i) from $A$ to $B$ and (ii) from $A \times A$ to $A \cup B$. The number of possible values of $|A|$ is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**2**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $n = |A|$.

- A bijection $A \to B$ gives $|B| = n$.
- A bijection $A \times A \to A \cup B$ gives $n^2 = |A \cup B|$.

**Range of $|A \cup B|$.** $A \cup B$ contains $A$ (so at least $n$ elements) and has at most $|A| + |B| = 2n$ elements. So

$$n \le n^2 \le 2n \;\Rightarrow\; n \le 2.$$

**Check both values** ($n \ge 1$ since $A$ is non-empty):

| $n$ | Need $\lvert A \cup B \rvert = n^2$ | Possible? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Yes, take $B = A$ |
| 2 | 4 | Yes, take $A$ and $B$ disjoint |

So $|A| \in \lbrace 1, 2 \rbrace$: **2** possible values.

**Key idea:** bound $|A \cup B|$ between $\max(|A|,|B|)$ and $|A| + |B|$.

</details>
