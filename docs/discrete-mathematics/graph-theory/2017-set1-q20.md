# 2017 CS1 Q20: Sum of degrees of all vertices in a tree with 10 vertices

## Question

Let $T$ be a tree with 10 vertices. The sum of the degrees of all the vertices in $T$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**18**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Edges of a tree.** A tree on $n$ vertices has $n - 1$ edges, so here $10 - 1 = 9$.

**Handshake lemma.** Each edge adds $1$ to the degree of each of its two endpoints:

$$\sum_v \deg(v) = 2|E| = 2 \times 9 = 18$$

The answer is **18**.

**Key idea:** the degree sum is twice the edge count, and a tree has $n-1$ edges.

</details>
