# 2019 CS2 Q22: Number of Hamiltonian cycles in a complete graph

## Question

Let $G$ be an undirected complete graph on $n$ vertices, where $n > 2$. Then, the number of different Hamiltonian cycles in $G$ is equal to

- (A) $n!$
- (B) $(n-1)!$
- (C) $1$
- (D) $\dfrac{(n-1)!}{2}$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D (official key: C OR D). My analysis finds only D correct for general $n$: see the solution**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Count.** Fix a starting vertex. The other $n-1$ vertices can follow in $(n-1)!$ orders. A cycle read forwards and backwards is the same undirected cycle, so each cycle is counted twice:

$$\frac{(n-1)!}{2}.$$

| $n$ | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cycles | 1 | 3 | 12 | 60 | 360 | 2520 |

**Code check:** brute force for $n = 3$ to $8$ gives exactly these numbers, equal to $(n-1)!/2$.

**Why the key says "C OR D".** Option (C), $1$, equals $(n-1)!/2$ only at $n = 3$. For $n = 4$ there are already 3 cycles. The question says $n > 2$ (all such $n$), so (C) is not a correct general formula. I could not justify (C) as correct, so I am recording the official key as given and my answer as D. I have not picked a side silently: the key accepts both.

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) $n!$ counts every rotation and direction separately.
- (B) $(n-1)!$ counts both directions of each cycle.

**Key idea:** fix a start vertex to remove rotations, then halve to remove reflections.

</details>
