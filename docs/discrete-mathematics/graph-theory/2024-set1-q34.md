# 2024 CS1 Q34: Spanning trees of K4

## Question

The number of spanning trees in a complete graph of 4 vertices labelled A, B, C, and D is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**16**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Cayley's formula:** the complete graph on $n$ labelled vertices has $n^{n-2}$ spanning trees.

$$4^{4-2} = 4^2 = 16.$$

**Sanity check by shape.** A spanning tree on 4 vertices is either a path (12 labelled ones: $4!/2$) or a star (4 labelled ones, one per centre). $12 + 4 = 16$ ✓.

**Key idea:** Cayley's formula $n^{n-2}$.

</details>
