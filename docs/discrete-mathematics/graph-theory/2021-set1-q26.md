# 2021 CS1 Q26: Edges in a planar graph from vertices and faces

## Question

In an undirected connected planar graph $G$, there are eight vertices and five faces. The number of edges in $G$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**11**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Euler's formula for a connected planar graph: $V-E+F=2$.

$8-E+5=2 \Rightarrow E=11$.

**Key idea:** $V-E+F=2$ (the outer face counts as a face).

</details>
