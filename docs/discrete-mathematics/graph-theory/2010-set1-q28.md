# 2010 CS Q28: Which degree sequences are not graphic

## Question

The degree sequence of a simple graph is the sequence of the degrees of the nodes in the graph in decreasing order. Which of the following sequences can **not** be the degree sequence of any graph?

- I. 7, 6, 5, 4, 4, 3, 2, 1
- II. 6, 6, 6, 6, 3, 3, 2, 2
- III. 7, 6, 6, 4, 4, 3, 2, 2
- IV. 8, 7, 7, 6, 4, 2, 1, 1

Options:

- (A) I and II
- (B) III and IV
- (C) IV only
- (D) II and IV

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Each sequence has 8 terms and an even sum, so test with Havel-Hakimi (remove the largest degree $d$, subtract 1 from the next $d$ terms, repeat).

- **I** $= 7,6,5,4,4,3,2,1$ $\to$ $5,4,3,3,2,1,0$ $\to$ $3,2,2,1,0,0$ $\to$ $1,1,0,0,0$ $\to$ $0,0,0,0$. **Graphic.**
- **II** $= 6,6,6,6,3,3,2,2$ $\to$ $5,5,5,2,2,1,2$, sorted $5,5,5,2,2,2,1$ $\to$ $4,4,1,1,1,1$ $\to$ $3,0,0,0,1$, sorted $3,1,0,0,0$. Now the 3 needs three other non-zero terms, but only one is left. **Not graphic.**
- **III** $= 7,6,6,4,4,3,2,2$ $\to$ $5,5,3,3,2,1,1$ $\to$ $4,2,2,1,0,1$, sorted $4,2,2,1,1,0$ $\to$ $1,1,0,0,0$ $\to$ done. **Graphic.**
- **IV** $= 8,7,7,6,4,2,1,1$: a vertex of degree 8 needs 8 neighbours, but a simple graph on 8 vertices allows at most 7. **Not graphic.**

So II and IV cannot be degree sequences: (D).

- (A) wrongly rejects I; (B) wrongly rejects III; (C) misses II.

**Code check:** the Erdős-Gallai test gave I true, II false, III true, IV false.

**Key idea:** test graphicality with Havel-Hakimi or Erdős-Gallai; also, a degree can never exceed $n-1$.

</details>
