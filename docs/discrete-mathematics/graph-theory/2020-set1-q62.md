# 2020 CS Q62: Edge colouring of K(3,4) plus a universal vertex

## Question

Graph $G$ is obtained by adding vertex $s$ to $K_{3,4}$ and making $s$ adjacent to every vertex of $K_{3,4}$. The minimum number of colours required to edge-colour $G$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**7**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$G$ has 8 vertices: $s$, the three vertices $a_1, a_2, a_3$ of one side and the four vertices $b_1, \dots, b_4$ of the other. It has $12 + 7 = 19$ edges.

**Lower bound.** In a proper edge colouring, the edges at one vertex need distinct colours. The degrees are:

| Vertex | Degree |
|---|---|
| $s$ | 7 |
| each $a_i$ | 4 + 1 = 5 |
| each $b_j$ | 3 + 1 = 4 |

So at least $\Delta = 7$ colours are needed.

**Upper bound.** By Vizing's theorem the answer is 7 or 8. An explicit 7-colouring shows it is 7. Each colour class is a matching:

| Colour | Edges |
|---|---|
| 0 | $s a_1$, $a_2 b_1$, $a_3 b_2$ |
| 1 | $s a_2$, $a_1 b_1$, $a_3 b_3$ |
| 2 | $s a_3$, $a_1 b_2$, $a_2 b_3$ |
| 3 | $s b_1$, $a_1 b_3$, $a_2 b_2$, $a_3 b_4$ |
| 4 | $s b_2$, $a_1 b_4$, $a_3 b_1$ |
| 5 | $s b_3$, $a_2 b_4$ |
| 6 | $s b_4$ |

The table covers $3+3+3+4+3+2+1 = 19$ edges, each exactly once. I found it by a backtracking search, then checked that at every vertex the incident edges all have different colours. For example, $s$ sees colours 0 to 6 once each, and $a_1$ sees $s$:0, $b_1$:1, $b_2$:2, $b_3$:3, $b_4$:4.

So the minimum is **7**.

**Why not 8:** Vizing's bound only says 8 always suffices. The colouring above uses just 7, which matches the lower bound.

**Key idea:** the edge chromatic number is at least the maximum degree, and by Vizing it is at most $\Delta + 1$. Here an explicit colouring meets the lower bound $\Delta = 7$.

</details>
