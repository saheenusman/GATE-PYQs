# 2007 CS Q23: Which graph has an Eulerian circuit

## Question

Which of the following graphs has an Eulerian circuit?

- (A) Any $k$-regular graph where $k$ is an even number.
- (B) A complete graph on 90 vertices.
- (C) The complement of a cycle on 25 vertices.
- (D) None of the above.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A graph has an Eulerian circuit iff it is **connected** and every vertex has **even degree**.

- (A) A $k$-regular graph with even $k$ has even degrees but need not be connected: two disjoint triangles is 2-regular with no Eulerian circuit.
- (B) $K_{90}$ has every degree $89$, which is odd.
- **(C)** In the complement of $C_{25}$ every vertex has degree $24 - 2 = 22$ (even), and the graph is connected (any two vertices are adjacent or share a common non-neighbour of the cycle). So it has an Eulerian circuit.
- (D) is wrong since (C) works.

**Code check:** networkx: complement of $C_{25}$ is connected with all degrees 22, `is_eulerian` is True; $K_{90}$ has all degrees 89 (False); two disjoint triangles are 2-regular but not Eulerian (False).
