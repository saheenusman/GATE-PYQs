# 2012 CS Q17: Bounded faces of a planar graph on 10 vertices and 15 edges

## Question

Let $G$ be a simple undirected planar graph on $10$ vertices with $15$ edges. If $G$ is a connected graph, then the number of **bounded** faces in any embedding of $G$ on the plane is equal to

- (A) 3
- (B) 4
- (C) 5
- (D) 6

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Euler's formula for a connected planar graph: $V - E + F = 2$. So

$$F = 2 - 10 + 15 = 7.$$

One of the 7 faces is the unbounded outer face, leaving $7 - 1 = 6$ bounded faces: (D).

- (A), (B), (C) come from forgetting the formula or counting $F$ off by a few.

**Key idea:** $F = E - V + 2$ counts all faces; subtract $1$ for the outer face.

</details>
