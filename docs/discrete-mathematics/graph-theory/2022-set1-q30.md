# 2022 CS Q30: Maximum edges in a disconnected graph

## Question

Consider a simple undirected graph of 10 vertices. If the graph is disconnected, then the maximum number of edges it can have is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**36**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Split the vertices into two parts** of sizes $a$ and $10 - a$ with no edges between them. The most edges come from making each part complete:

$$\binom{a}{2} + \binom{10-a}{2}$$

This is largest when the parts are as unbalanced as possible: $a = 9$ (and one isolated vertex).

$$\binom{9}{2} + \binom{1}{2} = 36 + 0 = 36$$

In general, a disconnected graph on $n$ vertices has at most $\binom{n-1}{2}$ edges.

**Key idea:** to maximise edges while staying disconnected, isolate a single vertex and make the rest a complete graph.

</details>
