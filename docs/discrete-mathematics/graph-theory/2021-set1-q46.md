# 2021 CS1 Q46: Diameter of the square of a graph

## Question

Let $G=(V,E)$ be an undirected unweighted connected graph. The *diameter* of $G$ is defined as:

$$\text{diam}(G)=\max_{u,v\in V}\lbrace\text{the length of shortest path between } u \text{ and } v\rbrace$$

Let $M$ be the adjacency matrix of $G$. Define graph $G_2$ on the same set of vertices with adjacency matrix $N$, where

$$N_{ij}=\begin{cases}1 & \text{if } M_{ij}>0 \text{ or } P_{ij}>0, \text{ where } P=M^2\\ 0 & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$

Which one of the following statements is true?

- (A) $\text{diam}(G_2)\le\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil$
- (B) $\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil<\text{diam}(G_2)<\text{diam}(G)$
- (C) $\text{diam}(G_2)=\text{diam}(G)$
- (D) $\text{diam}(G)<\text{diam}(G_2)\le2\,\text{diam}(G)$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$P_{ij}>0$ means there is a walk of length 2 from $i$ to $j$. So $G_2$ joins two vertices exactly when they are within distance 2 in $G$ (it is the square of $G$).

A shortest path of length $k$ in $G$ can be covered in hops of two edges, so the distance in $G_2$ is $\lceil k/2\rceil$. Taking the maximum over all pairs gives

$$\text{diam}(G_2)=\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil.$$

- (A) $\le\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil$ is true (with equality). **Correct.**
- (B) needs $\text{diam}(G_2)>\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil$, but they are equal.
- (C) is false once $\text{diam}(G)\ge2$.
- (D) needs $\text{diam}(G_2)>\text{diam}(G)$, but squaring only adds edges.

Checked on 3000 random connected graphs (2 to 10 vertices): in every one, $\text{diam}(G_2)=\lceil\text{diam}(G)/2\rceil$.

**Key idea:** squaring a graph (adding all distance-2 pairs) halves its diameter, rounding up.

</details>
