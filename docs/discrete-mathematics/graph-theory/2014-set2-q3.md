# 2014 CS2 Q3: Maximum number of edges in a bipartite graph on 12 vertices

## Question

The maximum number of edges in a bipartite graph on 12 vertices is __________________________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**36**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Split the 12 vertices into parts of sizes $m$ and $12 - m$. The most edges possible is every cross pair: $m(12 - m)$. This is largest when $m = 6$:

$$6 \times 6 = 36$$

This is the complete bipartite graph $K_{6,6}$.

**Code check:** `max(k*(12-k))` over $k = 0..12$ gives 36.

**Key idea:** a balanced split maximises $m(n-m)$, giving $\lfloor n^2/4 \rfloor$.

</details>
