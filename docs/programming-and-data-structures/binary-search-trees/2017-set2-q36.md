# 2017 CS2 Q36: Post-order traversal from the pre-order traversal of a BST

## Question

The pre-order traversal of a binary search tree is given by 12, 8, 6, 2, 7, 9, 10, 16, 15, 19, 17, 20. Then the post-order traversal of this tree is:

- (A) 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20
- (B) 2, 7, 6, 10, 9, 8, 15, 17, 20, 19, 16, 12
- (C) 7, 2, 6, 8, 9, 10, 20, 17, 19, 15, 16, 12
- (D) 7, 6, 2, 10, 9, 8, 15, 16, 17, 20, 19, 12

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Rebuild the tree.** The first key is the root. In a BST pre-order, the keys smaller than the root come next (left subtree), then the larger ones (right subtree). Apply this recursively:

- Root **12**. Smaller: 8, 6, 2, 7, 9, 10. Larger: 16, 15, 19, 17, 20.
- Left subtree: root **8**; its smaller keys 6, 2, 7 (root **6**, left 2, right 7); its larger keys 9, 10 (root **9**, right child 10).
- Right subtree: root **16**; smaller 15 (leaf); larger 19, 17, 20 (root **19**, left 17, right 20).

| Node | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | 8 | 16 |
| 8 | 6 | 9 |
| 6 | 2 | 7 |
| 9 | none | 10 |
| 16 | 15 | 19 |
| 19 | 17 | 20 |

**Post-order** (left, right, root):

- left of 12: $2, 7, 6$ then $10, 9$ then $8$ gives $2, 7, 6, 10, 9, 8$
- right of 12: $15$, then $17, 20, 19$, then $16$ gives $15, 17, 20, 19, 16$
- finally $12$.

$$2, 7, 6, 10, 9, 8, 15, 17, 20, 19, 16, 12$$

This is **(B)**.

**Why the others fail.** (A) is the in-order traversal (sorted). (C) and (D) put a leaf or subtree in the wrong place, e.g. 7 or 6 before 2 under node 6.

**Code check:** the keys were inserted into a BST in the given order. Its pre-order reproduced the given sequence, and its post-order equals option (B) only.

**Key idea:** the pre-order of a BST determines the tree uniquely: the first key is the root and the next larger key splits left from right.

</details>
