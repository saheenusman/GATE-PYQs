# 2015 CS3 Q28: Evaluating a postfix expression

## Question

The result evaluating the postfix expression $10\ 5\ +\ 60\ 6\ /\ *\ 8\ -$ is

- (A) 284
- (B) 213
- (C) 142
- (D) 71

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Scan left to right. Push operands. On an operator, pop two (the first popped is the right operand) and push the result.

| Token | Stack after |
|---|---|
| 10 | 10 |
| 5 | 10, 5 |
| + | 15 |
| 60 | 15, 60 |
| 6 | 15, 60, 6 |
| / | 15, 10 |
| * | 150 |
| 8 | 150, 8 |
| − | **142** |

Option (C).

**Code check:** a stack evaluation gives 142.

**Key idea:** in postfix, the later-popped operand is the left one: $a\ b\ - = a - b$.

</details>
