# 2011 CS Q23: Which tree is a max-heap

## Question

A max-heap is a heap where the value of each parent is greater than or equal to the value of its children. Which of the following is a max-heap?

![Four binary trees. (A) Root 10 with children 8 and 6; 8 has only a left child 4; 6 has children 5 and 2; 5 has a left child 1. (B) Root 10 with children 8 and 6; 8 has children 4 and 5; 6 has children 1 and 2. (C) Root 10 with children 5 and 6; 5 has children 4 and 8; 6 has children 2 and 1. (D) Root 5 with children 2 and 8; 2 has children 1 and 4; 8 has children 6 and 10.](../../assets/2011/set1-q23.png)

Options are the four trees (A) to (D) in the figure.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A binary heap needs two things: the **shape** is a complete binary tree (all levels full except possibly the last, filled left to right), and the **order** is that each parent is $\ge$ its children.

| Tree | Shape complete? | Order OK? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| (A) | no: $8$ has only a left child while $6$ has two children, and $5$ has a child | yes | not a heap |
| (B) | yes | $10 \ge 8, 6$; $8 \ge 4, 5$; $6 \ge 1, 2$ | **max-heap** |
| (C) | yes | $5 < 8$ (child larger than parent) | no |
| (D) | yes | the root $5$ has a larger child $8$ | no |

So (B).

**Code check:** the four trees were encoded by level-order position; only (B) is complete with every parent $\ge$ its children.

**Key idea:** a heap is a complete binary tree plus the parent-vs-child ordering; check both.

</details>
