# 2015 CS1 Q37: Max heap after inserting 35

## Question

Consider a max heap, represented by the array: 40, 30, 20, 10, 15, 16, 17, 8, 4.

| Array Index | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value | 40 | 30 | 20 | 10 | 15 | 16 | 17 | 8 | 4 |

Now consider that a value 35 is inserted into this heap. After insertion, the new heap is

- (A) 40, 30, 20, 10, 15, 16, 17, 8, 4, 35
- (B) 40, 35, 20, 10, 30, 16, 17, 8, 4, 15
- (C) 40, 30, 20, 10, 35, 16, 17, 8, 4, 15
- (D) 40, 35, 20, 10, 15, 16, 17, 8, 4, 30

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Insert 35 at the next free slot, index 10, then **sift up** (parent of index $i$ is $\lfloor i/2 \rfloor$).

| Step | Action | Array |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | place 35 at index 10 | 40, 30, 20, 10, 15, 16, 17, 8, 4, **35** |
| 2 | parent of 10 is index 5 (15). $35 > 15$, swap | 40, 30, 20, 10, **35**, 16, 17, 8, 4, 15 |
| 3 | parent of 5 is index 2 (30). $35 > 30$, swap | 40, **35**, 20, 10, **30**, 16, 17, 8, 4, 15 |
| 4 | parent of 2 is index 1 (40). $35 < 40$, stop | |

Result: 40, 35, 20, 10, 30, 16, 17, 8, 4, 15. Option (B).

(A) is the array before sifting up: 35 at index 10 is larger than its parent 15. (C) stops after one swap: 35 at index 5 is still larger than its parent 30 at index 2. (D) has 30 at index 10 under parent 15, which breaks the heap property.

**Key idea:** heap insertion is append then sift up, along one root path (at most $\log n$ swaps).

</details>
