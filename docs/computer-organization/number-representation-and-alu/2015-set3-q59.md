# 2015 CS3 Q59: Number of solutions of (43) in base x equal to (y3) in base 8

## Question

Consider the equation $(43)_x = (y3)_8$ where $x$ and $y$ are unknown. The number of possible solutions is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**5**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Convert both sides to decimal:

$$4x + 3 = 8y + 3 \;\Rightarrow\; 4x = 8y \;\Rightarrow\; x = 2y$$

**Constraints:**

- In $(43)_x$ the digit 4 must be less than the base: $x \ge 5$.
- In $(y3)_8$, $y$ is an octal digit: $y \le 7$ (and non-zero as a leading digit).

With $x = 2y \ge 5$ we need $y \ge 3$:

| $y$ | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $x = 2y$ | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 |

**Answer: 5.**

**Code check:** a search over $x$ up to 200 and $y \in 0..7$ finds exactly these five pairs. Allowing $y = 0$ adds none, since then $x = 0$ is not a valid base.

**Key idea:** digits must be smaller than their base. Equate the decimal values and apply the digit limits.

</details>
