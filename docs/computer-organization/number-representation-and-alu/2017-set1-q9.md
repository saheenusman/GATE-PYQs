# 2017 CS1 Q9: Overflow condition for a 2's complement ripple-carry adder

## Question

When two 8-bit numbers $A_7 \cdots A_0$ and $B_7 \cdots B_0$ in 2's complement representation (with $A_0$ and $B_0$ as the least significant bits) are added using a **ripple-carry adder**, the sum bits obtained are $S_7 \cdots S_0$ and the carry bits are $C_7 \cdots C_0$. An overflow is said to have occurred if

- (A) the carry bit $C_7$ is 1
- (B) all the carry bits $(C_7, \cdots, C_0)$ are 1
- (C) $(A_7 \cdot B_7 \cdot \overline{S_7} + \overline{A_7} \cdot \overline{B_7} \cdot S_7)$ is 1
- (D) $(A_0 \cdot B_0 \cdot \overline{S_0} + \overline{A_0} \cdot \overline{B_0} \cdot S_0)$ is 1

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Rule.** In 2's complement, overflow happens only when two numbers of the **same sign** give a result of the **opposite sign**. Looking at the sign bits (bit 7):

- two positives ($A_7=B_7=0$) giving a negative ($S_7=1$): $\overline{A_7}\cdot\overline{B_7}\cdot S_7$
- two negatives ($A_7=B_7=1$) giving a positive ($S_7=0$): $A_7\cdot B_7\cdot\overline{S_7}$

Their OR is the expression in **(C)**.

**Why the others fail.**

- (A) $C_7=1$: $-1 + -1 = -2$ gives $C_7 = 1$ but no overflow. Also $1 + 127 = 128$ overflows with $C_7 = 0$.
- (B) all carries 1: $(-1) + 1 = 0$ makes every carry 1 and there is no overflow.
- (D) it tests bit 0 (the least significant bit), which says nothing about the sign.

**Code check:** over all $256\times256$ input pairs, expression (C) matched the true overflow (sum outside $-128..127$) every time. $C_7$ disagreed with true overflow in 32512 pairs, "all carries 1" in 18571, and the bit-0 expression in 24576.

**Key idea:** overflow $=$ same-sign operands, different-sign result; equivalently $C_7 \oplus C_6 = 1$.

</details>
