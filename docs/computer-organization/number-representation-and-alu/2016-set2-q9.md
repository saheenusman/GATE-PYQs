# 2016 CS2 Q9: Difference in distinct 16-bit integers between 2's complement and sign magnitude

## Question

Let $X$ be the number of distinct 16-bit integers in 2's complement representation. Let $Y$ be the number of distinct 16-bit integers in sign magnitude representation. Then $X - Y$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**1**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- 2's complement: all $2^{16} = 65536$ bit patterns are different integers ($-32768$ to $32767$), so $X = 65536$.
- Sign magnitude: the patterns $0000\ldots0$ and $1000\ldots0$ both mean $0$ ($+0$ and $-0$), so $Y = 65536 - 1 = 65535$.

$$X - Y = 1$$

**Key idea:** sign magnitude has two zeros, so one fewer distinct value.

</details>
