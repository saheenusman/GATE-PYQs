# 2014 CS2 Q8: Number of solutions of (123) in base 5 equal to (x8) in base y

## Question

Consider the equation $(123)_5 = (x8)_y$ with $x$ and $y$ as unknown. The number of possible solutions is _____ .

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$(123)_5 = 25 + 10 + 3 = 38$. The right side is $(x8)_y = xy + 8$. So

$$xy = 30$$

The digit 8 needs $y > 8$, and the digit $x$ needs $1 \le x < y$. The factor pairs of 30 with $y > 8$:

| $y$ | $x$ | Valid? |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 3 | yes |
| 15 | 2 | yes |
| 30 | 1 | yes |

**3 solutions.** ($y = 6$ or $5$ would make the digit 8 illegal.)

**Code check:** a search over $y = 9..39$ finds $(3,10), (2,15), (1,30)$.

**Key idea:** a digit must be smaller than the base.

</details>
