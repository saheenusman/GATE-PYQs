# 2015 CS2 Q42: Average time to read a sector with a slow controller

## Question

Consider a typical disk that rotates at 15000 rotations per minute (RPM) and has a transfer rate of $50 \times 10^6$ bytes/sec. If the average seek time of the disk is twice the average rotational delay and the controller's transfer time is 10 times the disk transfer time, the average time (in milliseconds) to read or write a 512-byte sector of the disk is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**6.11**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- One rotation: $60{,}000/15{,}000 = 4$ ms, so average rotational delay $= 2$ ms.
- Average seek time $= 2 \times 2 = 4$ ms.
- Disk transfer time: $\dfrac{512}{50\times10^6}$ s $= 10.24\ \mu s = 0.01024$ ms.
- Controller transfer time: $10 \times 0.01024 = 0.1024$ ms.

$$4 + 2 + 0.01024 + 0.1024 = 6.11264 \approx 6.11\text{ ms}$$

The key accepts $6.1$ to $6.2$.

**Key idea:** average time $=$ seek $+$ rotational delay $+$ disk transfer $+$ controller transfer.

</details>
