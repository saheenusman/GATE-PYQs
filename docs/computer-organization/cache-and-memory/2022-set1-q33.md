# 2022 CS Q33: Minimum hit rate after a cache optimisation

## Question

A cache memory that has a hit rate of 0.8 has an access latency 10 ns and miss penalty 100 ns. An optimization is done on the cache to reduce the miss rate. However, the optimization results in an increase of cache access latency to 15 ns, whereas the miss penalty is not affected. The minimum hit rate (rounded off to two decimal places) needed after the optimization such that it should not increase the average memory access time is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.85**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Average memory access time** $= t_{\text{cache}} + (1 - h) \times \text{miss penalty}$, where the cache is always accessed first.

**Before:** $10 + 0.2 \times 100 = 30$ ns.

**After, with hit rate $h$:** $15 + (1 - h) \times 100 \le 30$

$$1 - h \le 0.15 \;\Rightarrow\; h \ge 0.85$$

The minimum hit rate is **0.85**.

**Assumption:** the miss penalty is paid on top of the cache access latency (hit time + miss rate × miss penalty). This is the reading that gives the official answer 0.85.

**Key idea:** AMAT $=$ hit time $+$ miss rate $\times$ miss penalty; a slower hit time must be paid for by a lower miss rate.

</details>
