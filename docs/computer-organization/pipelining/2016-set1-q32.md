# 2016 CS1 Q32: Throughput increase after splitting the slowest pipeline stage

## Question

The stage delays in a 4-stage pipeline are 800, 500, 400 and 300 picoseconds. The first stage (with delay 800 picoseconds) is replaced with a functionally equivalent design involving two stages with respective delays 600 and 350 picoseconds. The throughput increase of the pipeline is ______ percent.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**33.33**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A pipeline's clock period is set by its slowest stage, and its steady-state throughput is $1/\text{period}$.

| Design | Stage delays (ps) | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Before | 800, 500, 400, 300 | 800 ps |
| After | 600, 350, 500, 400, 300 | 600 ps |

$$\text{Throughput increase} = \frac{1/600 - 1/800}{1/800} = \frac{800}{600} - 1 = 0.3333 = 33.33\%$$

The key accepts $33$ to $34$.

**Key idea:** throughput $\propto 1/\text{longest stage}$: $800/600 - 1 = 33.3\%$.

</details>
