# 2026 CS1 Q60: Structural-hazard stalls in the EX stage

## Question

The EX stage of a pipelined processor performs the memory read operations for LOAD instructions, and the operations for the arithmetic and logic instructions. Let $t_{EX}$ denote the time taken by the EX stage to perform the operation for an instruction. For each instruction type, the values of $t_{EX}$ and $M$ (the number of instructions of that type in a sequence of 100 instructions for a program P) are given below.

The duration of the pipeline clock cycle is 1 ns. Assume that the latch time for the interstage buffers is negligible.

| Instruction | $t_{EX}$ (ns) | $M$ |
|---|---|---|
| LOAD | 1.8 | 15 |
| IMUL | 1.5 | 10 |
| IDIV | 2.5 | 5 |
| FADD | 1.7 | 10 |
| FSUB | 1.7 | 5 |
| FMUL | 2.8 | 15 |
| FDIV | 3.2 | 5 |
| All other | less than 1.0 | 35 |

When program P is executed, the number of clock cycles for which the pipeline is stalled due to structural hazards in the EX stage is ______. (answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**95**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Idea.** The EX stage is not pipelined, so an instruction that needs $n$ cycles holds it for $n$ cycles. The pipeline stalls for the **extra** $n - 1$ cycles.

Cycles needed $= \lceil t_{EX} / 1\text{ ns} \rceil$.

| Instr | $t_{EX}$ | Cycles | Stall each | $M$ | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOAD | 1.8 | 2 | 1 | 15 | 15 |
| IMUL | 1.5 | 2 | 1 | 10 | 10 |
| IDIV | 2.5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| FADD | 1.7 | 2 | 1 | 10 | 10 |
| FSUB | 1.7 | 2 | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| FMUL | 2.8 | 3 | 2 | 15 | 30 |
| FDIV | 3.2 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 15 |
| Others | < 1.0 | 1 | 0 | 35 | 0 |

Total stalls $= 15+10+10+10+5+30+15 = \mathbf{95}$.

**Sanity check:** extra cycles equal $\sum M(\lceil t \rceil - 1)$, and no row is negative.

**Key idea:** a non-pipelined unit that is busy for $n$ cycles stalls the following instructions for $n-1$ cycles.

</details>
