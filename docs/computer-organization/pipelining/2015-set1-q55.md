# 2015 CS1 Q55: Speedup from a five-stage pipeline with a slower clock

## Question

Consider a non-pipelined processor with a clock rate of 2.5 gigahertz and average cycles per instruction of four. The same processor is upgraded to a pipelined processor with five stages; but due to the internal pipeline delay, the clock speed is reduced to 2 gigahertz. Assume that there are no stalls in the pipeline. The speed up achieved in this pipelined processor is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3.2**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Time per instruction $=$ CPI $\div$ clock rate.

- Non-pipelined: $\dfrac{4}{2.5\text{ GHz}} = 1.6$ ns.
- Pipelined, no stalls: CPI $= 1$, so $\dfrac{1}{2\text{ GHz}} = 0.5$ ns.

$$\text{Speedup} = \frac{1.6}{0.5} = 3.2$$

**Key idea:** speedup is the ratio of time per instruction; with no stalls the pipelined CPI is 1.

</details>
