# 2010 CS Q33: Clock cycles for four dependent instructions with unequal PO stages

## Question

A 5-stage pipelined processor has Instruction Fetch (IF), Instruction Decode (ID), Operand Fetch (OF), Perform Operation (PO) and Write Operand (WO) stages. The IF, ID, OF and WO stages take $1$ clock cycle each for any instruction. The PO stage takes $1$ clock cycle for ADD and SUB instructions, $3$ clock cycles for MUL instruction, and $6$ clock cycles for DIV instruction respectively. Operand forwarding is used in the pipeline. What is the number of clock cycles needed to execute the following sequence of instructions?

| Instruction | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I0: MUL R2, R0, R1 | $R2 \leftarrow R0 \times R1$ |
| I1: DIV R5, R3, R4 | $R5 \leftarrow R3 / R4$ |
| I2: ADD R2, R5, R2 | $R2 \leftarrow R5 + R2$ |
| I3: SUB R5, R2, R6 | $R5 \leftarrow R2 - R6$ |

- (A) $13$
- (B) $15$
- (C) $17$
- (D) $19$

<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Status: disputed.** The official key in the supplied PDF is (C) $17$. My own timeline gives $15$, (B). The two differ only in **where the forwarded value enters the pipeline**, which the question does not state.

**My model (answer 15).** A result forwarded at the end of the producer's PO stage can be used by the consumer's PO stage in the very next cycle. Instructions move in order, one per stage.

| Instr | IF | ID | OF | PO | WO |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I0 MUL | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4-6 | 7 |
| I1 DIV | 2 | 3 | 4 | 7-12 | 13 |
| I2 ADD | 3 | 4 | 5 | 13 | 14 |
| I3 SUB | 4 | 5 | 6 | 14 | 15 |

I1 waits in OF (cycles $4$ to $6$) until the MUL leaves PO. I2 needs $R5$ from the DIV, which ends PO in cycle $12$, so its PO runs in $13$. I3 needs $R2$ from I2 (ready after $13$), so its PO runs in $14$ and it finishes in cycle $15$.

**Key's model (answer 17).** The forwarded value goes to the consumer's **operand fetch** stage, so the consumer must do OF after the producer's PO ends, and only then can it start PO:

- I2: OF in cycle $13$, PO in $14$, WO in $15$.
- I3: OF in cycle $15$ (after I2's PO ends in $14$), PO in $16$, WO in $17$.

That gives $17$, (C).

I could not show from the wording that one model is wrong. Other published solutions I looked at give $15$ (the first model), but the supplied key says $17$. I record the key and flag the difference.

- (A) $13$ and (D) $19$ do not come out of either timeline above.

**Code check:** a small in-order pipeline simulation gave $15$ when forwarding enters PO and $17$ when it enters OF.

**Key idea:** with forwarding, the stall is set by the slowest producer (the 6-cycle DIV); the exact total depends on which stage receives the forwarded value.
