# 2016 CS2 Q30: Minimum time to compute F(G(Xi)) for ten inputs on two UF and two UG units

## Question

Suppose the functions $F$ and $G$ can be computed in 5 and 3 nanoseconds by functional units $U_F$ and $U_G$, respectively. Given two instances of $U_F$ and two instances of $U_G$, it is required to implement the computation $F(G(X_i))$ for $1 \le i \le 10$. Ignoring all other delays, the minimum time required to complete this computation is ______ nanoseconds.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**28**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Each item needs a $G$ first, then an $F$ on its result. Overlap the two stages (a two-stage pipeline with two units each).

- $G$ stage: two units produce results in pairs at $3, 6, 9, 12, 15$ ns.
- $F$ stage: two units, each job $5$ ns. The first pair can start at $3$ ns. The $F$ units are the bottleneck ($2$ jobs per $5$ ns against $2$ per $3$ ns from $G$), so the next $F$ pairs are never waiting on $G$.

Ten $F$ jobs on two units take $5$ rounds $\times$ $5$ ns $= 25$ ns, starting at $3$ ns:

$$3 + 25 = 28\ \text{ns}$$

**Code check:** a small list-scheduling simulation of the two stages gave $28$.

**Key idea:** $G$ finishes the first pair at 3 ns; five rounds of $F$ at 5 ns each follow: $3 + 25$.

</details>
