# 2015 CS2 Q53: Clock cycles for MUL, DIV, ADD, SUB in a 4-stage pipeline

## Question

Consider the sequence of machine instructions given below:

```
MUL  R5, R0, R1
DIV  R6, R2, R3
ADD  R7, R5, R6
SUB  R8, R7, R4
```

In the above sequence, R0 to R8 are general purpose registers. In the instructions shown, the first register stores the result of the operation performed on the second and the third registers. This sequence of instructions is to be executed in a pipelined instruction processor with the following 4 stages: (1) Instruction Fetch and Decode (IF), (2) Operand Fetch (OF), (3) Perform Operation (PO) and (4) Write back the result (WB). The IF, OF and WB stages take 1 clock cycle each for any instruction. The PO stage takes 1 clock cycle for ADD or SUB instruction, 3 clock cycles for MUL instruction and 5 clock cycles for DIV instruction. The pipelined processor uses operand forwarding from the PO stage to the OF stage. The number of clock cycles taken for the execution of the above sequence of instructions is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**13**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Dependences.** ADD needs R5 (from MUL) and R6 (from DIV). SUB needs R7 (from ADD).

**Forwarding.** A result produced at the end of a PO cycle can be used by OF in the next cycle.

| Instr | IF | OF | PO | WB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MUL | 1 | 2 | 3–5 | 6 |
| DIV | 2 | 3 | 4–8 | 9 |
| ADD | 3 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| SUB | 4 | 11 | 12 | 13 |

- DIV's PO ends in cycle 8, later than MUL's (5), so ADD's OF is in cycle 9.
- ADD's PO ends in cycle 10, so SUB's OF is in cycle 11.
- SUB: PO in 12, WB in 13.

Total: **13** cycles.

**Assumption (needed for the key).** MUL and DIV are allowed to be in PO at the same time (cycles 4 and 5), as if they use separate functional units. The question does not say this. If PO were a single shared stage, DIV would wait for MUL, would run in cycles 6–10, and the total would be **15**. The official key is 13.

**Code check:** a cycle-by-cycle simulation gives 13 with overlapping PO and 15 with a shared PO.

**Key idea:** with forwarding, a dependent instruction waits only for the producer's PO to finish. The slowest producer (DIV) sets the pace.

</details>
