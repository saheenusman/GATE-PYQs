# 2013 CS Q45: Five-stage pipeline with a branch

## Question

Consider an instruction pipeline with five stages without any branch prediction: Fetch Instruction (FI), Decode Instruction (DI), Fetch Operand (FO), Execute Instruction (EI) and Write Operand (WO). The stage delays for FI, DI, FO, EI and WO are 5 ns, 7 ns, 10 ns, 8 ns and 6 ns, respectively. There are intermediate storage buffers after each stage and the delay of each buffer is 1 ns. A program consisting of 12 instructions $I_1, I_2, I_3, \dots, I_{12}$ is executed in this pipelined processor. Instruction $I_4$ is the only branch instruction and its branch target is $I_9$. If the branch is taken during the execution of this program, the time (in ns) needed to complete the program is

- (A) 132
- (B) 165
- (C) 176
- (D) 328

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Cycle time.** The slowest stage (FO, 10 ns) plus the 1 ns buffer: $11$ ns per cycle.

**Timeline** (cycle numbers). $I_4$ is fetched in cycle 4 and executes (EI) in cycle 7. Assume the branch outcome is known at the end of EI, so $I_9$ is fetched in cycle 8.

| Instruction | FI cycle | WO cycle |
|---|---|---|
| $I_1$ to $I_4$ | 1 to 4 | 5 to 8 |
| $I_9$ | 8 | 12 |
| $I_{10}$ | 9 | 13 |
| $I_{11}$ | 10 | 14 |
| $I_{12}$ | 11 | 15 |

$I_5$ to $I_8$ are skipped. The program finishes at the end of cycle 15: $15 \times 11 = 165$ ns.

**Why the others fail:**

- (A) $132 = 12 \times 11$ ignores the pipeline fill time.
- (C) $176 = 16 \times 11$ is what you get with no branch at all, $(5 + 11)\times 11$, and also if the branch is resolved only at WO.
- (D) $328$ is far larger than any pipelined schedule for 12 instructions.

**Flag (assumption):** the question does not say when the branch outcome is known. The key's 165 needs it known after **EI**. If it were known only after WO, the time would be 176 ns.

**Code check:** a cycle-by-cycle count gave 165 ns (branch resolved at EI) and 176 ns (resolved at WO).

**Key idea:** cycle time = slowest stage + buffer; a taken branch skips the instructions in between but pays the refill delay.

</details>
