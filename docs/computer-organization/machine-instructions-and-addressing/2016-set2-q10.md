# 2016 CS2 Q10: Immediate field width in a 32-bit instruction

## Question

A processor has 40 distinct instructions and 24 general purpose registers. A 32-bit instruction word has an opcode, two register operands and an immediate operand. The number of bits available for the immediate operand field is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**16**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- Opcode: $\lceil \log_2 40 \rceil = 6$ bits.
- Each register operand: $\lceil \log_2 24 \rceil = 5$ bits, so two of them take $10$ bits.
- Immediate: $32 - 6 - 10 = 16$ bits.

**Key idea:** $32 - 6 - 2 \times 5 = 16$ bits.

</details>
