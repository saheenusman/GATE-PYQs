# 2019 CS2 Q30: Probability the pivot lands in the worst position

## Question

An array of 25 distinct elements is to be sorted using quicksort. Assume that the pivot element is chosen uniformly at random. The probability that the pivot element gets placed in the worst possible location in the first round of partitioning (rounded off to 2 decimal places) is ________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.08**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

After partitioning, the pivot lands at the position equal to its rank. The worst split is when the pivot is the **smallest** or the **largest** element: one side is empty and the other has 24 elements. That is rank 1 or rank 25.

The pivot is uniform over 25 elements, and 2 of them are bad:

$$P = \frac{2}{25} = 0.08.$$

**Key idea:** the pivot's final position is its rank. The extremes (min or max) cause the worst-case split.

</details>
