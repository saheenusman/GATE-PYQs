# 2021 CS1 Q13: Order the growth of three functions

## Question

Consider the following three functions.

$f_1 = 10^n \qquad f_2 = n^{\log n} \qquad f_3 = n^{\sqrt n}$

Which one of the following options arranges the functions in the increasing order of asymptotic growth rate?

- (A) $f_3, f_2, f_1$
- (B) $f_2, f_1, f_3$
- (C) $f_1, f_2, f_3$
- (D) $f_2, f_3, f_1$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Compare the natural logs.

| Function | $\ln f$ |
|---|---|
| $f_1=10^n$ | $n\ln 10$ |
| $f_2=n^{\log n}$ | $(\log n)^2$ (up to a constant) |
| $f_3=n^{\sqrt n}$ | $\sqrt n\,\ln n$ |

Polylogarithmic $(\log n)^2$ grows slower than $\sqrt n\ln n$, which grows slower than linear $n\ln10$. So $f_2<f_3<f_1$.

Option (D). The other orders put $f_1$ (the exponential) too early.

Verified numerically at $n=10^3,10^6,10^9$: $\ln f_2<\ln f_3<\ln f_1$ in each case.

**Key idea:** compare growth rates by taking logs of both sides.

</details>
