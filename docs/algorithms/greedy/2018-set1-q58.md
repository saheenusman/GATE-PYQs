# 2018 CS Q58: Optimal versus greedy value in a 0/1 knapsack

## Question

Consider the weights and values of items listed below. Note that there is only one unit of each item.

| Item number | Weight (in Kgs) | Value (in Rupees) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 60 |
| 2 | 7 | 28 |
| 3 | 4 | 20 |
| 4 | 2 | 24 |

The task is to pick a subset of these items such that their total weight is no more than 11 Kgs and their total value is maximized. Moreover, no item may be split. The total value of items picked by an optimal algorithm is denoted by $V_{\text{opt}}$. A greedy algorithm sorts the items by their value-to-weight ratios in descending order and packs them greedily, starting from the first item in the ordered list. The total value of items picked by the greedy algorithm is denoted by $V_{\text{greedy}}$.

The value of $V_{\text{opt}} - V_{\text{greedy}}$ is ____________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**16**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Ratios (value per kg):** item 1: 6, item 2: 4, item 3: 5, item 4: 12. Order: **4, 1, 3, 2**.

**Greedy (capacity 11):**

| Item | Weight | Fits? | Total weight | Total value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 2 | yes | 2 | 24 |
| 1 | 10 | no (12 > 11), skip | 2 | 24 |
| 3 | 4 | yes | 6 | 44 |
| 2 | 7 | no (13 > 11), skip | 6 | 44 |

$V_{\text{greedy}} = 44$.

**Optimal.** Try the feasible subsets: item 1 alone gives 60; items 3 + 2 give 48 (weight 11); items 4 + 2 give 52; items 4 + 3 give 44. The best is item 1 alone: $V_{\text{opt}} = 60$.

$$V_{\text{opt}} - V_{\text{greedy}} = 60 - 44 = 16$$

**Note on the interpretation:** the key's 16 assumes greedy *skips* an item that does not fit and keeps going. If greedy stopped at the first item that does not fit, $V_{\text{greedy}}$ would be 24 and the difference 36. The key's answer corresponds to the skip-and-continue reading.

**Code check:** brute force over all 16 subsets gave $V_{\text{opt}} = 60$; the greedy simulation gave 44.

**Key idea:** greedy by value density is not optimal for 0/1 knapsack because items cannot be split.

</details>
