# 2014 CS1 Q39: Minimum comparisons to find both min and max of 100 numbers

## Question

The minimum number of comparisons required to find the minimum and the maximum of 100 numbers is _________________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**148**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Use the pairing method:

1. Compare the 100 numbers in 50 pairs: **50** comparisons. This gives 50 "larger" and 50 "smaller" elements.
2. The maximum is the largest of the 50 larger ones: **49** comparisons.
3. The minimum is the smallest of the 50 smaller ones: **49** comparisons.

$$50 + 49 + 49 = 148 = \frac{3n}{2} - 2 \quad (n = 100)$$

This is also the lower bound for even $n$, so 148 is the minimum.

**Code check:** `3*100//2 - 2` gives `148`.

**Key idea:** pair up the elements: $\lceil 3n/2 \rceil - 2$ comparisons for min and max together, against $2n-2$ done naively.

</details>
