# 2014 CS2 Q52: Number of distinct minimum spanning trees

## Question

The number of distinct minimum spanning trees for the weighted graph below is ______.

![Weighted undirected graph with 9 vertices. Left cluster of six vertices (top-left, top-right, left, centre, right, bottom). Edge weights: top-left to top-right 2, top-left to left 2, top-left to centre 1, top-right to centre 2, top-right to right 1, left to centre 2, centre to right 2, left to bottom 1, centre to bottom 1. A weight-2 edge joins the bottom vertex to the lower-left vertex of a right-hand triangle, whose other edges have weights 2 (to the top vertex), 1 (top to lower-right) and 2 (lower-left to lower-right).]](../../assets/2014/set2-q52.png)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**6**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Take the weight-1 edges first (Kruskal). They form no cycle, and give 4 components:

| Component | Vertices (1-edges joining them) |
|---|---|
| $A$ | top-left, centre, bottom, left (3 one-edges) |
| $B$ | top-right, right (1 one-edge) |
| $C$ | top and lower-right of the triangle (1 one-edge) |
| $D$ | lower-left triangle vertex (alone) |

That is 5 edges for 9 vertices, so 3 edges of weight 2 are needed to connect the four components.

The weight-2 edges between different components:

- $A$-$B$: **3** parallel edges (any one will do),
- $A$-$D$: **1** edge (the long weight-2 edge from the bottom vertex),
- $C$-$D$: **2** edges.

The components form a path $B - A - D - C$, so the tree must use one edge from each link:

$$3 \times 1 \times 2 = 6.$$

The MST weight is $5 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot 2 = 11$.

**Code check:** I entered the 13 edges as read from the figure and counted all spanning trees of weight 11 by brute force, which gives 6, in agreement with the key. NetworkX confirms the MST weight 11.

**Key idea:** MSTs are counted by contracting each weight class: the number of choices at each level multiplies.

</details>
