# 2021 CS2 Q65: Sum of quality scores of all vertices in a weighted DAG

## Question

In a directed acyclic graph with a source vertex $s$, the *quality-score* of a directed path is defined to be the product of the weights of the edges on the path. Further, for a vertex $v$ other than $s$, the quality-score of $v$ is defined to be the maximum among the quality-scores of all the paths from $s$ to $v$. The quality-score of $s$ is assumed to be 1.

![A weighted DAG on the vertices s, a, b, c, d, e, f, g, t laid out as a 3 by 3 grid. Edges: s to c weight 1, s to a weight 9, c to f weight 9, c to d weight 1, a to d weight 1, a to b weight 1, f to g weight 1, d to g weight 9, d to e weight 9, b to e weight 1, g to t weight 1, e to t weight 9.](../../assets/2021/set2-q65.png)

The sum of the quality-scores of all the vertices in the graph shown above is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**929**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Process the vertices in topological order. For each vertex take the maximum over its in-edges of (score of the tail $\times$ edge weight).

| vertex | candidates | score |
|---|---|---|
| $s$ | given | 1 |
| $c$ | $s\cdot1$ | 1 |
| $a$ | $s\cdot9$ | 9 |
| $f$ | $c\cdot9$ | 9 |
| $d$ | $c\cdot1=1$, $a\cdot1=9$ | 9 |
| $b$ | $a\cdot1$ | 9 |
| $g$ | $f\cdot1=9$, $d\cdot9=81$ | 81 |
| $e$ | $d\cdot9=81$, $b\cdot1=9$ | 81 |
| $t$ | $g\cdot1=81$, $e\cdot9=729$ | 729 |

Sum: $1+1+9+9+9+9+81+81+729=929$.

A script that runs the same recurrence gives 929.

**Key idea:** in a DAG, one pass in topological order gives the best path score to every vertex.

</details>
