# 2015 CS2 Q45: States in the minimal DFA for strings ending in 10

## Question

The number of states in the minimal deterministic finite automaton corresponding to the regular expression $(0+1)^*(10)$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The language is all strings over $\lbrace 0,1\rbrace$ that **end in `10`**. Track the longest useful suffix:

| State | Meaning | on 0 | on 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $q_0$ | no useful suffix (start, or ends in 0 but not 10) | $q_0$ | $q_1$ |
| $q_1$ | ends in 1 | $q_2$ | $q_1$ |
| $q_2$ | ends in 10 (accepting) | $q_0$ | $q_1$ |

These three states are pairwise distinguishable: $q_2$ is the only accepting state; $q_0$ and $q_1$ differ because the input `0` leads to acceptance from $q_1$ but not from $q_0$.

So the minimal DFA has **3** states.

**Code check:** subset construction on the NFA and Moore minimisation also gave 3 states.

**Key idea:** one state per relevant suffix of "10": none, "1", "10".

</details>
