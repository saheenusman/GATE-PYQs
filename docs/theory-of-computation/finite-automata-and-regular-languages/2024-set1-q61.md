# 2024 CS1 Q61: Counting short strings outside two regular expressions

## Question

Consider the following two regular expressions over the alphabet $\lbrace 0,1 \rbrace$:

$$r = 0^* + 1^*, \qquad s = 01^* + 10^*$$

The total number of strings of length less than or equal to 5, which are neither in $r$ nor in $s$, is ________

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**44**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**All strings of length $\le 5$:** $2^0 + 2^1 + \dots + 2^5 = 63$ (including the empty string).

**Strings in $r$:** all-0 or all-1 strings. Length 0: $\varepsilon$ (1). Lengths 1 to 5: 2 each (10). Total **11**.

**Strings in $s$:** $01^k$ and $10^k$ with $k+1 \le 5$, i.e. $k = 0..4$: 5 strings of each kind, **10** total.

**Overlap:** $0$ and $1$ are in both ($0 \in 0^*$ and $01^0$; $1 \in 1^*$ and $10^0$). Overlap $= 2$.

$$|r \cup s| = 11 + 10 - 2 = 19, \qquad 63 - 19 = 44.$$

A brute-force enumeration also gave 44.

**Key idea:** count with inclusion-exclusion, and watch for strings that belong to both expressions.

</details>
