# 2007 CS Q29: Minimum DFA for counts divisible by 3 and 5

## Question

A minimum state deterministic finite automaton accepting the language $L = \lbrace w \mid w \in \lbrace 0,1 \rbrace^*,\ \text{number of 0s and 1s in } w \text{ are divisible by 3 and 5, respectively} \rbrace$ has

- (A) 15 states
- (B) 11 states
- (C) 10 states
- (D) 9 states

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Track (number of 0s mod 3, number of 1s mod 5). That is $3 \times 5 = 15$ states, with start state $(0,0)$, which is also the only accepting state.

All 15 are reachable (read $0^i1^j$). They are pairwise distinguishable: if two states differ in the 0-counter or the 1-counter, adding the right number of 0s or 1s sends exactly one of them to $(0,0)$. So no two states merge, and the minimum is $15$: (A).

- (B), (C), (D) would require merging states that the argument above shows are distinguishable.

**Code check:** Python: built the 15-state product automaton and ran Moore minimisation; it stays at 15 classes.

**Key idea:** independent modular counters multiply: $\mathbb{Z}_3 \times \mathbb{Z}_5$ gives 15 states, all distinguishable.
