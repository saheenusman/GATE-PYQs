# 2025 CS2 Q60: Minimum DFA for product modulo 7

## Question

Let $\Sigma = \lbrace 1,2,3,4\rbrace$. For $x \in \Sigma^*$, let $prod(x)$ be the product of symbols in $x$ modulo 7. We take $prod(\epsilon) = 1$, where $\epsilon$ is the null string.

For example, $prod(124) = (1 \times 2 \times 4) \bmod 7 = 1$.

Define $L = \lbrace x \in \Sigma^* \mid prod(x) = 2\rbrace$.

The number of states in a minimum state DFA for $L$ is ___________. (Answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**6**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A DFA only needs to remember the **current product modulo 7**.

**Reachable states.** The symbols 1, 2, 3, 4 are all non-zero modulo 7 and 7 is prime, so a product is never $0$. The state is one of $\lbrace 1,2,3,4,5,6\rbrace$:

| Residue | One way to reach it |
|---|---|
| 1 | empty string |
| 2, 3, 4 | the single symbols |
| 5 | $3 \cdot 4 = 12 \equiv 5$ |
| 6 | $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ |

There is **no dead state**, because from every state some string leads to residue 2.

**Distinguishable states.** Take two different residues $r \ne s$. Since 3 is a generator of $\mathbb Z_7^*$, products of symbols from $\lbrace1,2,3,4\rbrace$ reach every non-zero residue, so there is a suffix with product $t \equiv 2 r^{-1}$. After reading it, $r$ goes to 2 (accept) while $s$ goes to $2 s r^{-1} \ne 2$ (reject). So all 6 states are distinguishable.

Running a partition-refinement minimisation by computer gave 6 classes as well.

**Answer: 6 states.**

**Key idea:** the DFA is the multiplication-mod-7 automaton, and the group $\mathbb Z_7^*$ has 6 elements, all reachable and all distinguishable.

</details>
