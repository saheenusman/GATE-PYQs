# 2007 CS Q75: Minimum state automaton equivalent to the given FSA

## Question

(Common data of Q.74; the automaton is shown again.)

![A finite automaton with states q0 (start, not accepting), q1 and q2 (both accepting, drawn with double circles) and q3 (not accepting). q0 has a self-loop on b and goes to q1 on a. q1 has a self-loop on b and goes to q2 on a. q2 has a self-loop on b and goes to q1 on a. q3 goes to q1 on a and to q2 on b, and has no incoming transitions.](../../assets/2007/set1-q75.png)

**Q.75** The minimum state automaton equivalent to the above FSA has the following number of states

- (A) 1
- (B) 2
- (C) 3
- (D) 4

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

1. **Remove unreachable states.** $q_3$ has no incoming edge, so it can never be entered from $q_0$. Remaining: $q_0, q_1, q_2$.
2. **Merge equivalent states.** $q_1$ and $q_2$ are both accepting and both send $a$ and $b$ to states in $\lbrace q_1, q_2 \rbrace$ ($q_1 \xrightarrow{a} q_2$, $q_1 \xrightarrow{b} q_1$, $q_2 \xrightarrow{a} q_1$, $q_2 \xrightarrow{b} q_2$), so they are indistinguishable.
3. $q_0$ is non-accepting, so it is distinguishable from the merged state.

Minimum DFA: $\lbrace q_0 \rbrace$ and $\lbrace q_1, q_2 \rbrace$, i.e. $2$ states: (B).

- (A) 1 state would accept everything or nothing, but the language needs $a$.
- (C) 3 keeps $q_1$ and $q_2$ apart, but they are equivalent.
- (D) 4 keeps the unreachable $q_3$ as well.

**Code check:** Python: reachable states $\lbrace q_0,q_1,q_2 \rbrace$; Moore partition refinement gives 2 classes, $\lbrace q_0 \rbrace$ and $\lbrace q_1,q_2 \rbrace$.

**Key idea:** minimise by dropping unreachable states and merging indistinguishable ones.

</details>
