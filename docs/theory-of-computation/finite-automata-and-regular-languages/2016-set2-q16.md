# 2016 CS2 Q16: States in the minimum DFA for (0+1)*(0+1)(0+1)*

## Question

The number of states in the minimum sized DFA that accepts the language defined by the regular expression

$$(0+1)^*(0+1)(0+1)^*$$

is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**2**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$(0+1)^*(0+1)(0+1)^*$ means "any string with at least one symbol", i.e. $\Sigma^+$.

A DFA needs only two states:

- $q_0$ (start, non-accepting): no symbol read yet;
- $q_1$ (accepting): at least one symbol read, with a self-loop on $0, 1$.

The two states are distinguishable ($\varepsilon$ is rejected, any nonempty string accepted), so $2$ is minimal.

**Key idea:** the language is $\Sigma^+$ (nonempty strings): two states.

</details>
