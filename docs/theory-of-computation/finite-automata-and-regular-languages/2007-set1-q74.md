# 2007 CS Q74: Regular expression for the given automaton

## Question

**Common data for Questions 74, 75.** Consider the following finite state automaton:

![A finite automaton with states q0 (start, not accepting), q1 and q2 (both accepting, drawn with double circles) and q3 (not accepting). q0 has a self-loop on b and goes to q1 on a. q1 has a self-loop on b and goes to q2 on a. q2 has a self-loop on b and goes to q1 on a. q3 goes to q1 on a and to q2 on b, and has no incoming transitions.](../../assets/2007/set1-q74.png)

**Q.74** The language accepted by this automaton is given by the regular expression

- (A) $b^*ab^*ab^*ab^*$
- (B) $(a+b)^*$
- (C) $b^*a(a+b)^*$
- (D) $b^*ab^*ab$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Read the automaton:

- $q_0$ loops on $b$ and moves to $q_1$ on the first $a$.
- $q_1$ and $q_2$ are both accepting. From $q_1$: $b$ stays, $a$ goes to $q_2$. From $q_2$: $b$ stays, $a$ goes to $q_1$. So once the automaton leaves $q_0$ it **never leaves the accepting set** $\lbrace q_1, q_2 \rbrace$.
- $q_3$ has no incoming transition, so it is unreachable.

Hence a string is accepted iff it contains at least one $a$ (the first $a$ takes it out of $q_0$): $b^*a(a+b)^*$: (C).

- (A) needs at least three $a$s; the automaton accepts $a$ alone.
- (B) would accept $\varepsilon$ and $b$, which stay in the non-accepting $q_0$.
- (D) demands exactly two $a$s and a final $b$; the automaton accepts $a$, $aaa$, and strings ending in $a$.

**Code check:** Python: ran the DFA and each regular expression on all binary strings over $\lbrace a,b \rbrace$ of length 0 to 10 (2,047 strings): option (C) had 0 mismatches; (A) 1,706, (B) 11 and (D) 2,000.

**Key idea:** a regular expression for a DFA: once the only non-accepting reachable state is left, every string stays accepted.

</details>
