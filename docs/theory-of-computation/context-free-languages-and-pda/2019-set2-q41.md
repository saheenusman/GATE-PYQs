# 2019 CS2 Q41: Which language over {a, b} is not context-free

## Question

Which one of the following languages over $\Sigma = \lbrace a, b \rbrace$ is NOT context-free?

- (A) $\lbrace ww^R \mid w \in \lbrace a,b \rbrace^* \rbrace$
- (B) $\lbrace w a^n b^n w^R \mid w \in \lbrace a,b \rbrace^*,\ n \ge 0 \rbrace$
- (C) $\lbrace w a^n w^R b^n \mid w \in \lbrace a,b \rbrace^*,\ n \ge 0 \rbrace$
- (D) $\lbrace a^n b^i \mid i \in \lbrace n, 3n, 5n \rbrace,\ n \ge 0 \rbrace$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**(A) Context-free.** Palindromes of even length: $S \to aSa \mid bSb \mid \varepsilon$.

**(B) Context-free.** Nest the $a^n b^n$ block inside the $w \ldots w^R$ match: $S \to aSa \mid bSb \mid T,\ T \to aTb \mid \varepsilon$.

**(D) Context-free.** A union of three CFLs: $a^n b^n$, $a^n b^{3n}$ and $a^n b^{5n}$. CFLs are closed under union.

**(C) Not context-free.** Two independent matchings must both be done with one stack:

- $w$ must be matched against $w^R$ across the block $a^n$.
- $a^n$ must be matched against $b^n$ after $w^R$.

While the stack remembers $w$, it cannot also remember $n$. Once $w$ is popped off, the count of $a^n$ is already gone. This is the intuition. I am relying on the official key and this stack argument here. I have not written out a full pumping-lemma proof.

**Key idea:** a single stack can check one nested match at a time. (C) needs two overlapping ones.

</details>
