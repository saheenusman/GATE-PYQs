# 2016 CS2 Q43: Context-freeness of a^n b^m c^(n+m) and a^n b^n c^(2n)

## Question

Consider the following languages:

$$L_1 = \lbrace a^n b^m c^{n+m} : m, n \ge 1 \rbrace$$
$$L_2 = \lbrace a^n b^n c^{2n} : n \ge 1 \rbrace$$

Which one of the following is TRUE?

- (A) Both $L_1$ and $L_2$ are context-free.
- (B) $L_1$ is context-free while $L_2$ is not context-free.
- (C) $L_2$ is context-free while $L_1$ is not context-free.
- (D) Neither $L_1$ nor $L_2$ is context-free.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**$L_1$ is context-free.** Write $a^n b^m c^{m+n}$ as $a^n\,(b^m c^m)\,c^n$. A grammar:

$$S \to a S c \mid a T c, \qquad T \to b T c \mid b c$$

(a PDA pushes the $a$'s and the $b$'s, then pops one symbol per $c$).

**$L_2$ is not context-free.** It needs three counts tied together ($a$'s, $b$'s, and twice as many $c$'s). By the pumping lemma for CFLs, pumping $v$ and $y$ changes at most two of the three blocks, which breaks the relation between the counts (as for $a^n b^n c^n$).

$L_1$ context-free and $L_2$ not: **(B)**.

**Key idea:** $L_1 = a^n(b^mc^m)c^n$ is nested, so CFL; $L_2$ ties three counts, so not CFL.

</details>
