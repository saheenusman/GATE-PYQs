# 2025 CS2 Q30: Closure properties of three language classes

## Question

Consider the two lists List I and List II given below:

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| (i) Context free languages | (a) Closed under union |
| (ii) Recursive languages | (b) Not closed under complementation |
| (iii) Regular languages | (c) Closed under intersection |

For matching of items in List I with those in List II, which of the following option(s) is/are CORRECT?

- (A) (i) – (a), (ii) – (b), and (iii) – (c)
- (B) (i) – (b), (ii) – (a), and (iii) – (c)
- (C) (i) – (b), (ii) – (c), and (iii) – (a)
- (D) (i) – (a), (ii) – (c), and (iii) – (b)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B and C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

First recall which class has which property.

| Class | Union | Intersection | Complement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular | closed | closed | closed |
| Recursive | closed | closed | closed |
| Context-free | closed | **not closed** | **not closed** |

Property (b), *not closed under complementation*, is satisfied by **only context-free languages**. So (i) must be matched with (b).

Recursive and regular languages are closed under **both** union (a) and intersection (c), so (ii) and (iii) can take (a) and (c) in either order.

| Option | Check |
|---|---|
| A | (ii)-(b): recursive languages are closed under complement, false |
| **B** | (i)-(b), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(c): all true |
| **C** | (i)-(b), (ii)-(c), (iii)-(a): all true |
| D | (iii)-(b): regular languages are closed under complement, false |

**Key idea:** only the context-free class fails complementation. The other two classes are closed under all three operations, so either assignment of (a) and (c) is valid.

</details>
