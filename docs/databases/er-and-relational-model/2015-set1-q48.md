# 2015 CS1 Q48: Minimum relations from an ER model with m:n and 1:n relationships

## Question

Consider an Entity-Relationship (ER) model in which entity sets $E_1$ and $E_2$ are connected by an $m : n$ relationship $R_{12}$. $E_1$ and $E_3$ are connected by a $1 : n$ (1 on the side of $E_1$ and $n$ on the side of $E_3$) relationship $R_{13}$.

$E_1$ has two single-valued attributes $a_{11}$ and $a_{12}$ of which $a_{11}$ is the key attribute. $E_2$ has two single-valued attributes $a_{21}$ and $a_{22}$ of which $a_{21}$ is the key attribute. $E_3$ has two single-valued attributes $a_{31}$ and $a_{32}$ of which $a_{31}$ is the key attribute. The relationships do not have any attributes.

If a relational model is derived from the above ER model, then the minimum number of relations that would be generated if all the relations are in 3NF is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**4**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- Each entity set gives a relation: $E_1(a_{11}, a_{12})$, $E_2(a_{21}, a_{22})$, $E_3(a_{31}, a_{32})$. That is 3.
- $R_{13}$ is $1:n$ with $n$ on the $E_3$ side, so it is **merged** into $E_3$ by adding the foreign key $a_{11}$: $E_3(a_{31}, a_{32}, a_{11})$. No extra relation.
- $R_{12}$ is $m:n$, so it needs its **own** relation $R_{12}(a_{11}, a_{21})$.

Total: $3 + 1 = 4$. All of these are in 3NF (each has a single-attribute key and no transitive dependency).

**Key idea:** 1:n relationships fold into the "many" side; m:n relationships need a separate relation.

</details>
