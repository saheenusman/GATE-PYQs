# 2024 CS2 Q20: Participation of weak and owner entity sets

## Question

In the context of owner and weak entity sets in the ER (Entity-Relationship) data model, which one of the following statements is TRUE?

- (A) The weak entity set MUST have total participation in the identifying relationship
- (B) The owner entity set MUST have total participation in the identifying relationship
- (C) Both weak and owner entity sets MUST have total participation in the identifying relationship
- (D) Neither weak entity set nor owner entity set MUST have total participation in the identifying relationship

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A weak entity set has no key of its own. Each weak entity is identified by the key of its **owner** together with a partial key. So every weak entity must be linked to some owner entity: the weak entity set has **total participation** in the identifying relationship.

An owner entity may exist with no weak entities (for example, an employee with no dependents), so the owner's participation can be partial.

**Why the others fail:** (B) and (C) force the owner to be total, which is not required. (D) ignores the total participation of the weak side.

**Key idea:** a weak entity cannot exist without its owner, so its participation is total.

</details>
