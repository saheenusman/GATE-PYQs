# 2018 CS Q21: Many-to-one relationship with total participation

## Question

In an Entity-Relationship (ER) model, suppose $R$ is a many-to-one relationship from entity set E1 to entity set E2. Assume that E1 and E2 participate totally in $R$ and that the cardinality of E1 is greater than the cardinality of E2.

Which one of the following is true about $R$?

- (A) Every entity in E1 is associated with exactly one entity in E2.
- (B) Some entity in E1 is associated with more than one entity in E2.
- (C) Every entity in E2 is associated with exactly one entity in E1.
- (D) Every entity in E2 is associated with at most one entity in E1.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Many-to-one from E1 to E2** means each E1 entity is related to *at most one* E2 entity. **Total participation of E1** means each E1 entity is related to *at least one*. Together: **exactly one**. So A is true.

**Why the others fail:**
- **B** contradicts "many-to-one": no E1 entity can have two E2 partners.
- **C** and **D:** since $|E1| > |E2|$ and each E1 entity maps to one E2 entity, by the pigeonhole principle some E2 entity is shared by at least two E1 entities. So an E2 entity is *not* always tied to exactly one or at most one E1 entity.

**Key idea:** "many-to-one" restricts the *many* side to a single partner; total participation forces that partner to exist.

</details>
