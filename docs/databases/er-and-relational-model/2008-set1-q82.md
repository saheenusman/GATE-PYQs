# 2008 CS Q82: Minimum number of tables for an ER diagram

## Question

Consider the following ER diagram.

![An ER diagram with three entities in a row. On the left is entity M with attributes M1 (underlined, the key), M2 and M3. M is joined by a double line (total participation) to a relationship R1, and R1 is joined by a line with an arrowhead to the entity P in the middle, which has attributes P1 (underlined) and P2. On the right is the weak entity N (double rectangle) with attributes N1 (dashed underline, the partial key) and N2. N is joined by a double line to the identifying relationship R2 (double diamond), and R2 is joined by a line with an arrowhead to P.](../../assets/2008/set1-q82.png)

The minimum number of tables needed to represent M, N, P, R1, R2 is

- (A) 2
- (B) 3
- (C) 4
- (D) 5

<summary>Answer</summary>

**A** (official key; see the Solution: disputed)

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Status: disputed.** The official key is (A). I get (B). Both views follow.

**Reading the diagram.**

- $R1$: many $M$ to one $P$, and every $M$ takes part in it (total participation).
- $R2$: $N$ is a weak entity identified through $P$ (many $N$ to one $P$), and every $N$ takes part.
- $P$ takes part in neither relationship totally: a $P$ may have no $M$ and no $N$.

**Key's view (2 tables).** $R1$ can be folded into $M$ because it is many-to-one with total participation on $M$: table $\lbrace M1, M2, M3, P1 \rbrace$. The key then merges $N$ and $P$ into one table $\lbrace P1, P2, N1, N2 \rbrace$.

**My view (3 tables).**

- $M$ and $R1$: $\lbrace M1, M2, M3, P1 \rbrace$ (with $P1$ as a foreign key).
- $N$ and $R2$: the weak entity needs the key of its owner, so $\lbrace P1, N1, N2 \rbrace$ with key $(P1, N1)$.
- $P$: $\lbrace P1, P2 \rbrace$.

$P$ cannot be merged into $N$'s table. One $P$ can have **many** $N$'s, so $P2$ would be repeated for each of them; and a $P$ with **no** $N$ (participation is partial) would have no row at all, so its data would be lost. Without the merge, 3 tables are needed.

I record the key (A) and mark the question disputed, since the merge it relies on does not preserve the data.

**Key idea:** an entity can be merged into another's table only if the relationship is one-to-one (or total on the many side); a one-to-many relationship from $P$ to $N$ needs $N$ in its own table.
