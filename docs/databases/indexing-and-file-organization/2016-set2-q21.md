# 2016 CS2 Q21: Why B+ trees are called balanced

## Question

B+ Trees are considered BALANCED because

- (A) the lengths of the paths from the root to all leaf nodes are all equal.
- (B) the lengths of the paths from the root to all leaf nodes differ from each other by at most 1.
- (C) the number of children of any two non-leaf sibling nodes differ by at most 1.
- (D) the number of records in any two leaf nodes differ by at most 1.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A B+ tree is balanced because **every path from the root to a leaf has the same length**: it grows only by splitting the root, which lengthens all paths together. **(A)**.

- (B) is wrong: the lengths are equal, not merely "within 1".
- (C) is wrong: sibling nodes may have different numbers of children (each only has to be at least half full).
- (D) is wrong: leaf nodes only need to be at least half full, so their record counts can differ a lot.

**Key idea:** all leaves are at the same depth.

</details>
