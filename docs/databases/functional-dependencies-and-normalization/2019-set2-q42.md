# 2019 CS2 Q42: Normal forms and decomposition of a relation

## Question

Let the set of functional dependencies $F = \lbrace QR \to S,\ R \to P,\ S \to Q \rbrace$ hold on a relation schema $X = (PQRS)$. $X$ is not in BCNF. Suppose $X$ is decomposed into two schemas $Y$ and $Z$, where $Y = (PR)$ and $Z = (QRS)$.

Consider the two statements given below.

- I. Both $Y$ and $Z$ are in BCNF
- II. Decomposition of $X$ into $Y$ and $Z$ is dependency preserving and lossless

Which of the above statements is/are correct?

- (A) Both I and II
- (B) I only
- (C) II only
- (D) Neither I nor II

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Keys.** $X$ has candidate keys $QR$ and $RS$. $X$ is not in BCNF because $R \to P$ and $S \to Q$ have left sides that are not keys.

**$Y = (PR)$.** Its only dependency is $R \to P$, and $R$ is a key of $Y$. So $Y$ is in BCNF.

**$Z = (QRS)$.** The dependencies on $Z$ are $QR \to S$ and $S \to Q$ (and their consequences). Its keys are $QR$ and $RS$. $S \to Q$ has a left side $S$ that is **not** a key of $Z$ ($S^+ = \lbrace S, Q\rbrace$ misses $R$). So $Z$ is **not** in BCNF. **I is false.**

**II is true.**

| Property | Check |
|---|---|
| Lossless | $Y \cap Z = \lbrace R\rbrace$ and $R^+ = \lbrace R, P\rbrace \supseteq Y$, so $R$ is a key of $Y$ |
| Dependency preserving | $R \to P$ lives in $Y$. $QR \to S$ and $S \to Q$ live in $Z$. Every original dependency is preserved |

**Code check:** closure computations give keys $QR$ and $RS$ for $X$ and $Z$, a BCNF violation $S \to Q$ in $Z$ only, a lossless join, and dependency preservation.

**Key idea:** BCNF needs every left side to be a key. Lossless means the shared attributes form a key of one part.

</details>
