# 2026 CS2 Q42: Armstrong's axioms needed to prove the union rule

## Question

Armstrong's axioms:

- Reflexivity: if $Y \subseteq X$, then $X \to Y$.
- Augmentation: if $X \to Y$, then $XZ \to YZ$ for any $Z$.
- Transitivity: if $X \to Y$ and $Y \to Z$, then $X \to Z$.

The additional **Union** rule says: if $X \to Y$ and $X \to Z$, then $X \to YZ$. Which one of the following combinations of the three rules is both **necessary and sufficient** to prove it?

- (A) Reflexivity, Augmentation, and Transitivity
- (B) Reflexivity and Augmentation
- (C) Transitivity
- (D) Augmentation and Transitivity

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Proof of Union.** Assume $X \to Y$ and $X \to Z$.

1. Augment $X \to Y$ with $X$: $X \to XY$ (since $XX = X$). *(Augmentation)*
2. Augment $X \to Z$ with $Y$: $XY \to YZ$. *(Augmentation)*
3. From 1 and 2 by transitivity: $X \to YZ$. *(Transitivity)*

The proof uses **Augmentation and Transitivity** only. Reflexivity is not used.

**Why each is needed:**

- Without augmentation you cannot get $X \to XY$ or $XY \to YZ$.
- Without transitivity you cannot chain them.

**Why the others fail:**

- **(A)** includes reflexivity, which is not needed, so it is sufficient but not necessary.
- **(B)** has no transitivity, so it cannot chain.
- **(C)** has no augmentation, so it cannot build $XY \to YZ$.

**Key idea:** "necessary and sufficient" means no extra rule and none missing. Here that is exactly augmentation plus transitivity.

</details>
