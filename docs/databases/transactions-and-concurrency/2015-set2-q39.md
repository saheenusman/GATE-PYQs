# 2015 CS2 Q39: Undo and redo lists after a crash with a simple checkpoint

## Question

Consider a simple checkpointing protocol and the following set of operations in the log.

```
(start, T4); (write, T4, y, 2, 3); (start, T1); (commit, T4); (write, T1, z, 5, 7);
(checkpoint);
(start, T2); (write, T2, x, 1, 9); (commit, T2); (start, T3); (write, T3, z, 7, 2);
```

If a crash happens now and the system tries to recover using both undo and redo operations, what are the contents of the undo list and the redo list?

- (A) Undo: T3, T1; Redo: T2
- (B) Undo: T3, T1; Redo: T2, T4
- (C) Undo: none; Redo: T2, T4, T3, T1
- (D) Undo: T3, T1, T4; Redo: T2

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Classify transactions by what the log shows at the crash.

| Txn | Committed? | Relative to checkpoint | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| T4 | yes, before the checkpoint | already safely on disk at the checkpoint | nothing |
| T1 | **no** | active at the checkpoint | **undo** |
| T2 | yes, after the checkpoint | started after it | **redo** |
| T3 | **no** | started after it | **undo** |

Undo: T3, T1. Redo: T2. Option (A).

(B) and (D) put T4 in the lists, but T4 committed before the checkpoint, so its effects are already on disk. (C) ignores the commit records entirely.

**Key idea:** at a checkpoint, committed work is flushed; after a crash, undo the uncommitted and redo only transactions that committed after the checkpoint.

</details>
