# 2022 CS Q39: Conflict-equivalent serial schedule

## Question

Let $R_i(z)$ and $W_i(z)$ denote read and write operations on a data element $z$ by a transaction $T_i$, respectively. Consider the schedule $S$ with four transactions.

$S$: $R_4(x)\ R_2(x)\ R_3(x)\ R_1(y)\ W_1(y)\ W_2(x)\ W_3(y)\ R_4(y)$

Which one of the following serial schedules is conflict equivalent to $S$?

- (A) $T_1 \to T_3 \to T_4 \to T_2$
- (B) $T_1 \to T_4 \to T_3 \to T_2$
- (C) $T_4 \to T_1 \to T_3 \to T_2$
- (D) $T_3 \to T_1 \to T_4 \to T_2$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A ($T_1 \to T_3 \to T_4 \to T_2$)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Build the precedence graph** from conflicting pairs (same item, different transactions, at least one write), ordered as in $S$.

On $x$: reads by $T_4, T_2, T_3$ come before $W_2(x)$, giving $T_4 \to T_2$ and $T_3 \to T_2$.

On $y$: the order is $R_1, W_1, W_3, R_4$, giving $T_1 \to T_3$, $T_1 \to T_4$ ($W_1$ before $R_4$) and $T_3 \to T_4$ ($W_3$ before $R_4$).

Edges: $T_1 \to T_3,\ T_1 \to T_4,\ T_3 \to T_4,\ T_3 \to T_2,\ T_4 \to T_2$. No cycle, so $S$ is conflict serializable.

**Topological order.** $T_1$ has no incoming edge, then $T_3$, then $T_4$ (needs $T_1$ and $T_3$), then $T_2$:

$$T_1 \to T_3 \to T_4 \to T_2$$

This is the only valid order, which is option A.

**Why the others fail:** (B) puts $T_4$ before $T_3$, breaking $T_3 \to T_4$. (C) puts $T_4$ before $T_1$, breaking $T_1 \to T_4$. (D) puts $T_3$ before $T_1$, breaking $T_1 \to T_3$.

**Code check:** a conflict-graph builder gave the same edges and the single topological order.

**Key idea:** a serial schedule is conflict equivalent iff it is a topological order of the precedence graph.

</details>
