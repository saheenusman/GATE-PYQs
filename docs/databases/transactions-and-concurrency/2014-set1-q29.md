# 2014 CS1 Q29: Which schedule is conflict serializable

## Question

Consider the following four schedules due to three transactions (indicated by the subscript) using *read* and *write* on a data item `x`, denoted by `r(x)` and `w(x)` respectively. Which one of them is conflict serializable?

- (A) `r1(x); r2(x); w1(x); r3(x); w2(x)`
- (B) `r2(x); r1(x); w2(x); r3(x); w1(x)`
- (C) `r3(x); r2(x); r1(x); w2(x); w1(x)`
- (D) `r2(x); w2(x); r3(x); r1(x); w1(x)`

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Build the precedence graph: draw $T_i \to T_j$ when an operation of $T_i$ conflicts with and comes before an operation of $T_j$ (different transactions, same item, at least one write). The schedule is conflict serializable iff the graph has no cycle.

| Schedule | Key edges | Cycle? |
|---|---|---|
| (A) | $r_2 \to w_1$ gives $T_2 \to T_1$; $r_1 \to w_2$ gives $T_1 \to T_2$ | yes |
| (B) | $r_1 \to w_2$ gives $T_1 \to T_2$; $w_2 \to w_1$ gives $T_2 \to T_1$ | yes |
| (C) | $r_1 \to w_2$ gives $T_1 \to T_2$; $w_2 \to w_1$ gives $T_2 \to T_1$ | yes |
| (D) | $T_2 \to T_3$, $T_2 \to T_1$, $T_3 \to T_1$ | **no** |

(D) is equivalent to the serial order $T_2, T_3, T_1$.

**Code check:** a precedence-graph cycle test returns serializable only for (D).

**Key idea:** conflict serializable iff the precedence graph is acyclic.

</details>
