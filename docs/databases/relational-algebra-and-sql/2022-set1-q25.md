# 2022 CS Q25: Relational algebra: owners of all brands

## Question

Consider the following three relations in a relational database.

$Employee(\underline{eId}, Name)$, $Brand(\underline{bId}, bName)$, $Own(\underline{eId}, \underline{bId})$

Which of the following relational algebra expressions return the set of $eId$s who own all the brands?

- (A) $\Pi_{eId}\big(\Pi_{eId,bId}(Own) \,/\, \Pi_{bId}(Brand)\big)$
- (B) $\Pi_{eId}(Own) - \Pi_{eId}\big((\Pi_{eId}(Own) \times \Pi_{bId}(Brand)) - \Pi_{eId,bId}(Own)\big)$
- (C) $\Pi_{eId}\big(\Pi_{eId,bId}(Own) \,/\, \Pi_{bId}(Own)\big)$
- (D) $\Pi_{eId}\big((\Pi_{eId}(Own) \times \Pi_{bId}(Own)) \,/\, \Pi_{bId}(Brand)\big)$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A and B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**The target:** the $eId$s paired in $Own$ with *every* $bId$ in $Brand$.

- **(A) Correct.** This is the textbook division: $Own$ divided by the set of all brands.
- **(B) Correct.** $\Pi_{eId}(Own) \times \Pi_{bId}(Brand)$ lists every (owner, brand) pair that could exist. Subtracting the real pairs leaves the missing ones. Their $eId$s are the owners who lack some brand. Removing these from all owners leaves exactly those who own every brand.
- **(C) Wrong.** It divides by the brands that *someone* owns, not by all brands. If a brand is owned by nobody, an employee owning all *owned* brands is returned even though he does not own all brands.
- **(D) Wrong.** The cross product $\Pi_{eId}(Own) \times \Pi_{bId}(Own)$ contains every pair of an owner with an owned brand, whether or not that owner owns it. Dividing by $Brand$ then returns every owner whenever every brand is owned by someone. It ignores who owns what.

**Code check:** on 20,000 random relations (including brands owned by nobody and owned ids not in $Brand$), A and B always equalled the true answer; C and D did not.

**Key idea:** "for all" in relational algebra is either division by the full set or "all minus those with some missing pair".

</details>
