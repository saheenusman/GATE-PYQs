# 2012 CS Q50: Theta join of a union with a relation

## Question

**Common data for Questions 50 and 51.** Consider the following relations A, B and C:

**A**

| Id | Name | Age |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Arun | 60 |
| 15 | Shreya | 24 |
| 99 | Rohit | 11 |

**B**

| Id | Name | Age |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Shreya | 24 |
| 25 | Hari | 40 |
| 98 | Rohit | 20 |
| 99 | Rohit | 11 |

**C**

| Id | Phone | Area |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 2200 | 02 |
| 99 | 2100 | 01 |

How many tuples does the result of the following relational algebra expression contain? Assume that the schema of $A \cup B$ is the same as that of $A$.

$$(A \cup B) \bowtie_{A.Id > 40 \,\vee\, C.Id < 15} C$$

- (A) 7
- (B) 4
- (C) 5
- (D) 9

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Union.** $A \cup B$ removes duplicates. $(15, \text{Shreya}, 24)$ and $(99, \text{Rohit}, 11)$ appear in both, so the union has $3 + 4 - 2 = 5$ tuples, with Ids $12, 15, 25, 98, 99$.

**Join.** The condition is a theta join: each union tuple $u$ is paired with each $C$ tuple $c$ when $u.Id > 40$ or $c.Id < 15$.

| Union tuples | Pairs with $c = (10, \dots)$ | Pairs with $c = (99, \dots)$ | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ids 98, 99 ($> 40$) | both | both | 4 |
| Ids 12, 15, 25 | all (since $10 < 15$) | none | 3 |

$$4 + 3 = 7.$$

This is (A).

- (B) $4$ counts only the first row of the table.
- (C) $5$ is $|A \cup B|$, as if the join were a lookup.
- (D) $9$ does not come from the condition; the cross product has only $5 \times 2 = 10$ pairs.

**Code check:** Python sets gave $|A \cup B| = 5$ and $7$ qualifying pairs.

**Key idea:** a theta join keeps exactly the pairs from the cross product that satisfy the condition.

</details>
