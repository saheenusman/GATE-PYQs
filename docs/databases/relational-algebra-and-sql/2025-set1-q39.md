# 2025 CS1 Q39: Tuple relational calculus for players of team MI

## Question

Consider two relations describing $teams$ and $players$ in a sports league:

- $teams(tid, tname)$: $tid$, $tname$ are team-id and team-name, respectively
- $players(pid, pname, tid)$: $pid$, $pname$, and $tid$ denote player-id, player-name and the team-id of the player, respectively

Which ONE of the following tuple relational calculus queries returns the name of the players who play for the team having $tname$ as $'MI'$?

- (A) $\lbrace p.pname \mid p \in players \wedge \exists t\,(t \in teams \wedge p.tid = t.tid \wedge t.tname = 'MI')\rbrace$
- (B) $\lbrace p.pname \mid p \in teams \wedge \exists t\,(t \in players \wedge p.tid = t.tid \wedge t.tname = 'MI')\rbrace$
- (C) $\lbrace p.pname \mid p \in players \wedge \exists t\,(t \in teams \wedge t.tname = 'MI')\rbrace$
- (D) $\lbrace p.pname \mid p \in teams \wedge \exists t\,(t \in players \wedge t.tname = 'MI')\rbrace$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The query needs: a player $p$, a team $t$ **linked to** that player by $p.tid = t.tid$, and the team's name equal to 'MI'.

| Option | Problem |
|---|---|
| **A** | Correct: $p$ ranges over *players*, $t$ over *teams*, they are joined on $tid$, and $t.tname = 'MI'$ |
| B | $p \in teams$, but $pname$ is an attribute of $players$ not $teams$. Also $t \in players$ has no $tname$ |
| C | **No join condition** between $p$ and $t$. If any team is named MI, **every** player is returned |
| D | Same wrong relations as B, and no join condition |

**Key idea:** every tuple variable must range over the relation that has the attributes used, and the existential variable must be tied to the outer one by a join condition.

</details>
