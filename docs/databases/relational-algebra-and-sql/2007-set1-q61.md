# 2007 CS Q61: Employees earning more than everyone in department 5

## Question

Consider the table $employee(empId, name, department, salary)$ and the two queries $Q_1$, $Q_2$ below. Assuming that department 5 has more than one employee, and we want to find the employees who get higher salary than anyone in the department 5, which one of the statements is **TRUE** for any arbitrary employee table?

```sql
Q1: Select e.empId
    From employee e
    Where not exists
      (Select * From employee s
       Where s.department = "5" and s.salary >= e.salary)

Q2: Select e.empId
    From employee e
    Where e.salary > Any
      (Select distinct salary From employee s
       Where s.department = "5")
```

- (A) $Q_1$ is the correct query.
- (B) $Q_2$ is the correct query.
- (C) Both $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ produce the same answer.
- (D) Neither $Q_1$ nor $Q_2$ is the correct query.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The target: employees whose salary is **strictly greater than every** salary in department 5.

- **$Q_1$:** keeps $e$ only if there is no department-5 employee with salary $\ge e.salary$. That is exactly "$e.salary$ exceeds all department-5 salaries". Correct. (An employee of department 5 is excluded, as they should be, because they are their own witness.)
- **$Q_2$:** `> Any` means greater than **at least one** value in the subquery, i.e. greater than the **minimum** department-5 salary. That returns many more employees (everyone above the lowest salary in the department), so it is wrong.

So $Q_1$ is correct and $Q_2$ is not: (A).

- (B) and (C) fail because $Q_2$ means "some" where "all" is needed.
- (D) is wrong since $Q_1$ is correct.

**Code check:** SQLite on random employee tables (department 5 with at least two employees): $Q_1$ always returned exactly the employees with a salary higher than every department-5 salary (0 mismatches); $Q_2$ returned a different set on 1,190 of the tables. (SQLite has no `ANY`, so $Q_2$ was run as `> (select min(salary) ...)`, which is what `> ANY` means.)

**Status note:** `unverified`. GATE 2007 had no official answer key, so there is nothing to compare against. This is my own answer.

**Key idea:** `> ANY` is a comparison with the minimum, `> ALL` with the maximum; "not exists ... >=" expresses "greater than all".

</details>
