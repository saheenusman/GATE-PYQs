# 2014 CS3 Q21: Optimising a cascade of projections and selections

## Question

What is the optimized version of the relation algebra expression $\pi_{A1}(\pi_{A2}(\sigma_{F1}(\sigma_{F2}(r))))$, where $A1, A2$ are sets of attributes in $r$ with $A1 \subset A2$ and $F1, F2$ are Boolean expressions based on the attributes in $r$?

- (A) $\pi_{A1}(\sigma_{(F1 \wedge F2)}(r))$
- (B) $\pi_{A1}(\sigma_{(F1 \vee F2)}(r))$
- (C) $\pi_{A2}(\sigma_{(F1 \wedge F2)}(r))$
- (D) $\pi_{A2}(\sigma_{(F1 \vee F2)}(r))$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Two standard equivalences:

1. **Cascade of selections:** $\sigma_{F1}(\sigma_{F2}(r)) = \sigma_{F1 \wedge F2}(r)$. A tuple passes both selections exactly when it satisfies both conditions.
2. **Cascade of projections:** $\pi_{A1}(\pi_{A2}(x)) = \pi_{A1}(x)$ when $A1 \subseteq A2$. The inner projection to the larger set is redundant.

Together: $\pi_{A1}(\sigma_{F1 \wedge F2}(r))$: **A**.

- (B) and (D) use $\vee$, which keeps tuples satisfying only one condition.
- (C) and (D) output $A2$ instead of $A1$, so the result has more columns than the original.

**Key idea:** merge cascaded selections with $\wedge$ and keep only the outer projection.

</details>
