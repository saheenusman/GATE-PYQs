# 2024 CS2 Q45: Cross products needed to find cities with at least 3 persons

## Question

The relation schema, Person(pid, city), describes the city of residence for every person uniquely identified by pid. The following relational algebra operators are available: selection, projection, cross product, and rename.

To find the list of cities where at least 3 persons reside, using the above operators, the minimum number of cross product operations that must be used is

- (A) 1
- (B) 2
- (C) 3
- (D) 4

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B (2)**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

To decide that a city has at least 3 persons, we must see **three different rows** of `Person` side by side. A selection can compare only attributes of one combined row, so we need three copies.

**Plan:**

$$\pi_{city}\Big(\sigma_{\,c_1 = c_2 = c_3 \;\wedge\; p_1 \ne p_2 \;\wedge\; p_1 \ne p_3 \;\wedge\; p_2 \ne p_3}\big(\rho_1(Person) \times \rho_2(Person) \times \rho_3(Person)\big)\Big)$$

Three copies joined by two cross products. With only **one** cross product we can compare two persons, which shows only "at least 2".

**Key idea:** "at least $k$" needs $k$ copies of the relation, so $k - 1$ cross products.

</details>
