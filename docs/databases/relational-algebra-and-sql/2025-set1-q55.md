# 2025 CS1 Q55: Minimum age of players of Mark's team

## Question

Consider the following database tables of a sports league.

$player(pid, pname, age)$, $team(tid, tname, city, cid)$, $coach(cid, cname)$, $members(pid, tid)$

An instance of the table and an SQL query are given.

**player**

| pid | pname | age |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jasprit | 31 |
| 2 | Atharva | 24 |
| 3 | Ishan | 26 |
| 4 | Axar | 30 |

**coach**

| cid | cname |
|---|---|
| 101 | Ricky |
| 102 | Mark |
| 103 | Trevor |

**team**

| tid | tname | city | cid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | MI | Mumbai | 102 |
| 20 | DC | Delhi | 101 |
| 30 | PK | Mohali | 103 |

**members**

| pid | tid |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 30 |
| 3 | 10 |
| 4 | 20 |

```sql
SELECT MIN(P.age)
FROM player P
WHERE P.pid IN (
    SELECT M.pid
    FROM team T, coach C, members M
    WHERE C.cname = 'Mark'
          AND T.cid = C.cid
          AND M.tid = T.tid
    )
```

The value returned by the given SQL query is ______ . (Answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**26**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Evaluate the inner query first.

1. $C.cname = 'Mark'$ selects coach $cid = 102$.
2. $T.cid = C.cid$ selects the team with $cid = 102$: $tid = 10$ (MI).
3. $M.tid = T.tid$ selects the members of team 10: $pid = 1$ and $pid = 3$.

The inner query returns $\lbrace 1, 3\rbrace$.

The outer query takes the players with these ids: Jasprit (age 31) and Ishan (age 26). Then

$$\text{MIN}(31, 26) = 26.$$

**Key idea:** follow the join chain coach $\to$ team $\to$ members to get the player ids, then take the minimum age over those players.

</details>
