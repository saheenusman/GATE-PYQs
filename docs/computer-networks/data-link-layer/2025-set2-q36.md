# 2025 CS2 Q36: Channel utilisation of Stop-and-Wait

## Question

Suppose we are transmitting frames between two nodes using Stop-and-Wait protocol. The frame size is 3000 bits. The transmission rate of the channel is 2000 bps (bits/second) and the propagation delay between the two nodes is 100 milliseconds. Assume that the processing times at the source and destination are negligible. Also, assume that the size of the acknowledgement packet is negligible.

Which ONE of the following most accurately gives the channel utilization for the above scenario in percentage?

- (A) 88.23
- (B) 93.75
- (C) 85.44
- (D) 66.67

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Per frame the sender is busy transmitting for

$$T_t = \frac{3000}{2000} = 1.5 \text{ s},$$

then waits for the acknowledgement to come back, which takes a round trip: $2T_p = 2 \times 0.1 = 0.2$ s. (The ACK transmission time is negligible.)

$$\text{Utilisation} = \frac{T_t}{T_t + 2T_p} = \frac{1.5}{1.5 + 0.2} = \frac{1.5}{1.7} \approx 0.8824 = 88.24\%.$$

This matches option A (88.23).

| Option | Mistake |
|---|---|
| B, 93.75 | Uses $T_p$ instead of $2T_p$ in the denominator: $1.5/(1.5+0.1)$ |
| C, D | Do not follow from this formula |

**Key idea:** Stop-and-Wait utilisation $= \dfrac{T_t}{T_t + 2T_p}$.

</details>
