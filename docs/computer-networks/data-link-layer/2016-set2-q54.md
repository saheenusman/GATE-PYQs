# 2016 CS2 Q54: Statements about the IEEE 802.11 MAC protocol

## Question

For the IEEE 802.11 MAC protocol for wireless communication, which of the following statements is/are TRUE?

- I. At least three non-overlapping channels are available for transmissions.
- II. The RTS-CTS mechanism is used for collision detection.
- III. Unicast frames are ACKed.

- (A) All I, II, and III
- (B) I and III only
- (C) II and III only
- (D) II only

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

- **I true.** In the 2.4 GHz band, channels 1, 6 and 11 do not overlap, so at least three non-overlapping channels exist.
- **II false.** RTS-CTS is used to reserve the channel and handle the *hidden terminal* problem (collision **avoidance**). 802.11 does not detect collisions, because a wireless station cannot listen while it transmits.
- **III true.** Unicast data frames are acknowledged by the receiver; a missing ACK triggers a retransmission.

I and III only: **(B)**.

**Key idea:** 802.11 has ≥3 non-overlapping channels and ACKs unicast frames; RTS/CTS avoids collisions, it does not detect them.

</details>
