# 2016 CS2 Q23: Key needed to verify a digital signature

## Question

Anarkali digitally signs a message and sends it to Salim. Verification of the signature by Salim requires

- (A) Anarkali's public key.
- (B) Salim's public key.
- (C) Salim's private key.
- (D) Anarkali's private key.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Anarkali signs with **her private key**. Anyone can then verify with **her public key**, which proves that only she could have signed. **(A)**.

(B) and (C) involve Salim's keys, which play no part in verifying her signature. (D) Salim does not have Anarkali's private key.

**Key idea:** the signer's public key verifies the signature.

</details>
