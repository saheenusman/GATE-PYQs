# 2014 CS2 Q25: Maximum TTL when the datagram reaches R

## Question

In the diagram shown below, L1 is an Ethernet LAN and L2 is a Token-Ring LAN. An IP packet originates from sender S and traverses to R, as shown. The links within each ISP and across the two ISPs, are all point-to-point optical links. The initial value of the TTL field is 32. The maximum possible value of the TTL field when R receives the datagram is ____________.

![Network diagram. Sender S, drawn as a triangle, sits on Ethernet LAN L1 with two more triangle devices on separate bus segments and one square device at the edge of L1. ISP1 contains two square devices joined by point-to-point links, and ISP2 contains two more. The last square connects to Token Ring LAN L2, a ring of three circles, one of which is R. Arrows trace the path from S to R.](../../assets/2014/set2-q25.png)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**26** (official key)

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Disputed.** The official key is **26**. My count from the figure gives a different number, and the figure has no legend, so I have not picked one.

TTL is reduced by 1 at each **router**. Hubs, bridges and ring stations do not touch it.

**My reading.** The squares are the routers on the path:

| Where | Routers |
|---|---|
| Edge of L1 | 1 |
| ISP1 | 2 |
| ISP2 | 2 |
| **Total** | **5** |

The triangles in L1 and the circles in L2 sit inside LANs, so I treat them as layer-1/2 devices and hosts. That gives $32 - 5 = 27$.

**The key.** 26 needs 6 decrements, one more than the 5 squares. The question does not say what the symbols mean, and I could not find which additional device the key counts as a router.

Both numbers rest on how the unlabelled symbols are read. Treating the triangles as routers would give a lower TTL still, so the "maximum possible" wording points to the smallest router count the figure allows.

**Key idea:** TTL falls by 1 at every router hop; devices within a LAN do not change it.

</details>
