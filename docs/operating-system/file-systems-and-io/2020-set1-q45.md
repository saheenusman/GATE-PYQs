# 2020 CS Q45: The false statement about SSTF disk scheduling

## Question

Consider the following five disk access requests of the form (request id, cylinder number) that are present in the disk scheduler queue at a given time.

(P, 155), (Q, 85), (R, 110), (S, 30), (T, 115)

Assume the head is positioned at cylinder 100. The scheduler follows Shortest Seek Time First scheduling to service the requests.

Which one of the following statements is FALSE?

- (A) T is serviced before P.
- (B) Q is serviced after S, but before T.
- (C) The head reverses its direction of movement between servicing of Q and P.
- (D) R is serviced before P.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**SSTF** always services the pending request nearest to the current head position.

| Step | Head at | Distances to pending requests | Serve | Seek |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | P 55, Q 15, **R 10**, S 70, T 15 | R (110) | 10 |
| 2 | 110 | P 45, Q 25, S 80, **T 5** | T (115) | 5 |
| 3 | 115 | P 40, **Q 30**, S 85 | Q (85) | 30 |
| 4 | 85 | P 70, **S 55** | S (30) | 55 |
| 5 | 30 | P 125 | P (155) | 125 |

Service order: **R, T, Q, S, P**. I confirmed this with a short simulation.

**Check each statement:**

- **(A)** T before P: T is 2nd and P is 5th. True.
- **(B)** Q after S but before T: Q is 3rd, S is 4th, T is 2nd. Q is serviced *after T* and *before S*. **False.**
- **(C)** Between Q and P the head goes 85 down to 30 (S), then up to 155 (P), so it reverses direction. True.
- **(D)** R before P: R is 1st. True.

The false statement is **(B)**.

**Key idea:** SSTF is greedy by distance from the *current* head position, so the head can jump back and forth. Re-measure the distances after every service.

</details>
