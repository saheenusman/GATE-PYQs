# 2013 CS Q34: Maximum value of x with a counting semaphore

## Question

A shared variable $x$, initialized to zero, is operated on by four concurrent processes $W$, $X$, $Y$, $Z$ as follows. Each of the processes $W$ and $X$ reads $x$ from memory, increments by one, stores it to memory, and then terminates. Each of the processes $Y$ and $Z$ reads $x$ from memory, decrements by two, stores it to memory, and then terminates. Each process before reading $x$ invokes the $P$ operation (i.e., *wait*) on a counting semaphore $S$ and invokes the $V$ operation (i.e., *signal*) on the semaphore $S$ after storing $x$ to memory. Semaphore $S$ is initialized to two. What is the maximum possible value of $x$ after all processes complete execution?

- (A) $-2$
- (B) $-1$
- (C) $1$
- (D) $2$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$S = 2$ lets two processes into the critical section at once, so updates can be lost (a read-modify-write race).

**A schedule that ends at $x = 2$:**

| Step | Event | $x$ | $S$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $W$: P, reads 0 | 0 | 1 |
| 2 | $Y$: P, reads 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | $Y$ writes $-2$, V | $-2$ | 1 |
| 4 | $Z$: P, reads $-2$, writes $-4$, V | $-4$ | 1 |
| 5 | $W$ writes $0+1$ (stale read), V | 1 | 2 |
| 6 | $X$: P, reads 1, writes 2, V | **2** | 2 |

$Y$'s and $Z$'s decrements are wiped out by $W$'s stale write, and $X$ then adds 1 more.

**Why not higher.** The largest possible total from $W$ and $X$ is $+2$, and $Y$ and $Z$ can only lower $x$ or have their effect overwritten. So $2$ is the maximum.

**Why the others fail:** (A), (B), (C) are reachable values but not the maximum.

**Code check:** a state-space search over all interleavings gave reachable final values $\lbrace -4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2 \rbrace$, so the maximum is 2.

**Key idea:** a counting semaphore of 2 allows two concurrent writers; lost updates decide the extremes.

</details>
