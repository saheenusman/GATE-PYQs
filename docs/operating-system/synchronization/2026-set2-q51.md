# 2026 CS2 Q51: Possible outputs of three processes with semaphores

## Question

Three processes P1, P2 and P3 run identical code. $A$ and $B$ are binary semaphores initialised to 1 and 0. $X$ is a shared variable initialised to 0. Each line executes atomically.

```text
Wait(A);
Print(*);
X = X + 1;
If (X == 2) {
    Print($);
    Signal(B);
}
Signal(A);
Wait(B);
Print(#);
Signal(B);
```

Context switching can happen at any time and in any order. Which of the following patterns is/are possible outputs?

- (A) `**$*###`
- (B) `**$#*##`
- (C) `**$##*#`
- (D) `***$###`

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A, B and C**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Step 1: who prints `$`?** $A$ works as a lock around the first block. Each process does `Print(*)` and `X = X+1` while holding $A$. The `$` is printed by the process that makes $X$ equal 2, which is the **second** process through the lock.

So exactly **two stars** appear before `$`, giving the prefix `**$`.

**Step 2: who prints `#`?** $B$ starts at 0. Every `#` needs `Wait(B)`, and the only way to get $B$ above 0 is the `Signal(B)` after `$`. After that, each process that prints `#` does `Signal(B)` and lets the next one through. So all three `#` come **after** `$`.

**Step 3: where does the third `*` go?** The third process prints its `*` once $A$ is free (after the second process's `Signal(A)`), and its own `#` comes after its own `*`.

After `**$`, the remaining output is three `#` and one `*`, with the `*` before the last `#`:

| Position of `*` | Pattern |
|---|---|
| before all `#` | `**$*###` (A) |
| after the first `#` | `**$#*##` (B) |
| after the second `#` | `**$##*#` (C) |
| after the third `#` | impossible: the third process prints `*` before its own `#` |

**(D) `***$###` is impossible.** `$` is printed as soon as the second process increments $X$, before any third `*`.

**Verified.** I explored every interleaving of the three processes in Python (each line atomic). The only reachable outputs are exactly the three patterns above.

**Key idea:** `$` marks the second entry, and the `#` lines are gated by $B$, which only opens after `$`.

</details>
