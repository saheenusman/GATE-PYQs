# 2012 CS Q42: Page faults under LRU, FIFO and optimal replacement with 3 frames

## Question

Consider the virtual page reference string

$$1,\ 2,\ 3,\ 2,\ 4,\ 1,\ 3,\ 2,\ 4,\ 1$$

on a demand paged virtual memory system running on a computer system that has main memory size of $3$ page frames which are initially empty. Let LRU, FIFO and OPTIMAL denote the number of page faults under the corresponding page replacement policy. Then

- (A) OPTIMAL $<$ LRU $<$ FIFO
- (B) OPTIMAL $<$ FIFO $<$ LRU
- (C) OPTIMAL $=$ LRU
- (D) OPTIMAL $=$ FIFO

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Faults are marked F, hits H.

| Ref | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 1 | Faults |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FIFO** | F | F | F | H | F | F | H | F | H | H | **6** |
| **LRU** | F | F | F | H | F | F | F | F | F | F | **9** |
| **OPT** | F | F | F | H | F | H | H | F | H | H | **5** |

**FIFO** (evict oldest): frames $\lbrace 1,2,3 \rbrace$; page 4 evicts 1; page 1 evicts 2; page 2 evicts 3.

**LRU** (evict least recently used): after the first hit, every later reference is a fault, because the reference pattern is cyclic over 4 pages with 3 frames.

**OPT** (evict the page used farthest ahead): page 4 evicts 2; page 2 later evicts 3; everything else hits.

So $5 < 6 < 9$: OPTIMAL $<$ FIFO $<$ LRU, which is (B).

- (A) has LRU better than FIFO; here LRU is worse.
- (C), (D) need equal counts, but $5 \ne 9$ and $5 \ne 6$.

**Code check:** simulators for FIFO, LRU and OPT gave 6, 9 and 5 faults.

**Key idea:** LRU is not always better than FIFO; on cyclic patterns slightly larger than memory it can be the worst.

</details>
