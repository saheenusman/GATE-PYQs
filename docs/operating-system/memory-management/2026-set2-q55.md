# 2026 CS2 Q55: Best-fit allocation and remaining small holes

## Question

Contiguous allocation of memory uses variable partitioning. There are 8 holes of sizes 20 KB, 4 KB, 25 KB, 18 KB, 7 KB, 9 KB, 15 KB and 12 KB. No two holes are adjacent. Processes P1 (16 KB) and P2 (9 KB) arrive in that order and are placed by the **best-fit** technique.

After allocating P1 and P2, the number of holes of size less than 8 KB is _____. (answer in integer)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Refresher.** Best fit picks the **smallest** hole that is big enough.

**P1 (16 KB).** Holes $\ge 16$: 20, 25, 18. The smallest is **18 KB**. Remainder: $18 - 16 = 2$ KB.

Holes now: 20, 4, 25, **2**, 7, 9, 15, 12.

**P2 (9 KB).** Holes $\ge 9$: 20, 25, 9, 15, 12. The smallest is **9 KB**, an exact fit, so that hole disappears.

Holes now: 20, 4, 25, 2, 7, 15, 12.

**Holes smaller than 8 KB:** 4, 2 and 7. That is **3**.

**Verified** by simulating best-fit in Python.

**Key idea:** best fit leaves the smallest possible remainder, and an exact fit removes the hole entirely.

</details>
