# 2014 CS3 Q20: LRU page faults with 3 frames

## Question

A system uses 3 page frames for storing process pages in main memory. It uses the Least Recently Used (**LRU**) page replacement policy. Assume that all the page frames are initially empty. What is the total number of page faults that will occur while processing the page reference string given below?

4, 7, 6, 1, 7, 6, 1, 2, 7, 2

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**6**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Frames are shown oldest to newest (the oldest is the LRU page, which is evicted on a fault).

| Ref | Frames after | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 4 | fault |
| 7 | 4 7 | fault |
| 6 | 4 7 6 | fault |
| 1 | 7 6 1 | fault (evict 4) |
| 7 | 6 1 7 | hit |
| 6 | 1 7 6 | hit |
| 1 | 7 6 1 | hit |
| 2 | 6 1 2 | fault (evict 7) |
| 7 | 1 2 7 | fault (evict 6) |
| 2 | 1 7 2 | hit |

Faults: 4, 7, 6, 1, 2, 7: a total of **6**.

**Code check:** an LRU simulation gives 6 faults, with the same trace.

**Key idea:** on a fault, evict the page whose last use is oldest; a hit refreshes the page's recency.

</details>
