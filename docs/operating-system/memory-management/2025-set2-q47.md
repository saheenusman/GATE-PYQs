# 2025 CS2 Q47: Which policy produced this page-frame trace

## Question

Consider a demand paging system with three frames, and the following page reference string: 1 2 3 4 5 4 1 6 4 5 1 3 2. The contents of the frames are as follows initially and after each reference (from left to right):

| | initially | 1* | 2* | 3* | 4* | 5* | 4 | 1 | 6* | 4 | 5 | 1* | 3* | 2* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| frame 1 | - | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 2 |
| frame 2 | - | - | 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| frame 3 | - | - | - | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 3 |

The $*$-marked references cause page replacements.

Which one or more of the following could be the page replacement policy/policies in use?

- (A) Least Recently Used page replacement policy
- (B) Least Frequently Used page replacement policy
- (C) Most Frequently Used page replacement policy
- (D) Optimal page replacement policy

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D (official key). My analysis finds C consistent as well: see the solution**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Victims read from the table** (page evicted at each replacement):

| Incoming page | 4 | 5 | 6 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frames before | 1, 2, 3 | 1, 4, 3 | 1, 4, 5 | 6, 4, 5 | 6, 1, 5 | 6, 1, 3 |
| Page evicted | **2** | **3** | **1** | **4** | **5** | **6** |

**(A) LRU: no.** When 4 arrives, the least recently used page is 1 (referenced first), but the table evicts 2.

**(B) LFU: no.** When 6 arrives the frames hold 1, 4, 5. Page 1 has been used twice (once to load it, once as a hit), page 4 twice, page 5 once. LFU would evict **5**, but the table evicts 1. This holds under either way of counting.

**(D) Optimal: yes.** Optimal evicts the resident page whose next use is farthest in the future. Number the references 1 to 13 (the string is 1 2 3 4 5 4 1 6 4 5 1 3 2):

| Fault on (position) | Resident pages and their next use | Farthest | Table's victim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (position 4) | 1 at 7, 3 at 12, 2 at 13 | 2 | 2 |
| 5 (position 5) | 4 at 6, 1 at 7, 3 at 12 | 3 | 3 |
| 6 (position 8) | 4 at 9, 5 at 10, 1 at 11 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 (position 11) | 6, 4, 5 are never used again | any | 4 |
| 3 (position 12) | 6, 1, 5 are never used again | any | 5 |
| 2 (position 13) | 6, 1, 3 are never used again | any | 6 |

Every victim in the table is a valid optimal choice. For the last three replacements all the resident pages are equally good, so any of them is allowed.

**(C) MFU: this is where my analysis differs from the key.** MFU evicts the page with the **highest** reference count. Counting references **since the page was brought in**, and allowing any choice among tied counts:

| Fault on | Counts of resident pages | MFU victim set | Table's victim |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 1:1, 2:1, 3:1 | any of 1, 2, 3 | 2 (allowed) |
| 5 | 1:1, 4:1, 3:1 | any of 1, 4, 3 | 3 (allowed) |
| 6 | 1:2, 4:2, 5:1 | 1 or 4 | 1 (allowed) |
| 1 | 6:1, 4:3, 5:2 | 4 | 4 (allowed) |
| 3 | 6:1, 1:1, 5:2 | 5 | 5 (allowed) |
| 2 | 6:1, 1:1, 3:1 | any | 6 (allowed) |

Every victim in the table is allowed, so MFU **is consistent** with the trace. I confirmed this with a simulation. The same simulation shows MFU would **not** fit if reference counts were kept across earlier residencies (then it would evict 1, not 5, at the fault on 3).

**Why this is marked disputed.** The official key lists only (D). The verdict on (C) depends on two conventions the question does not state: how reference counts are kept, and how ties are broken. The question says a policy "could be" in use. Treating ties as free for MFU is the same standard that makes Optimal valid at the last three replacements, where the table's victims are chosen among equally good pages. I am not picking a side: the key says D only, and my analysis finds both C and D consistent.

| Policy | Consistent with the table? |
|---|---|
| LRU | No |
| LFU | No |
| MFU | Yes (counts since page load, free tie-breaking), the point of difference from the key |
| Optimal | Yes |

**Key idea:** test each policy fault by fault against the victims the table shows, and be explicit about tie-breaking and how counts are maintained.
