# 2026 CS1 Q64: Average waiting time under FCFS

## Question

A CPU runs two types of processes. Actuators (A) need a CPU burst of 6 seconds. Controllers (C) need 8 seconds. A new A process arrives at $t = 10, 20, 30, 40, 50$ and a new C process at $t = 11, 22, 33, 44, 55$ (seconds). The scheduling policy is First Come First Serve (FCFS), and the first A process starts at $t = 10$.

The average waiting time (seconds) for the 10 processes is _____. (rounded off to one decimal place)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**9.5**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Refresher.** Under FCFS, processes run in order of arrival. Waiting time = start time $-$ arrival time.

Sort by arrival and run one after another (the CPU never goes idle after $t = 10$):

| Process | Arrival | Start | Burst | Finish | Waiting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 10 | 10 | 6 | 16 | 0 |
| C1 | 11 | 16 | 8 | 24 | 5 |
| A2 | 20 | 24 | 6 | 30 | 4 |
| C2 | 22 | 30 | 8 | 38 | 8 |
| A3 | 30 | 38 | 6 | 44 | 8 |
| C3 | 33 | 44 | 8 | 52 | 11 |
| A4 | 40 | 52 | 6 | 58 | 12 |
| C4 | 44 | 58 | 8 | 66 | 14 |
| A5 | 50 | 66 | 6 | 72 | 16 |
| C5 | 55 | 72 | 8 | 80 | 17 |

Total waiting: $0+5+4+8+8+11+12+14+16+17 = 95$.

$$\text{Average} = \frac{95}{10} = 9.5 \text{ s}.$$

**Verified** by simulating FCFS in Python.

**Key idea:** list the arrivals in order, then each start time is the larger of the arrival time and the previous finish time.

</details>
