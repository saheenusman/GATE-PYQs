# 2021 CS1 Q35: Minimum average waiting time, non-preemptive

## Question

Three processes arrive at time zero with CPU bursts of 16, 20 and 10 milliseconds. If the scheduler has prior knowledge about the length of the CPU bursts, the minimum achievable average waiting time for these three processes in a non-preemptive scheduler (rounded to nearest integer) is ______ milliseconds.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**12**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

With known burst lengths, shortest-job-first minimises the average waiting time.

Order: 10, 16, 20.

| Process (burst) | Starts at | Waits |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0 | 0 |
| 16 | 10 | 10 |
| 20 | 26 | 26 |

Average $=\dfrac{0+10+26}{3}=\mathbf{12}$ ms.

Checked with a script: waits $[0,10,26]$, mean 12.0.

**Key idea:** non-preemptive SJF gives the minimum average waiting time when all arrive together.

</details>
