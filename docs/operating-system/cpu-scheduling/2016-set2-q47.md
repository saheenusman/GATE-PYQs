# 2016 CS2 Q47: Average turnaround time under preemptive shortest remaining time first

## Question

Consider the following processes, with the arrival time and the length of the CPU burst given in milliseconds. The scheduling algorithm used is preemptive shortest remaining-time first.

| Process | Arrival Time | Burst Time |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 0 | 10 |
| P2 | 3 | 6 |
| P3 | 7 | 1 |
| P4 | 8 | 3 |

The average turn around time of these processes is ______ milliseconds.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**8.25**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Gantt chart (SRTF).**

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | P1 runs (7 ms left) |
| 3 | P2 arrives with 6 ms, less than P1's 7, so P2 preempts |
| 3 to 7 | P2 runs (2 ms left) |
| 7 to 8 | P3 arrives with 1 ms, less than 2, so P3 runs and finishes at 8 |
| 8 to 10 | P4 arrives (3 ms), P2 has 2 ms, the smallest, so P2 finishes at 10 |
| 10 to 13 | P4 runs and finishes at 13 |
| 13 to 20 | P1 finishes at 20 |

**Turnaround = completion − arrival.**

| Process | Completion | Arrival | TAT |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 20 | 0 | 20 |
| P2 | 10 | 3 | 7 |
| P3 | 8 | 7 | 1 |
| P4 | 13 | 8 | 5 |

$$\text{Average} = \frac{20 + 7 + 1 + 5}{4} = \frac{33}{4} = 8.25\ \text{ms}$$

**Code check:** a time-stepped SRTF simulation gave the same turnaround times.

**Key idea:** simulate SRTF: completions P1 20, P2 10, P3 8, P4 13; average TAT $= 33/4$.

</details>
