# 2020 CS Q60: Average turnaround time, SJF versus RR

## Question

Consider the following set of processes, assumed to have arrived at time 0. Consider the CPU scheduling algorithms Shortest Job First (SJF) and Round Robin (RR). For RR, assume that the processes are scheduled in the order $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$.

| Process | $P_1$ | $P_2$ | $P_3$ | $P_4$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Burst time (ms) | 8 | 7 | 2 | 4 |

If the time quantum for RR is 4 ms, then the absolute value of the difference between the average turnaround times (in ms) of SJF and RR (round off to 2 decimal places) is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**5.25**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

All processes arrive at time 0, so turnaround time = completion time.

**SJF** (non-preemptive; with equal arrival times it makes no difference). Order by burst: $P_3$ (2), $P_4$ (4), $P_2$ (7), $P_1$ (8).

| Process | Runs | Completion |
|---|---|---|
| $P_3$ | 0 to 2 | 2 |
| $P_4$ | 2 to 6 | 6 |
| $P_2$ | 6 to 13 | 13 |
| $P_1$ | 13 to 21 | 21 |

Average $= (2 + 6 + 13 + 21)/4 = 42/4 = 10.5$ ms.

**RR, quantum 4 ms**, order $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$.

| Time | Runs | Remaining after | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 4 | $P_1$ | 4 | |
| 4 to 8 | $P_2$ | 3 | |
| 8 to 10 | $P_3$ | 0 | done at 10 |
| 10 to 14 | $P_4$ | 0 | done at 14 |
| 14 to 18 | $P_1$ | 0 | done at 18 |
| 18 to 21 | $P_2$ | 0 | done at 21 |

Average $= (18 + 21 + 10 + 14)/4 = 63/4 = 15.75$ ms.

**Difference:** $|10.5 - 15.75| = 5.25$ ms.

I simulated both schedules in code and got the same figures.

**Key idea:** SJF gives the smallest average turnaround time when all jobs arrive together. RR with a small quantum makes long jobs wait through many rounds, which raises the average.

</details>
