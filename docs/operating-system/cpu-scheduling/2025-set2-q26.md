# 2025 CS2 Q26: Average turnaround time under SRTF

## Question

Processes $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$ arrive in that order at times 0, 1, 2, and 8 milliseconds respectively, and have execution times of 10, 13, 6, and 9 milliseconds respectively. Shortest Remaining Time First (SRTF) algorithm is used as the CPU scheduling policy. Ignore context switching times.

Which ONE of the following correctly gives the average turnaround time of the four processes in milliseconds?

- (A) 22
- (B) 15
- (C) 37
- (D) 19

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**D**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Follow the schedule, always running the process with the shortest remaining time.

| Time | Event | Running |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | $P_1$ runs (remaining 10 to 8). At 1, $P_2$ arrives (13), not shorter | $P_1$ |
| 2 | $P_3$ arrives with 6, shorter than $P_1$'s remaining 8: **preempt** | $P_3$ |
| 2 to 8 | $P_3$ finishes at **8**. At 8, $P_4$ arrives (9) | $P_3$ |
| 8 | Ready: $P_1$ (8 left), $P_2$ (13), $P_4$ (9). Shortest is $P_1$ | $P_1$ |
| 8 to 16 | $P_1$ finishes at **16** | $P_1$ |
| 16 to 25 | $P_4$ (9) runs, finishing at **25** | $P_4$ |
| 25 to 38 | $P_2$ (13) runs, finishing at **38** | $P_2$ |

Timeline: $P_1$ (0 to 2), $P_3$ (2 to 8), $P_1$ (8 to 16), $P_4$ (16 to 25), $P_2$ (25 to 38).

Turnaround time = finish time $-$ arrival time.

| Process | Arrival | Finish | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| $P_1$ | 0 | 16 | 16 |
| $P_2$ | 1 | 38 | 37 |
| $P_3$ | 2 | 8 | 6 |
| $P_4$ | 8 | 25 | 17 |

$$\text{average} = \frac{16 + 37 + 6 + 17}{4} = \frac{76}{4} = 19 \text{ ms}.$$

A simulation of SRTF gives the same finish times.

**Key idea:** in SRTF, re-check the shortest remaining time at every arrival, and turnaround = completion $-$ arrival.

</details>
