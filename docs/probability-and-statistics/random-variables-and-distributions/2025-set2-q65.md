# 2025 CS2 Q65: Expected length of the piece containing a fixed point

## Question

The unit interval $(0,1)$ is divided at a point chosen uniformly distributed over $(0,1)$ in $\mathbb{R}$ into two disjoint subintervals.

The expected length of the subinterval that contains 0.4 is ___________. (rounded off to two decimal places)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.74**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $U$ be the uniformly random cut point.

- If $U < 0.4$, the piece containing 0.4 is $(U, 1)$, of length $1 - U$.
- If $U > 0.4$, the piece containing 0.4 is $(0, U)$, of length $U$.

$$E = \int_0^{0.4}(1 - u)\,du + \int_{0.4}^{1} u\,du.$$

First integral: $\left[u - \tfrac{u^2}{2}\right]_0^{0.4} = 0.4 - 0.08 = 0.32$.

Second integral: $\left[\tfrac{u^2}{2}\right]_{0.4}^{1} = 0.5 - 0.08 = 0.42$.

$$E = 0.32 + 0.42 = 0.74.$$

(A symbolic integration also gives $\tfrac{37}{50} = 0.74$.) This lies in the official range 0.70 to 0.80.

Note that the answer is larger than $0.5$: the piece containing a fixed point is longer than an average piece, because longer pieces are more likely to contain the point.

**Key idea:** split on whether the cut falls left or right of the point, and integrate the piece length over each case.

</details>
