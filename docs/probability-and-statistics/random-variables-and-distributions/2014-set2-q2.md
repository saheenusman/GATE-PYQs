# 2014 CS2 Q2: Expected length of a word drawn from 'The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog'

## Question

Each of the nine words in the sentence "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog" is written on a separate piece of paper. These nine pieces of paper are kept in a box. One of the pieces is drawn at random from the box. The *expected* length of the word drawn is _____________. (The answer should be rounded to one decimal place.)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**3.9**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

The word lengths are:

| The | quick | brown | fox | jumps | over | the | lazy | dog |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 |

The total is $35$ over 9 equally likely words:

$$E = \frac{35}{9} \approx 3.89 \approx 3.9$$

**Code check:** `35/9` gives `3.888...`.

**Key idea:** expectation over equally likely outcomes is the plain mean.

</details>
