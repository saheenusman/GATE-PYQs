# 2019 CS2 Q56: Expected distance between two random leaves of a full binary tree

## Question

Let $T$ be a full binary tree with 8 leaves. (A full binary tree has every level full.) Suppose two leaves $a$ and $b$ of $T$ are chosen uniformly and independently at random. The expected value of the distance between $a$ and $b$ in $T$ (i.e., the number of edges in the unique path between $a$ and $b$) is (rounded off to 2 decimal places) ________.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**4.25**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

A full binary tree with 8 leaves has depth 3. The two leaves are chosen **independently**, so $a = b$ is possible (probability $1/8$).

Fix $a$. The distance to $b$ depends on how high up their paths meet. If they meet at height $h$ above the leaves, the distance is $2h$.

| Where $b$ is | Leaves | Probability | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| $b = a$ | 1 | $1/8$ | 0 |
| Sibling of $a$ | 1 | $1/8$ | 2 |
| Under the same grandparent | 2 | $2/8$ | 4 |
| In the other half of the tree | 4 | $4/8$ | 6 |

$$E = \frac{0\cdot 1 + 2\cdot 1 + 4\cdot 2 + 6\cdot 4}{8} = \frac{34}{8} = 4.25.$$

**Code check:** averaging the distance over all 64 ordered pairs gives 4.25.

**Key idea:** use linearity over the cases of where $b$ lies relative to $a$. Distance equals twice the height of the meeting point.

</details>
