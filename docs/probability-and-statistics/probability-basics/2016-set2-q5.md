# 2016 CS2 Q5: Probability that a randomly chosen LED bulb lasts over 100 hours

## Question

Suppose that a shop has an equal number of LED bulbs of two different types. The probability of an LED bulb lasting more than 100 hours given that it is of Type 1 is 0.7, and given that it is of Type 2 is 0.4. The probability that an LED bulb chosen uniformly at random lasts more than 100 hours is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.55**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

By the law of total probability, with $P(T_1) = P(T_2) = 0.5$:

$$P(L) = 0.5 \times 0.7 + 0.5 \times 0.4 = 0.35 + 0.20 = 0.55$$

**Key idea:** law of total probability: $0.5 \times 0.7 + 0.5 \times 0.4$.

</details>
