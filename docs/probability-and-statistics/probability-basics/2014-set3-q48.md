# 2014 CS3 Q48: Maximum of P(A)P(B) for a partition of the sample space

## Question

Let $S$ be a sample space and two mutually exclusive events $A$ and $B$ be such that $A \cup B = S$. If $P(\cdot)$ denotes the probability of the event, the maximum value of $P(A)P(B)$ is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.25**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

$A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive and cover $S$, so $P(A) + P(B) = P(S) = 1$. Let $p = P(A)$. Then

$$P(A)P(B) = p(1 - p).$$

This is maximised at $p = \tfrac12$ (by AM-GM, or by setting the derivative $1 - 2p$ to zero), where it equals $\tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12 = 0.25$.

**Key idea:** for two non-negative numbers with a fixed sum, the product is largest when they are equal.

</details>
