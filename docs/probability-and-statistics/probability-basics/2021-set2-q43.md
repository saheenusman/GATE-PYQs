# 2021 CS2 Q43: Polya urn: red ball on the fourth draw

## Question

A bag has $r$ red balls and $b$ black balls. All balls are identical except for their colours. In a trial, a ball is randomly drawn from the bag, its colour is noted and the ball is placed back into the bag along with another ball of the same colour. Note that the number of balls in the bag will increase by one, after the trial. A sequence of four such trials is conducted. Which one of the following choices gives the probability of drawing a red ball in the fourth trial?

- (A) $\dfrac{r}{r+b}$
- (B) $\dfrac{r}{r+b+3}$
- (C) $\dfrac{r+3}{r+b+3}$
- (D) $\left(\dfrac{r}{r+b}\right)\left(\dfrac{r+1}{r+b+1}\right)\left(\dfrac{r+2}{r+b+2}\right)\left(\dfrac{r+3}{r+b-3}\right)$

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

This is a Pólya urn. The draws are **exchangeable**: the probability of any sequence of colours depends only on how many reds and blacks it has, not on their order. So the probability of red on the 4th trial equals the probability of red on the 1st trial:

$$P=\frac{r}{r+b}.$$

**Check with the second trial:**

$$\frac{r}{r+b}\cdot\frac{r+1}{r+b+1}+\frac{b}{r+b}\cdot\frac{r}{r+b+1}
=\frac{r(r+1+b)}{(r+b)(r+b+1)}=\frac{r}{r+b}.$$

I also computed the 4th-trial probability exactly (fractions, summing over all 8 histories of the first three draws) for $(r,b)=(3,5),(2,7),(4,4)$. Each gives $r/(r+b)$.

- (B), (C): these assume the first three draws were all black or all red.
- (D): this is the shape of the probability of four reds in a row, not of red on trial 4 (and its last denominator, as printed, is $r+b-3$).

**Key idea:** in a Pólya urn the draws are exchangeable, so every trial has the same marginal probability as the first.

</details>
