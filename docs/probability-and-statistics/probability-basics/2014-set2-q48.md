# 2014 CS2 Q48: Probability that a number up to 100 is divisible by none of 2, 3, 5

## Question

The probability that a given positive integer lying between 1 and 100 (both inclusive) is NOT divisible by 2, 3 or 5 is ______.

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.26**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

Let $A$, $B$, $C$ be the sets of multiples of 2, 3, 5 in $1..100$. By inclusion-exclusion:

$$|A \cup B \cup C| = (50 + 33 + 20) - (16 + 10 + 6) + 3 = 103 - 32 + 3 = 74$$

(the pair counts are $\lfloor 100/6 \rfloor = 16$, $\lfloor 100/10 \rfloor = 10$, $\lfloor 100/15 \rfloor = 6$, and the triple is $\lfloor 100/30 \rfloor = 3$).

Numbers divisible by none: $100 - 74 = 26$, so the probability is $26/100 = 0.26$.

**Code check:** counting $n \in 1..100$ with $n \bmod 2, 3, 5$ all non-zero gives 26.

**Key idea:** inclusion-exclusion on the divisibility sets.

</details>
