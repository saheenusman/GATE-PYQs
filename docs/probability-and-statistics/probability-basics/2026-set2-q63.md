# 2026 CS2 Q63: Conditional probability with coin tosses

## Question

Suppose an unbiased coin is tossed 6 times. Each coin toss is independent of all previous coin tosses. Let $E_1$ be the event that among the second, fourth, and sixth coin tosses, there are at least two heads. Let $E_2$ be the event that among the first, second, third, and fifth coin tosses, there are equal number of heads and tails.

The conditional probability $P(E_1 \mid E_2)$ is equal to ________. (rounded off to one decimal place)

<details class="success" markdown="1">
<summary>Answer</summary>

**0.5**

</details>

<details class="info" markdown="1">
<summary>Solution</summary>

**Understand $E_2$.** Among tosses 1, 2, 3, 5 there are exactly **2 heads and 2 tails**. There are $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ equally likely patterns.

**Toss 2 is shared** by $E_1$ and $E_2$. Tosses 4 and 6 are independent of $E_2$ (fair coins).

**Split by toss 2, given $E_2$:**

- $P(\text{toss 2 = H} \mid E_2) = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}$ (toss 2 is a head, the other head is one of 3 positions).
- $P(\text{toss 2 = T} \mid E_2) = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Now $E_1$** needs at least 2 heads among tosses 2, 4, 6:

| Toss 2 | Needed from tosses 4, 6 | Probability |
|---|---|---|
| H | at least 1 head | $\frac{3}{4}$ |
| T | both heads | $\frac{1}{4}$ |

$$P(E_1 \mid E_2) = \frac12 \cdot \frac34 + \frac12 \cdot \frac14 = \frac{4}{8} = \mathbf{0.5}$$

I also enumerated all 64 outcomes by script and got $0.5$.

**Key idea:** condition on the shared toss; the other tosses stay independent fair coins.

</details>
