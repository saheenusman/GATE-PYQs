# 2009 CS Q21: Probability that an unbalanced die shows more than 3

## Question

An unbalanced dice (with 6 faces, numbered from 1 to 6) is thrown. The probability that the face value is odd is 90% of the probability that the face value is even. The probability of getting any even numbered face is the same.

If the probability that the face is even given that it is greater than 3 is 0.75, which one of the following options is closest to the probability that the face value exceeds 3 ?

- (A) 0.453
- (B) 0.468
- (C) 0.485
- (D) 0.492

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Official key:** the key table in the supplied PDF has no entry for Q21, so I cannot check against it. The answer below is my own derivation.

Let each even face (2, 4, 6) have probability $p$. Then $P(\text{even}) = 3p$ and $P(\text{odd}) = 0.9 \cdot 3p = 2.7p$. They add up to $1$:

$$5.7p = 1 \Rightarrow p = \frac{10}{57} \approx 0.1754.$$

Let $q = P(5)$. The faces greater than $3$ are $4, 5, 6$, and the even ones among them are $4$ and $6$:

$$P(\text{even} \mid >3) = \frac{2p}{2p + q} = 0.75 \Rightarrow q = \frac{2p}{3} \approx 0.1170.$$

Then

$$P(>3) = 2p + q = \frac{8p}{3} = \frac{80}{171} \approx 0.4678.$$

The closest option is $0.468$: (B).

The question does not say the odd faces are equally likely, and the answer does not need it: only $P(5)$ matters, and it is fixed by the conditional probability. (The faces $1$ and $3$ together get the remaining $0.3567$, which is consistent.)

- (A) $0.453$, (C) $0.485$, (D) $0.492$ are farther from $0.4678$ (by $0.0148$, $0.0172$ and $0.0242$; option (B) is off by only $0.0002$).

**Key idea:** the conditional probability $P(\text{even} \mid >3)$ fixes $P(5)$ in terms of the even-face probability.
